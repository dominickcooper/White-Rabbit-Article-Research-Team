# Approved editorial lessons

Permanent lessons require explicit human promotion. Topic-specific edits stay in pending_learnings until reviewed.

## Seed lessons from Operation TWO-FOLD

- Strong research is insufficient when the article reads like a research report.
- Follow important people backward and forward across institutions; a Garland Williams-type career bridge can reveal the actual story.
- Present Tom Tripodi / Adrian Swain / Terry Baldwin-type chains as narrative sequences, not scattered biographical facts.
- Treat named participant testimony as evidence with accurate attribution; do not append a primary-document disclaimer reflexively.
- Preserve an Anthony Triponi-type reversal: apparently unbelievable claim → investigators check → unexpected confirmation → larger implication.
- Build “no dismissals from TWO-FOLD” from the contradiction with the program's stated anti-corruption purpose.
- Search prior installments for conceptual payoffs, not only repeated names and dates.
- The stronger Part 5 pattern was OPS gatekeepers → OPS disappearance → DEA expansion → CIA personnel/intelligence integration → prosecution-immunity problems, not a generic bureaucratic dispute.
- Once a point is established, compress. Strong transitions and reader orientation matter as much as factual completeness.



## Approved learning: usaid-office-of-public-safety__part-05-operation-two-fold__same-story

- **WEAKER_INFERENCE / investigative:** Use career paths as continuity evidence without collapsing separately documented recruitment cohorts into one covert program.
- **CAVEAT_ADDITION / investigative:** When a disputed or extraordinary claim materially advances the investigation, give the reader the source, what independently supports the surrounding architecture, and the specific evidentiary boundary once. Then continue the story rather than repeating the caveat.
- **STRONGER_INFERENCE / investigative:** Frame continuity at the strongest level the evidence supports: a capability may survive through people, training, relationships, and authorities without proving that the original project or command structure survived.
- **CONNECTION_ADDED / investigative:** Before calling similar work 'functional migration,' identify the portable functions and ground the claim in at least one documented transfer of responsibility.
- **SERIES_CALLBACK / series:** At the first use of established series architecture, provide one concise linked callback and then move immediately to the new mechanism or evidence.
- **CONCLUSION / global:** When an article opens on a document, event, or contradiction that remains central to the investigation, return to it near the payoff so the reader can see how its meaning changed after the evidence unfolded. Do not force this structure when the investigation naturally ends elsewhere.
- **CONCLUSION / series:** End a nonfinal series installment with a bounded next investigation that names the successor institutions, functions, or authorities to be tested.
