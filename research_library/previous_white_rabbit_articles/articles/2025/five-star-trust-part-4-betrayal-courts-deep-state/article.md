## **HORN V. BUSH et al. (2004–2006)**

So, in 2004, after Marion Horn was out of prison, he did the unthinkable.

He filed a **federal civil case** in Washington, D.C.—**Horn v. Bush et al. ([Case No. 1:03-CV-00725](https://ia904608.us.archive.org/17/items/gov.uscourts.dcd.2747/gov.uscourts.dcd.2747.docket.html))**.

**Bush** as in **sitting US President George W. Bush.**

The goal? Force the government to admit the trust existed.

But the defense had a trump card: **national security privilege**.

The CIA confirmed it had files on Horn and the trust. The IRS confirmed it had issued a Tax ID (**61-6234232**). But no one—no agency, no department—would release any documents.

**The judge ruled in the government’s favor.**

Horn countered: “If the trust doesn’t exist, *why invoke national security*?”

Horn wasn’t trying to bring down the intelligence community. He just wanted acknowledgment. He wanted a chance to clear his name.

Instead, he got silence.

The government refused to:

- Deny the trust’s existence
- Release files confirming its operations
- Sign affidavits clearing its own name

The final act wasn’t a trial. It was an erasure.

Horn’s lawsuit was buried. No appeal was encouraged.

And additional FOIA inquiries were **effectively stonewalled**.

## **ROGUE DIPLOMACY (2005–2006)**

So, **January of 2005**, the **HORN v. BUSH et al** case was terminated.

But **Marion Horn** was **not** out of the game. Despite the legal wreckage left behind by the U.S. government’s full-court press, he still held the one thing that couldn’t be erased: **knowledge**.

And someone still wanted what he knew.

Later that same year, that someone came knocking through the backdoor—specifically, via the **Bahamas**.

Enter **[Maurice Moore](https://prabook.com/web/maurice_e.moore/512866)**, former Bahamian Ambassador to the United Nations, and his brother, **[Walter Moore](https://www.thenassauguardian.com/obituaries/mrs-sandra-moore/article_0166807b-1bbd-5802-b9f9-aa65422114d3.html)**, a shadowy figure with alleged ties to the **DEA** and **Department of Justice**.

The Moores weren’t just smooth-talking operators. They had access. And that meant something in Horn’s world.

A meeting was arranged. **Joe Alley**, a United Nations official, confirmed in person that FST accounts existed—but he wanted **$500,000 upfront** and a **third of all funds recovered**.

Horn declined. **That was extortion.**

Still, the pitch was seductive: help move billions in FST capital from Barclays and the Nigerian government, and in return, **Horn would be made whole**.

Past charges expunged.   
Accounts reinstated.   
A new tax ID, fresh from the U.S. Treasury.

**Walter Moore** even promised to testify before a U.S. grand jury. That’s how legitimate it looked on paper.

So Horn bought in.

### **The UN and Nigerian Deals**

The pitch was audacious:

- **$31 million USD** owed to FST from a Nigerian energy contract (subcontract NNPC/PED/318X93).
- Another **$5 billion USD** parked at **Barclays Bank**, Isle of Man, to be routed through Luxembourg to the Bahamas.

**Walter Moore** claimed he could move it **all**.

He said he had met with U.S. Treasury officials, high-ranking DOJ contacts, and even secured support from his brother, **Herschel Moore**, an attorney with the U.S. Attorney’s Office in Miami. Horn was told this was the real deal. Not another grift. Not a trap.

The final meeting happened at the home of **[Burton Miller](http://www.fredmitchelluncensored.com/December99.html)**, then-Mayor (or *Chief Councillor*, as its called) of Freeport, Bahamas. Present were Walter, Maurice, Miller, Horn, and Horn’s **new** attorney **[Kevin Russell](https://bahamasbarassociation.com/frm_display/bar-members-public-view/bar-member/360/)**.

Horn even wired **$5,000** through Western Union to help cover Moore’s travel and expenses.

Everything was moving forward.

Until it wasn’t.

### **Deceit and Silent Theft**

After the meeting, Walter Moore disappeared. No calls. No emails. Nothing. Horn sent **cease and desist letters** demanding the return of documents, reimbursement of funds, and a full accounting of any actions taken.

The silence was deafening.

**Then came the betrayal.**

Maurice and Herschel Moore reemerged—with a new plan: **proceed without Horn**.

According to reports, they (Walter and “[other high officials](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/chapter-nine-five-star-trust/9235847#87)”) tried to hijack the trust’s assets entirely. No authorization. No signatures. Just theft, aided by banking intermediaries and shadow diplomats.

Horn responded the only way he could—he **voided all agreements** and issued letters to Barclays, Dresdner, and other banks stating that **no funds** were to be released without **his explicit authorization**.

But the damage was done.

**The trust’s wealth** had been cracked open. And a quiet war had begun over who would control the pieces.

## **THE GHOST ACCOUNTS**

While the Bahamas drama unfolded, something else confirmed Horn’s claims—the banks.

Between 2000 and 2006, a number of high-level financial insiders quietly verified what had long been dismissed as fantasy.

- **Over 300 accounts**, all tied to CD #4051100-92.
- Locations? **Credit Suisse**, **UBS**, **Barclays**, **Bank of New Zealand**, **Royal Bank of Scotland**, **HSBC**, and more.
- Currencies? U.S. dollars, Swiss francs, and gold—lots of gold.

Two men stood out:

- **[Eduard Steiner](https://databasesets.com/national-company/id/171034713)**, a Swiss banker based in Bern, confirmed the existence of Credit Suisse accounts tied to Five Star Trust—but warned it was “too dangerous” to talk.
- **Alexei Ostroukov**, head of The First Russian Independent Bank and a close ally of Vladimir Putin, also verified the holdings, particularly those in Zurich.

According to Ostroukov, the accounts were real, and the ownership was legitimate. Horn wasn’t bluffing. He was sitting on a fortune. And he had names, receipts, and official validations to prove it.

But access? That was another story.

### **Trillions of Dollars**

By the early 2000s, the value of the Five Star Trust’s holdings defied logic.

- **$135 billion USD** reportedly held at **Barclays Bank**, Isle of Man.
- **$1.6 trillion USD** traced to **Credit Suisse**, allegedly moved without authorization.
- Over **60,000 metric tons of gold** stored in vaults from the Philippines, Russia, and South Africa—allegedly under the control of or linked to **OECD**, **Rothschild Group**, and the **CIA**.

At face value, the numbers were absurd. Approximately **$6.5 Trillion USD** in2025**.**

It was too much money to just **let it go.**

## **BURIED SECRETS**

By the late 2000s, the Five Star Trust had seemingly vanished—its accounts locked, its key players dead, silenced, or missing.

**Horns last grab had failed.**

Horn maintained a low-profile online presence, with his [LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/col-marion-horn-jr-8a646512/) listing him as Executive Trustee of Five Star Trust from 1989 to "Present" (as of recent views) and Chairman of board of “[White Knights Invest LLC](https://www.kycompanydir.com/companies/white-knights-invest-llc/).”

In August 2019, he joined X (formerly Twitter) as [HornColonel](https://x.com/HornColonel/), posting sporadically about the trust, offering financial assistance to figures like Donald Trump (e.g., to [retire national debt or fund medical care](https://x.com/HornColonel/status/1181552876507844608)), and endorsing politicians like [Mitch McConnell](https://x.com/HornColonel/status/1211306902304501761) and [Matt Bevin](https://x.com/HornColonel/status/1162135863486234625).

But there are still **so many questions** unanswered.

It’s possible the trust was never meant to be killed. It was just absorbed—fragmented into other structures, buried in shell companies, or turned into a covert asset by factions inside the intelligence community.

Because when you build a tool this powerful, you don’t destroy it.

You hide it.
