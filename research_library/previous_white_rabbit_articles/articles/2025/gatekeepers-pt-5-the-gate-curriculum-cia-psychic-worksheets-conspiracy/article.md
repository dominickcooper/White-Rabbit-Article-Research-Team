Zener Cards, designed to test Extrasensory Perception (ESP)

These simple cards, bearing five repeating symbols—**star, wave, square, circle, and plus sign**—were created in the early 1930s by perceptual psychologist **Karl Zener** to test for **extrasensory perception (ESP)**.

*The idea?*

One person looks at the cards.   
Another tries to “see” the image without visual cues.

**Yeah, it sounds like a party trick.**

But it was **used by the CIA, the Stanford Research Institute, and the U.S. military** in serious, taxpayer-funded research on **telepathy and remote viewing**.

And if you were in GATE, there’s a good chance you were quietly tested with them too.
