# The Good Shepherd: Part 2/3 – The Real Men Behind the Movie

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/47b26d5c-528b-4790-a56b-5258e940e0e6_1024x972.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!o5N2!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F47b26d5c-528b-4790-a56b-5258e940e0e6_1024x972.png)

*The Good Shepherd* might seem like a typical slow-burn spy thriller at first glance—an emotionally distant protagonist, a labyrinth of secrets, and a world of betrayal lurking just beneath the surface.

**But when you look closer, something more profound emerges.**

This isn't just a movie about espionage. It’s a **coded history**—a dramatized glimpse into the real men, real events, and real agencies that built the **intelligence** **empire** that **still defines** the world today.

At the center of the story is **Edward Wilson**, played by Matt Damon—a man who, much like the covert world he inhabits, is closed off and emotionally frozen. While Wilson’s personal story is fictional, his character is a composite of some of the most influential figures in intelligence history, chief among them **[James Angleton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Jesus_Angleton)**.

**Angleton, the CIA’s first chief of counterintelligence**, was obsessed with secrecy and betrayal, trusting no one—not even his closest allies. It’s the same cold paranoia that Wilson carries with him throughout the film.

**National security. No matter the cost.**

But Wilson isn’t alone in his struggle. The other characters in *The Good Shepherd*—**Franklin Wills**, **Philip Allen**, **Ray Brocco**, and **Richard Hayes**—may not be household names, but they’re each inspired by men who, in real life, helped shape the CIA’s covert operations and the dark undercurrent of global power.

**Frank Wisner**, **Allen Dulles**, **Raymond Rocca**, **Richard Helms**, and others were the ones pulling the strings behind the scenes, orchestrating coups, managing intelligence networks, and setting the stage for the hidden world that still runs much of geopolitics today.

## **Edward Wilson: The Composite Ghost**

At the center of *The Good Shepherd* is **Edward Wilson**, portrayed by Matt Damon with an eerie stillness—a man who sees everything, feels nothing, and trusts no one. He's quiet, cerebral, methodical. A ghost even before he disappears into the shadows.

And that’s exactly the point.

Wilson isn’t based on one man. He’s a **composite**, stitched together from the lives and legacies of the CIA’s earliest architects:

- **James Angleton**
- **Frank Wisner**
- **Richard Bissell**

Men who built the bones of the **modern intelligence state** while slowly breaking themselves in the process.

Let’s start with the **first person of the Edward Wilson composite**, who also contributed the most to the creation of the character.

### **James Angleton: The Architect of Counterintelligence**

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/1ff2e653-eaea-4d3a-88fc-63a163cf2f90_260x365.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!ogqx!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F1ff2e653-eaea-4d3a-88fc-63a163cf2f90_260x365.jpeg)

James Angleton

If Edward Wilson is the soul of *The Good Shepherd*, then **[James Angleton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Jesus_Angleton)** is the soul's blueprint.

Angleton was the **CIA’s first chief of counterintelligence**. Brilliant, obsessive, and often unsettling, he believed the world was best understood in fragments—nothing whole, **everything suspect**.

His life reads **like a spy novel.**

Born in **Milan**, educated at **Malvern College** in England, and later **Yale**, Angleton moved easily through the elite corridors where empire and espionage met. He served in the **OSS** first in London. Here he trained under **Kim Philby**, future MI6-CIA traitor.

“The purpose of [my stay in London] was to absorb the British experience and break German codes…So London was in a sense a staging area with full access to British, or the fruits of British activity, going back through their entire history. And it was the advance headquarters for General Donovan, and Mr. Murphy.”  
—James Angleton, [Testimony to US Senate](https://www.archives.gov/files/research/jfk/releases/2025/0318/157-10014-10005.pdf)

According to his sworn testimony before the **US Senate,** which you can read in the **[2025 release of the JFK Files](https://www.archives.gov/files/research/jfk/releases/2025/0318/157-10014-10005.pdf)**, Angleton didn’t enter the CIA blindly—he was placed **directly under James R. Murphy**, the protégé of **“Wild Bill” Donovan** himself (head of the OSS).

**We will get more into him in Part 3.**

Murphy, a wartime OSS veteran, was head of **X-2**, the counterintelligence branch.

After London, **Angleton** went to Italy, where he would go on (in 1945) to become [the head of X-2](https://spartacus-educational.com/Raymond_Rocca.htm) for the whole of the country. Initially though, he ran **Secret Counterintelligence Unit Z** and built deep connections with the **Vatican**, the **Italian Mafia**, and local political operatives.

We will later see that this was **[Operation Gladio](https://thewhiterabbitreport.substack.com/s/operation-gladio).**

“I came in—sent by General **Donovan**—and got in touch with the heads of the military junta of the **Committee of National Liberation**—that was the joint partisan group operating in Italy during the occupation.

**Andy Birding** was the first X-2 guy who came into Rome. He’d been an old **Associated Press** correspondent, and I thought I could trust him—he was a good guy. So we turned over to him everything we had on the **German stay-behind networks**.

Then **Jim Angleton** showed up in August. He started recruiting **fascists**, figuring that the best way to control the Communists was to hire their enemies.

One of the toughest among them was **Prince Valerio Borghese**, who had run what was known as the **Decima MAS**, the 10th Flotilla. These were the men who had executed partisans and hung their bodies from lampposts all over Italy.”

— *Peter Tompkins*, former OSS officer in Rome (1943–1945), as cited in declassified interviews and historical transcripts

After the war, Angleton stayed in Italy and was involved in insuring that the **US interests** (*I mean, US National Security*) were looked after.

When he returned to the U.S., Angleton became head of **Staff A** and liaison to the **[Israeli Mossad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mossad) and [Shin Bet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shin_Bet)**. He saw Israel as a strategic extension of Western intelligence—believing émigrés from the **Eastern Bloc** could be turned into assets, and that Israeli services could act as **proxies in countries where the CIA couldn’t tread**.

**Some even believe** he is the one **[who gave Israel their plutonium](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/nuclear-vault/2016-11-02/numec-affair-did-highly-enriched-uranium-us-aid-israels-nuclear-weapons-program)** and nuclear power.

**When Dulles came to power** as **CIA Director in 1953**, he assigned Angleton responsibility for **coordination with allied intelligence services**.

This assuredly would have included **Operation Gladio.**

Angleton later helped support **[Operation CHAOS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_CHAOS)**, a domestic espionage program **targeting American citizens**—antiwar activists, civil rights groups, and political dissidents.

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/9f1a73a9-62dc-42b7-aafa-cf9d0342e84e_600x482.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!3Piq!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9f1a73a9-62dc-42b7-aafa-cf9d0342e84e_600x482.jpeg)

Under his watch, the CIA amassed **files on over 300,000 Americans**, many completely innocent. He also ran **[HTLINGUAL](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/DOC_0001420864.pdf)**, an illegal mail-opening program targeting correspondence with the Soviet Union.

Even **[Lee Harvey Oswald](https://www.archives.gov/files/research/jfk/releases/2025/0318/157-10014-10005.pdf)** [had a file](https://www.archives.gov/files/research/jfk/releases/2025/0318/157-10014-10005.pdf), well before Dallas. **And** Oswald lived in the Soviet Union **between 1959 and 1962**, when the CIA would have been monitoring the US-Soviet mail through Angleton’s **HTLINGUAL.**

**[Angleton also confirmed](https://www.archives.gov/files/research/jfk/releases/2025/0318/157-10014-10005.pdf)** the agency’s partnership with **organized crime figures** like **Sam Giancana** (who Joe Pesci’s character in Good Shepherd is based on) and **Johnny Roselli**.

In addition, [he admitted](https://www.archives.gov/files/research/jfk/releases/2025/0318/157-10014-10005.pdf) that the **CIA used journalists and media organizations as assets** for intelligence gathering and **propaganda**, establishing relationships with major outlets to plant stories or collect information.

By the 1970s, Angleton had become **the embodiment of institutional distrust**. When it all came crashing down (*around the time of the Church Committee…suspiciously*), he was **quietly** pushed out.

*The Good Shepherd* doesn’t name him, but it doesn't have to. Wilson’s mannerisms, his blankness, his obsession with loyalty and betrayal—it’s all pure **Angleton**.

A man who spent his life chasing phantoms, **only to become one.**

### **Frank Wisner: The Master of Psychological Warfare**

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/0991d10f-6cfc-48cd-b5f3-a8f7f3035a56_1190x1787.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!cSMb!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F0991d10f-6cfc-48cd-b5f3-a8f7f3035a56_1190x1787.jpeg)

Frank Wisner

If James Angleton was the CIA’s ghost, **[Frank Wisner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Wisner)** was its fire. Ruthless, charismatic, and possessed by purpose, he didn’t just believe in the Cold War—he **set it ablaze**.

Recruited by **Dean Acheson** himself, Wisner rose quickly through the ranks of America’s budding covert empire. He wasn’t a field agent in the shadows—he was the **architect behind the curtain**, the man who designed the playbooks and **built the mechanisms of manipulation**.

His domain wasn’t espionage in the narrow sense. It was **narrative control**. Influence. Mass persuasion. What he called, with a wink, the **“[mighty Wurlitzer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mighty_Wurlitzer) (a line of pipe organs)”**—a metaphor for the global propaganda machine he built to make foreign audiences dance to Washington’s tune.

And make no mistake: they danced.

**We’ll see all that in Part 3.**

Wisner’s hands were everywhere.

- He worked closely with **Richard Bissell** (*who is the 3rd part of the Edward Wilson persona*) on the **U-2 spy program**, laying the groundwork for surveillance that stretched across the Iron Curtain.
- Later, he ran the **CIA’s London Station**, liaising with **MI6** and coordinating anti-communist psyops from **Europe to the Caribbean**.
- In **British Guiana**, he helped destabilize a democratically elected government simply because it leaned too far left for Langley’s taste. (*This set the stage for Jonestown, by the way*. **You can read about that below.**)

But the same brilliance that made Wisner indispensable also **fractured him from the inside**. By 1962, he broke.

That year, **Frank Wisner put a shotgun to his head and pulled the trigger**. When asked why, [his son would later say](https://www.thenation.com/article/archive/i-smell-metternich-everywhere-frank-wisner-and-egypt/), simply:

> “He killed too many people.”

### **Richard Bissell: The Engineer Behind the Curtain**

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/21418a78-f241-4fb7-8b11-0173d3f52485_551x725.webp)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!o5-r!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F21418a78-f241-4fb7-8b11-0173d3f52485_551x725.webp)

Richard Bissell

If Wisner was the fire and Angleton the soul, **[Richard Bissell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_M._Bissell_Jr.)** was the man who gave the machine its **gears**.

Where Wisner was **passion** and Angleton was **paranoia**, Bissell was **precision**—a Yale-trained economist who never looked like a spymaster, but thought like an engineer of empires.

Bissell saw **the world as a system** that could be managed… so long as you weren’t afraid to break it apart first.

His early career gave no hint of what he’d become. As **Assistant Deputy Administrator of the Marshall Plan**, Bissell helped rebuild postwar Europe—not just with money, but with motive.

[![File:US-MarshallPlanAid-Logo.svg - Wikipedia](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/13cfc531-1eb2-4763-b404-bcf6334ad28c_1729x2048.png "File:US-MarshallPlanAid-Logo.svg - Wikipedia")](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!ydFq!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F13cfc531-1eb2-4763-b404-bcf6334ad28c_1729x2048.png)

Logo for the Marshall Plan

**The Marshall Plan** wasn’t just aid—it was a **financial Trojan horse**. A way to bind Western Europe into an **American-led orbit**, establish **economic** **dependency**, and embed intelligence networks under the **veneer of reconstruction**.

**It worked.**

And Bissell proved he understood something deeper: that **power doesn’t always come from guns or spies—it often flows through banks, budgets, and balance sheets**.

But he wasn’t content with influence. **He wanted reach.**

At the CIA, Bissell became the architect of the **U-2 spy program**, like we mentioned.

It also nearly started World War III when **[Francis Gary Powers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Gary_Powers)** was shot down in 1960. *Whoops.*

Then came **Cuba**.

Bissell was the man behind the disastrous **[Bay of Pigs invasion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bay_of_Pigs_Invasion)**, the failed attempt to overthrow **Fidel Castro** using CIA-trained Cuban exiles. The operation was rushed, under-resourced, and fatally flawed.

**He was forced to resign.**

But his influence **still remained** on every gear inside the CIA.

He was a founding member of the **“Georgetown Set”**, an elite circle of **journalists, diplomats, spies, and bureaucrats** who shaped Cold War policy over cocktails and consensus. You might recognize some of the members:

- **James Angleton**
- **Frank Wisner**
- **Dean Acheson**
- **Allen Dulles**

Together, they weren’t just managing foreign policy—they were managing **reality itself**.

Bissell’s influence didn’t vanish with Cuba. The systems he built—**economic leverage**, **intelligence infrastructure**, **covert funding channels**—became foundational to the emerging **International Syndicate**: a transnational network of military, intelligence, and financial operators who could project power without public consent or legal oversight.

Bissell didn’t need to believe in ideology. He believed in systems. And **by the time he walked away from Langley**, those systems had already taken on **a life of their own.**

### **Edward Wilson: One Man, Three Ghosts**

The character of Edward Wilson is carved from the obsessions of **Angleton**, the fire of **Wisner**, and the cold calculus of **Bissell**.

His silence is Angleton’s distrust, his sacrifices are Wisner’s unraveling, and his detachment is pure Bissell—a man who moves people like numbers on a ledger.

In Wilson, *The Good Shepherd* distills the entire genesis of the CIA into a single haunted man—loyal to “national security”, **no matter the cost.**

In one of the film’s most intimate scenes, Wilson recalls the moment his father took his own life. What lingers isn’t just the blood or the gunshot—it’s the words left behind:

> ***“He asked me if I knew what trust was. And I said, ‘Trust is when you feel safe with someone, like with my friends. Like with you and Mother.'***
>
> ***And then he said, 'Don’t ever lie. Cause you lie to your friends, they won’t trust you. And you will have nothing. And you’ll never be safe.'"***

That lesson—**don’t lie, or you’ll never be safe**—is the twisted compass by which Wilson tried to live his life.

**The irony is brutal:** in trying to protect himself through lies, Wilson becomes the very thing his father feared—a man with no friends, no love, and no place to feel safe. It’s the same paradox that haunted **Angleton**, who trusted no one after Philby.

Because in the world these men built—Angleton, Wisner, and Bissel—**you don’t earn safety by telling the truth**—**you survive by never needing to.**

## **Allen Dulles: The Creator of the Hidden Alliance**

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/a7a5d766-b4ef-49fe-8e03-9629a98eb2d8_3108x3916.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!b4KM!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa7a5d766-b4ef-49fe-8e03-9629a98eb2d8_3108x3916.jpeg)

Allen Dulles

In *The Good Shepherd*, **Philip Allen**, played with veiled authority by William Hurt, is the onscreen proxy for **[Allen Dulles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allen_Dulles)**—the CIA’s longest-serving director and its most enduring enigma.

A polished Wall Street lawyer turned wartime spymaster, Dulles brought to Langley the same corporate efficiency he once applied to international finance: **deals in the front room, betrayals in the back**.

Dulles didn’t believe in limits—only leverage. Under his direction, the CIA moved from intelligence gathering to **covert empire-building**:

- **[Operation Ajax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1953_Iranian_coup_d%27%C3%A9tat) (Iran, 1953)**  
  The CIA engineered a coup to remove Prime Minister Mohammad Mossadegh after he nationalized British oil interests. Using propaganda, street mobs, and bribes to military officers, the U.S. reinstalled the Shah, trading democracy for oil access.
- **[Operation PBSUCCESS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1954_Guatemalan_coup_d%27%C3%A9tat) (Guatemala, 1954)**  
  Dulles’ CIA overthrew President Jacobo Árbenz, whose land reforms threatened the interests of United Fruit Company—a firm with deep ties to the Dulles brothers. The operation sparked civil war and brutal repression that lasted for decades.
- **[MK-Ultra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MKUltra) (1953–1973)**  
  A secret mind control program that tested LSD, hypnosis, and sensory deprivation on unwitting U.S. and Canadian citizens. Many subjects were institutionalized or permanently harmed. Consent was almost never asked, only assumed—or ignored.
- **Project [ARTICHOKE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Artichoke) / [BLUEBIRD](https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu/NSAEBB/NSAEBB54/st02.pdf)**  
  Early black-site experiments to explore “involuntary confession.” Techniques included sleep deprivation, forced drug use, and electroshock—testing the boundaries of what a human mind could endure, or forget.
- **[Bay of Pigs Invasion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bay_of_Pigs_Invasion) (1961)**  
  The CIA armed and trained Cuban exiles to overthrow Fidel Castro. The invasion collapsed within days. Kennedy took the fall, but the blueprint was Dulles’—a gamble that backfired and pushed Cuba closer to the Soviet Union.
- **Assassination Plots (aka “Executive Action”)**  
  The CIA plotted to eliminate foreign leaders:

  - [Fidel Castro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CIA_assassination_attempts_on_Fidel_Castro#:~:text=The%20United%20States'%20Central%20Intelligence,attempts%20between%201960%20and%201965.) (with poison cigars and exploding seashells, among other things)
  - [Patrice Lumumba](https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/studies-in-intelligence/studies-in-intelligence-68-no-1-extracts-march-2024/review-the-lumumba-plot-the-secret-history-of-the-cia-and-a-cold-war-assassination/) (with tainted toothpaste)
  - Even [Rafael Trujillo](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/RAFAEL%20TRUJILLO%2C%20THE%20DOMI%5B15603377%5D.pdf) and others deemed “inconvenient” to U.S. interests.
- **[Operation Mockingbird](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Mockingbird#:~:text=Operation%20Mockingbird%20is%20an%20alleged,media%20organizations%20for%20propaganda%20purposes.)**  
  A sprawling network of journalists, editors, and media outlets secretly paid or influenced by the CIA to steer public opinion and plant disinformation—both abroad and at home.
- **Support for Authoritarian Regimes**  
  To halt the spread of communism, the CIA helped install or support brutal regimes across the globe—from [Mobutu in Congo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CIA_activities_in_the_Democratic_Republic_of_the_Congo) to [Suharto in Indonesia](https://www.theatlantic.com/international/archive/2017/10/the-indonesia-documents-and-the-us-agenda/543534/)—trading liberty for loyalty.
- **Election Interference and Propaganda**  
  In Europe, Latin America, and Asia, the CIA funneled cash, forged documents, and spread lies to sway elections. [In Italy](https://thewhiterabbitreport.substack.com/p/operation-gladio-natos-covert-war), the Agency propped up centrist parties to block communists. [In Vietnam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CIA_activities_in_Vietnam#:~:text=into%20full%20effect.-,Vietnam%201963,was%20a%20non%2Dcommunist%20coup.), they backed Diem, despite warnings he’d fail.
- **False-Flag Operations**  
  The CIA crafted events meant to provoke war or justify intervention. In some cases, plans were drawn up to stage attacks on U.S. assets and blame them on foreign actors. Most were shelved—but not all.

## **Richard Helms: The Cold Executor**

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/d2b0d071-d725-4c98-b13d-8293fa281fad_321x450.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!EiJp!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fd2b0d071-d725-4c98-b13d-8293fa281fad_321x450.jpeg)

Richard Hayes

**Richard Hayes**, the CIA’s icy operations chief in the film, draws heavily from **[Richard Helms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Helms)**—the agency’s real-life spymaster who directed clandestine services before becoming CIA Director in 1966.

Helms wasn’t theatrical. He didn’t posture. He just made things happen.

Helms was the **master of subversion**, a man who viewed coups, assassinations, and psychological warfare as tools—not outrages. He worked closely with **MI6’s [George Kennedy Young](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Kennedy_Young)**, helping align CIA operations with British strategy during the height of the **Gladio years**.

Together, they coordinated destabilization campaigns in **[Greece](https://socialistvoice.ie/2023/10/the-greek-junta-and-the-cia/), [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CIA_activities_in_Italy), and [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CIA_activities_in_Turkey)**—using NATO infrastructure to run **preemptive counterinsurgency inside supposed democracies**.

**Helms didn’t need ideology.** What he needed was control.

And in **the International Syndicate**, control was everything.

## **Rocca, Papich, and Donovan: The Hidden Hand**

Even the quieter names in *The Good Shepherd* are drawn from the footnotes of history—the men who operated just outside the spotlight but kept the whole system humming.

### RAYMOND ROCCA

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/c21031af-70ea-4884-a069-f9e77f5cbfbc_163x215.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!zixI!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fc21031af-70ea-4884-a069-f9e77f5cbfbc_163x215.jpeg)

**Ray Brocco** mirrors **[Raymond Rocca](https://spartacus-educational.com/Raymond_Rocca.htm)**, **Angleton’s fiercely loyal deputy** in counterintelligence and longtime enforcer of the agency’s internal war against infiltration—real or imagined. Rocca had served as **X-2 in the Italian section**, a designation later tied directly to **Operation Gladio**, and when Angleton returned to Washington after the war, it was Rocca who **remained behind in Italy**, acting as his handler on the ground and managing postwar networks with Vatican and mafia ties.

By July 1969, Rocca had risen to **Deputy Chief of the Counterintelligence Staff**, a position he held until shortly after Angleton was ousted. But his loyalty didn’t stop at foreign operations—it extended into **domestic counter-subversion** as well. When New Orleans District Attorney **Jim Garrison** began investigating a network of right-wing figures—**Clay Shaw, Guy Banister, David Ferrie, Carlos Bringuier, and Eladio del Valle**—for their alleged role in the **assassination of John F. ### **The Role of MI6: British Intelligence’s Quiet Influence**

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/41e396ba-568b-4301-8b50-8831e76a6843_549x493.webp)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!24NJ!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F41e396ba-568b-4301-8b50-8831e76a6843_549x493.webp)

The logo of MI6 (Military Intelligence Section 6)

**The CIA doesn’t work alone.**

It’s part of a larger network, one that spans oceans and borders, driven by a quiet, unseen alliance between **MI6** and the CIA.

The British intelligence service wasn’t just an ally; it was a **key player in the world of espionage**, one that operated in the shadows with a **quiet**, yet **powerful**, influence. From the beginning of the Cold War, **MI6** and **the CIA** formed an unspoken partnership that shaped the intelligence world for decades.

- **[The Iran Coup – Operation Ajax](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/the%20central%20intelligence%20%5B15369853%5D.pdf) (1953)**

  **Joint operation between MI6 and the CIA to overthrow Prime Minister Mohammad Mossadegh.**

  - MI6 first approached the CIA to coordinate efforts after Britain lost influence in Iran following the nationalization of Anglo-Iranian Oil.
  - The British provided intelligence networks and contacts; the CIA brought money, muscle, and political cover.
  - Together, they ran a disinformation campaign, bribed Iranian officials, and orchestrated riots to reinstall the Shah.
- **[The Berlin Tunnel – Operation GOLD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Gold) (1954–1956)**

  **A daring wiretap operation built on both agencies’ technical and logistical cooperation.**

  - MI6 conceived the idea: dig a tunnel into Soviet-controlled East Berlin to tap landlines used by the Red Army.
  - The CIA executed it, constructing the 1,500-foot tunnel.
  - Soviet double agent George Blake (in MI6) betrayed the operation to the KGB before it even began—but the Soviets didn’t act right away, allowing the tunnel to collect intel for nearly a year.
- **[Operation Gladio](https://thewhiterabbitreport.substack.com/p/operation-gladio-natos-covert-war) (Post-WWII–1990s)**

  **A secret NATO stay-behind network, supported by both the CIA and MI6.**

  - Designed to activate resistance forces in case of Soviet invasion in Europe.
  - Involved arms caches, covert training, and sleeper agents scattered across Western Europe.
  - Though mainly coordinated through NATO, MI6 and CIA cooperation was crucial in setting up and managing local cells—especially in Italy, France, and West Germany.
- **Sharing Human Intelligence ([HUMINT](https://media.defense.gov/2021/Jul/15/2002763704/-1/-1/0/APPENDICES_A-G_26FEB46.PDF)) and Signals Intelligence ([SIGINT](https://media.defense.gov/2021/Jul/15/2002763709/-1/-1/0/AGREEMENT_OUTLINE_5MAR46.PDF))**

  **Ongoing Cold War cooperation, especially through the “[Five Eyes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Five_Eyes)” alliance (US, UK, CAN, NZ, AUS).**

  - The UK and U.S. shared intercepted communications, espionage findings, and intelligence assets worldwide.
  - MI6 often provided local contacts in former British colonies and Commonwealth nations where the CIA lacked reach.
  - In return, the CIA supported British efforts in hotspots like Malaya, Kenya, and Cyprus during decolonization conflicts.
- **[Irish Troubles and Counterterrorism](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP88-01315R000400420017-7.pdf) (1970s–1990s)**

  **U.S. and British cooperation on Irish Republican terrorism.**

  - While official U.S. policy was neutral, the CIA quietly shared counterterrorism insights and helped trace IRA funding, especially in the U.S. and Canada.
  - MI6 provided domestic intelligence on IRA activity in Northern Ireland and mainland Britain.
- **[The Afghan-Soviet War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet%E2%80%93Afghan_War) (1979–1989)**

  **Joint involvement in [Operation Cyclone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Cyclone)—arming and training the Afghan mujahideen.**

  - The CIA led the effort with funding and arms; MI6 provided training and intelligence on the ground.
  - MI6 operatives worked in Pakistan and Afghanistan to vet fighters and coordinate with British-aligned networks.

While the CIA's role in covert operations is often front and center, **MI6 has long been the quieter partner**—an intelligence service deeply embedded in global affairs, working behind the scenes, often with much less recognition than its American counterpart.

**MI6’s primary role was similar to that of the CIA**: intelligence gathering, espionage, and counterintelligence. But where the CIA often acted as the muscle of covert operations, MI6 provided the **strategic, diplomatic intelligence** and was **deeply involved in the political maneuvers** that went hand-in-hand with the CIA’s more direct action.

MI6’s influence was particularly felt in Europe, where **the intelligence service** had deep connections, both politically and militarily, across the continent. Their operations helped shape key aspects of the Cold War, including efforts to contain Soviet influence, manipulate political outcomes, and even engage in covert sabotage operations.
