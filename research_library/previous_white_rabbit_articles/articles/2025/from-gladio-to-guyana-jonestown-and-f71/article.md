- **[Resistance to U.S. Economic Pressure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil%E2%80%93United_States_relations_during_the_Jo%C3%A3o_Goulart_government):** He resisted U.S. demands for economic stabilization policies, prioritizing domestic development and social reforms over foreign investment interests.
