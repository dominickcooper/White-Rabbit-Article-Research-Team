When the dust settled from Singapore, Marion “J.R.” Horn was supposed to disappear—forgotten, blacklisted, erased from the global financial system. Instead, something stranger occurred. Something more dangerous.

They called it **[The Five Star Trust](https://dn721906.ca.archive.org/0/items/ted-kennedy-fbi-files/TedKennedyFBIFiles_text.pdf)**.

**And it worked.**

By the late 1980s, The Five Star Trust had done what Eagle II and Golden Triangle could not: **turn dirty money into strategic power**.

Gold-backed loans, secret settlements with the Department of Defense, notarized records from the IRS and international banks—this was no scam. It was a machine.

**But power invites problems.**

What began as a desperate effort to redeem his name evolved into a financial empire. Soon, global intelligence networks, rogue bankers, U.S. Treasury officials, and even the Rothschild banking dynasty were circling the Five Star Trust—some looking to control it, others to destroy it.

## **FOUNDING THE TRUST (1988–1989)**

[In](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/chapter-nine-five-star-trust/9235847) **[December 1988](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/chapter-nine-five-star-trust/9235847)**, as the heat from the Studebaker fallout continued to rise, a new entity quietly appeared in one of the world’s most secrecy-friendly jurisdictions: the **Isle of Man**. It was officially established as a **private asset protection trust** for the heirs of **John Booth**, an ancestor of Marion Horn and Lon E. Williamson.

*Why Lon E. Williamson?*

The Five Star Trust had (*has?*) [several trustees](https://www.kycompanydir.com/companies/five-star-trust/):

- [J.R. Horn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/col-marion-horn-jr-8a646512) - Executive Trustee and Main beneficiary

- William L. Clancy (**one of the “[untouchables](https://dn721906.ca.archive.org/0/items/ted-kennedy-fbi-files/TedKennedyFBIFiles_text.pdf)”**)

The trust’s true function was to act as a legal firewall—a sovereign offshore vault where black-budget funds, forged instruments, and war spoils could be parked beyond the reach of congressional inquiry.

### **Transition to Nevis (1999)**

A decade later, the trust was [officially](https://offshoreleaks.icij.org/nodes/200801831) **[re-domiciled to Nevis](https://offshoreleaks.icij.org/nodes/200801831)**, West Indies—a jurisdiction infamous for its **[ironclad secrecy](https://www.theguardian.com/news/2018/jul/12/nevis-how-the-worlds-most-secretive-offshore-haven-refuses-to-clean-up)**, no-tax environment, and impenetrable banking laws.

In **1999**, it was registered as **Trust #287/99**.

The move wasn’t random. By the late ’90s, scrutiny was increasing across Europe, especially for [Isle of Man-registered entities](https://www.offshore-protection.com/isle-of-man-tax-haven). Shifting to Nevis distanced the trust from Western regulators and further insulated its holdings from U.S. court reach. From that moment on, Five Star Trust would operate with **near-complete financial anonymity**.

And it had some very powerful friends helping it stay that way.

### **Assignment of the Mitsui CD**

The trust’s real legitimacy—at least within intelligence circles—came from what it held.

On **January 5, 1989**, the long-contested **Mitsui Certificate of Deposit #4051100-92**, originally issued in Tokyo in 1983 for **¥318 billion** at **5% interest**, was **formally assigned to the Five Star Trust**.

The transfer gave Five Star Trust control of not only the certificate, but its accrued interest—which by 1989 had grown exponentially.

That same month, an **[alleged confidential settlement](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/five-star-trust-history/9235358)** was reached between **General Ferrera** and the **U.S. Department of Defense**. As part of the deal:

- **¥340 billion** (including damages) was **deposited into the Bank of New Zealand**, under the same reference number as the CD.
- The **IRS issued full tax exemption** to Marion Horn and family—treating the entire transaction as government-sanctioned, despite officially denying the Trust’s existence.

In the eyes of law, this should’ve been the final word.

But Ferrera had something else in mind.

By the early 1990s, *The Five Star Trust* had evolved from a covert cleanup operation into something far more audacious—a parallel financial system. Anchored by the Mitsui CD, the trust began asserting control over what it claimed were **more than 300 offshore accounts** tied to that original certificate.

The [numbers were staggering](https://archive.org/details/five-star-trust_202501/page/36/mode/2up?q=trillion):

- **Bank of New Zealand**: Initial ¥340 billion ($2B+) deposit from DoD settlement
- **Citibank and Chase Manhattan**: Unspecified holdings linked to trust instruments

**[Warren Goss](https://opengovco.com/attorney/16413#google_vignette)**, a CIA-tied [attorney](https://www.texasbar.com/AM/Template.cfm?Section=Find_A_Lawyer&template=/Customsource/MemberDirectory/MemberDirectoryDetail.cfm&ContactID=169590) from Boulder, Goss had all the right language and a plan to “transition” Five Star Trust to U.S. Treasury control.

(represented by President **Jim Georges**) sent a letter dated August 29, 1997, addressed to the trust and copied to attorneys [Warren Goss](https://opengovco.com/attorney/16413#google_vignette) and [Terrell Black](https://www.courier-journal.com/obituaries/lcj267212).
- They proposed a $3.5 billion payout over 35 year ($100 million per year). This money would be invested in a program expected to yield a 280% annual return, with payouts distributed bi-monthly over 12 months each year. Beneficiaries (including executive trustee Marion Horn Jr.) would reinvest 55% of returns for the first 5 years, also at the projected 280% return rate, creating a compounding effect.
- $3.5 billion would be placed in a secure account at FIRST INTERNATIONAL PRIVATE BANK (within CITIBANK, New York City) 30-45 days after contract signing. These funds would be released as a bonus at the end of the 35-year term if no defaults occurred, or handled per default terms otherwise.
- Every 5 years for the first 25 years, an extra $2.5 billion would be remitted if certain conditions were met, potentially increasing the total value to $19-23 billion over the full term (excluding other assets).

But there were some conditions:

1. Horn would resign after the first payment and agree not to use Five Star-related names post-transaction.
2. Horn would place his personal tax returns in escrow.
3. **[Terrell Black](https://www.courier-journal.com/obituaries/lcj267212)** (attorney, designated agent for service in Kentucky) would hold the Bahamian Charter in trust post-deal and ensure prior commitments were honored.

To Horn, **this sounded like a sweetheart deal.** A formal agreement for the sale was dated September 24, 1997, notarized (with the exact notary date left blank in documents), involving asset and stock transfers.

**Georges** and **Trans/Tech Holdings** went into default son the contract, as confirmed in a letter from attorney **[Warren Goss](https://opengovco.com/attorney/16413#google_vignette)**, who noted a conflict of interest with attorney **[Terrell Black](https://www.courier-journal.com/obituaries/lcj267212)** (allegedly representing both sides after being influenced by Georges) and stated he would shut down the acquisition.
