He won in 1990. *But the real story wasn’t about him.*

**The real power behind the throne? [Vladimiro Montesinos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimiro_Montesinos).**

According to **[NSA](https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu/NSAEBB/NSAEBB37/docs/doc01.pdf) and CIA documents**, Montesinos had been a known U.S. intelligence asset [since the mid-1970s](https://web.archive.org/web/20071208030453/http://www.publicintegrity.org/report.aspx?aid=257)—long before Fujimori ever stepped into the political spotlight. The U.S. government **suspended $167 million** in aid, and Congress halted all military assistance not related to drug enforcement.

However, this initial condemnation **was followed by a more pragmatic approach**.

Within two weeks, **the Bush administration** recognized Fujimori as Peru's legitimate leader, influenced [mostly by](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberto_Fujimori) his opposition to the Shining Path insurgency.
