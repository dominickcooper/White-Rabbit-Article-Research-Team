## The Guard Dog Program

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/9d347d86-9fe7-4cb9-a077-88caa02cb15e_500x250.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!ZCsW!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9d347d86-9fe7-4cb9-a077-88caa02cb15e_500x250.png)

Excerpt from a [heavily redacted letter](https://documents2.theblackvault.com/documents/cia/behavioral/C00021827.pdf) requesting funding for the guard dog program

**[Various MKULTRA projects](https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/cia-mkultra-collection/)** embarked on experiments aiming to **control animal behavior** through electronic brain stimulation. One such endeavor involved [implanting electrodes into the brains of six dogs](https://www.newsweek.com/cia-mkultra-documents-files-remote-control-dogs-1250519), enabling operatives to **remotely induce movements** such as running, turning, and stopping. This research sought to explore the potential of directing animals for intelligence operations.

A related project, known as "**[Acoustic Kitty](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/cia-experimented-animals-1960s-too-just-ask-acoustic-kitty-180964313/)**," attempted to transform cats into **mobile eavesdropping devices**. By surgically embedding microphones and transmitters into felines, the CIA aimed to utilize their natural movements to gather intelligence.

> *Working with outside audio equipment contractors, the CIA built a 3/4-inch-long transmitter to embed at the base of the cat’s skull. Finding a place for the microphone was difficult at first, but the ear canal turned out to be prime, and seemingly obvious, real estate. The antenna was made from fine wire and woven, all the way to the tail, through the cat’s long fur to conceal it. The batteries also gave the techies a little trouble, since the cats’ size limited them to using only the smallest batteries and restricted the amount of time the cat would be able to record.*
>
> - Excerpt from “[The CIA Plan to Use Cats as Spies](https://www.mentalfloss.com/article/27790/cia-plan-use-cats-spies-and-taxi-ruined-it)”

However, practical challenges, **including the cats' unpredictable behavior**, led to the project's eventual termination.

The greatest advancement, however, was demonstrated by [José Manuel Rodríguez Delgado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jos%C3%A9_Manuel_Rodr%C3%ADguez_Delgado).

Though not explicitly mentioned in MKULTRA or CIA documents, Delgado’s work in the 1950s and 60s **paralleled the goals of MKULTRA**. Additionally, certain aspects of his life would appear to connect him to western intelligence:

- Delgado was a professor at **[Yale University](https://www.ctinsider.com/connecticutmagazine/health/article/Yale-s-Forgotten-Master-of-Mind-Control-17039481.php)**, which had **connections to CIA-funded research programs** during the MKULTRA period.
- Some of his funding came from institutions like the **Office of Naval Research** and **National Institutes of Health**, both of which had ties to **classified military research**.

- His research on **remote-controlled behavior** aligns with certain **MKULTRA subprojects**, particularly those involving **electrical brain stimulation and non-lethal control methods**.

## Subprojects 4, 15, and 19: Spy Magic

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/abf13e1c-0eb8-4db1-be41-266fd741c4f1_750x1700.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!MjRz!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fabf13e1c-0eb8-4db1-be41-266fd741c4f1_750x1700.png)

**[John Mulholland](https://www.e-flux.com/journal/148/631017/society-of-the-psyop-part-2-ai-mind-control-and-magic/),** the renowned American magician, was specifically involved in three MKUltra subprojects: **[Subprojects 4, 15, and 19](https://archive.org/details/mkultra-overview-of-each-project/page/n13/mode/2up?q=15)**.

In early 1953, **John** **Mulholland** withdrew from the public eye, shutting down *The Sphinx* (his Magician niche magazine) and stepping away from most of his professional obligations.

Officially, he **cited health concerns** as the reason for his departure.

However, he had actually taken on **a covert role** within the **MKULTRA** program.

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/c00da611-6c59-4fb8-82ef-41a903fc9b08_416x552.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Jmj3!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fc00da611-6c59-4fb8-82ef-41a903fc9b08_416x552.png)

These subprojects were part of the CIA’s broader effort to harness deception and covert techniques for espionage, aligning with MKUltra’s mission to explore mind control and behavioral manipulation during the Cold War.

### Subproject 4

This was Mulholland’s initial assignment, beginning in 1953 under the direction of Sidney Gottlieb, head of the CIA’s Technical Services Staff (TSS).

For this subproject, Mulholland was tasked with writing a manual titled *[Some Operational Applications of the Art of Deception](https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/smoke-and-mirrors-the-magic-of-spycraft/)*, completed in May 1953 for a payment of $3,000 (equivalent to roughly $35,000 today).

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/9232beb7-3ed4-44b0-b5a9-422d5e72d871_697x445.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Dx9n!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9232beb7-3ed4-44b0-b5a9-422d5e72d871_697x445.png)

The document, spanning about 80 pages, outlined **sleight-of-hand techniques** for **surreptitiously administering drugs**—such as **slipping** **pills** or **liquids** into drinks—and **misdirection tactics to conceal these actions**.

It was designed to train CIA operatives in delivering substances like LSD to unwitting targets, supporting MKUltra’s experiments in interrogation and behavioral control.

Declassified documents, such as those referenced in [The Official CIA Manual of Trickery and Deception](https://ia801604.us.archive.org/31/items/cia-manual-trickery-deception-2009/cia-manual-trickery-deception-2009.pdf) by H. Keith Melton and Robert Wallace (2009), confirm Subproject 4 as Mulholland’s entry point, with a memo from May 5, 1953, noting the project’s approval and funding.

### **Subproject 15 and 19**

Following the completion of Subproject 4, Mulholland’s work was expanded under Subproject 15 and 19, starting around 1954 and continuing through at least 1958. This subproject involved **revising and extending the original manual** to incorporate additional techniques, including methods **tailored for female agents** and **procedures** for collaborative operations between two operatives.

The updated manual, completed by 1957 or 1958, **grew to about 130 pages** and included new sections on **recognition** **signals** (e.g., [specific ways to tie shoelaces](https://web.archive.org/web/20150707225807/http://www.boston.com/bostonglobe/ideas/CIA_illusion?pg=3) or [wear clothing](https://web.archive.org/web/20150707231608/http://www.boston.com/bostonglobe/ideas/CIA_illusion?pg=7)) and **psychological** **strategies** (e.g., using a disheveled appearance to reduce suspicion).

By the [end of these 3 subprojects](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/MKULTRA%20SUBPROJECT%2019%20%20%281%5B8144532%5D.pdf), the CIA had a manual to teach their agents how to deliver “various materials to unwitting subjects”. This would become especially useful in **Operation Midnight Climax.**

## Subproject 54: Perfect Amnesia

**[Subproject 54](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/MKULTRA%20SUBPROJECT%2054%20%20%288%5B8145490%5D.pdf)**, known as the “Perfect Amnesia” project, was one of MK Ultra’s more ambitious efforts, focusing on developing techniques to **erase specific memories** or induce **total memory loss** in subjects without causing visible physical harm. The idea was to create an effective, reliable means of blocking or removing memories, particularly in intelligence agents or captured operatives, to prevent the disclosure of sensitive information or to implant false memories for covert operations.

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/7cf8c2cb-da23-4eef-bbff-5a4e8a38f08d_552x240.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Uxh8!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7cf8c2cb-da23-4eef-bbff-5a4e8a38f08d_552x240.png)

### **Techniques Explored in Perfect Amnesia**

Subproject 54 experimented with various techniques, many of which focused on **disrupting brain activity and neural pathways associated with memory**. These methods were designed to induce amnesia selectively, so a subject would retain other memories while "forgetting" specific information or experiences.

#### **1. High-Frequency Sound Waves**

One of the more unusual methods tested in Subproject 54 involved the use of **high-frequency sound waves** directed at the subject’s head to disrupt brain function. The concept was based on the idea that targeted sonic waves could **temporarily scramble neural patterns** related to memory storage, essentially “deleting” recent memories. This approach was also known as the **“[Perfect Concussion](https://jeff-kaye.medium.com/the-unknown-story-of-cias-mk-decoy-subproject-61-cde9a4089c16)” technique** and was thought to create the effect of a mild, controlled concussion without visible physical injury.

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/26e47972-4e74-4513-9a8b-8cc6673c0abb_818x507.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!FtKC!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F26e47972-4e74-4513-9a8b-8cc6673c0abb_818x507.jpeg)

"Using cadavers to determine just how and where to strike the human skull to cause concussion"

Documents indicate that this technique was tested on volunteers as well as unwitting subjects in controlled settings. Subjects exposed to high-frequency sound waves reported experiencing confusion, disorientation, and temporary gaps in memory. However, the effects were inconsistent; some participants experienced **complete memory loss of recent events**, while others retained only hazy, fragmented memories.

But the implications were harrowing:

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/844ac3e4-42cf-4ed7-a324-bcb381e6bb6c_544x202.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!UEAJ!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F844ac3e4-42cf-4ed7-a324-bcb381e6bb6c_544x202.png)

#### **2. Pharmacological Amnesia Agents**

The CIA also explored the use of **pharmacological agents** to induce selective amnesia. Various drugs were tested in controlled doses to see if they could selectively disrupt memory without impairing other mental faculties.

**Scopolamine**, a drug historically used to **[induce confusion and disorientation](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/scopolamine-induced-amnesia)**, was [one of the substances tested](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/project%20mk-ultra%5B15545700%5D.pdf). Combined with other chemicals, it produced memory lapses in subjects, although it often led to **unpredictable side effects**, including paranoia and extreme anxiety.

Another drug tested was **[sodium amytal](https://digitalcommons.cedarville.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1005&context=history_capstones)**, also known as a “truth serum,” which, in higher doses, caused severe disorientation and memory loss. The CIA hoped that it could be dosed in such a way as to “blank” specific memories, especially when combined with psychological conditioning techniques, but achieving consistent results was a challenge.

#### **3. Electromagnetic Brain Stimulation**

Subproject 54 also investigated **electromagnetic fields** as a potential way to interfere with memory consolidation. Scientists theorized that if certain parts of the brain could be temporarily “shut down” during the memory-encoding process, they might prevent short-term memories from transferring to long-term storage. By experimenting with electromagnetic pulses directed at specific regions of the brain, such as the **hippocampus**, they aimed to interrupt the brain's natural memory formation process.

## Subproject 136: ESP and Alters

**[Subproject 136](https://substack.com/home/post/p-145417160?utm_campaign=post&utm_medium=web)** sought to crack the code of **ESP (extrasensory perception)**—or at least figure out whether it was real.

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/f257f9b3-916f-44b2-8c82-461481a3f439_490x465.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!ceEG!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff257f9b3-916f-44b2-8c82-461481a3f439_490x465.png)

From **August 1961 to September 1962**, the agency funneled **[$8,763](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/MKULTRA%20%20SUBPROJECT%20136%20%20%5B8144493%5D.pdf)** into an unnamed institution (probably **Stanford or Duke**, given their parapsychology research) in hopes of **designing repeatable ESP experiments, identifying psychological markers for success, and mapping the limits of psychic ability**.

With whispers of **[Soviet psychic programs](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/NSA-RDP96X00790R000100010041-2.pdf)**, the CIA was willing to try anything—even if that meant blending **fringe science with psychological warfare**.

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/2cb13921-b58f-4f5a-9737-d0cd27d39657_332x209.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!-K5W!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2cb13921-b58f-4f5a-9737-d0cd27d39657_332x209.png)

The experiments had three key parts.

1. First, **group ESP tests**, where **everyone was assumed to have at least a little psychic ability**, and results were matched against psychological profiles—essentially a mix of **personality tests and physiological tracking**.
2. Second, they played with **subjective probability**, likely using **hypnosis** to alter mental states. The documents explicitly mention hypnosis as *“a technique for creating various subjective states through suggestion,”* hinting at an effort to **amplify psychic abilities by shifting consciousness**.
3. And third, though some records are too damaged to read, the project explored **how far and fast ESP signals could travel**, treating the brain like a radio transmitter. Overseeing this was **Sidney Gottlieb**, the mastermind of many MKULTRA subprojects, alongside two unnamed consultants—possibly **J.B. Rhine, the father of modern parapsychology**.

While the psychic spy angle is intriguing, what stands out is **the hypnosis component**. Unlike the dice-rolling and telepathy tests, hypnosis delivered **tangible results**, particularly in creating **temporary personality shifts**—a core interest of MKULTRA’s **mind control research**.

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/f6bf6e1c-ae3b-41d3-ae80-492a390ba09b_526x260.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!djAW!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff6bf6e1c-ae3b-41d3-ae80-492a390ba09b_526x260.png)

This ties into **[Project Monarch](https://blackatlantic.uga.edu/what-is-project-monarch)**, the rumored CIA subprogram focused on **breaking and rebuilding the human mind through trauma-based programming**. In Monarch terminology, these induced **dissociative states** were used to create **“Alters”**—separate, programmable identities within a subject’s mind.

If Subproject 136 **used hypnosis to alter personality traits**, the leap to **split identities (Alters) for intelligence purposes** isn’t far-fetched. While the CIA’s official stance was that ESP research yielded **“interesting but not conclusive”** results, the real breakthroughs may have been in **fine-tuning hypnosis for behavioral programming**—a tool far more useful for intelligence than psychic guessing games.

In the end, **[Subproject 136](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/MKULTRA%20%20SUBPROJECT%20136%20%20%5B8144493%5D.pdf)** didn’t deliver the **psychic spy toolkit** Hollywood loves to imagine, but it did **refine the CIA’s arsenal of behavioral control techniques**.

Whether through **hypnosis, trauma, or suggestion**, the agency’s ultimate goal was always the same: **bending the human mind to its will**.

## Experiments on Children

The use of **[children in MKUltra-linked research](https://truthout.org/articles/the-hidden-tragedy-of-the-cias-experiments-on-children/)** is a **largely overlooked** aspect of the program’s history. Researchers such as **[Dr. Lauretta Bender](https://blogs.uoregon.edu/autismhistoryproject/people/lauretta-bender-1897-1987/)** and **[Dr. Harold Abramson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harold_Alexander_Abramson)**, whose work aligned with MKUltra’s goals, subjected children to **electroshock therapy and high doses of LSD** under the guise of psychiatric treatment.
