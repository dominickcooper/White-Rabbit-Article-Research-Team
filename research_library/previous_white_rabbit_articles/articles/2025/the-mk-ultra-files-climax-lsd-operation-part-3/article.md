MK Ultra's experiments weren’t confined to the walls of laboratories or the hands of trusted researchers. The CIA pushed MK Ultra’s boundaries into the real world, applying techniques and testing drugs in everyday environments to see how unwitting participants would react.

> *“I think every last one of us felt sorry to attempt this type of thing. We knew we were crossing the line.”*
>
> - CIA Agent. 1979 ABC Special on MK Ultra.

These "field tests" were conducted in public settings, prison cells, and hospital wards, all under the radar of those involved. The goal was to observe how experimental drugs and techniques would work in uncontrolled settings, using real people who often had no idea they were being tested.

## Operation Midnight Climax (*yes, THAT climax*)

One of the most infamous MK Ultra field tests, **Operation Midnight Climax**, involved setting up CIA-controlled “safehouses” in San Francisco and New York City, where agents could conduct LSD experiments on unsuspecting citizens. The CIA hired **George Hunter White**, a narcotics officer, to run these safehouses.

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/8c6a4b2c-7f50-47a4-a462-223bdf312b79_1000x600.webp)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!ntZl!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8c6a4b2c-7f50-47a4-a462-223bdf312b79_1000x600.webp)

Mike Burke, former OSS colleague of white said “He was fascinating because ou knew somehing about him but no enough about him to really get a fix on him. He also knew a great deal about the “swifter elements” of society, the gamier side of life. He was very impressive in his technical knowledge of the underworld, so to speak.”

### How Operation Midnight Climax Worked

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/bd8ef77b-26f6-495b-911b-55c04cbf123c_700x520.webp)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!uUxb!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fbd8ef77b-26f6-495b-911b-55c04cbf123c_700x520.webp)

The CIA’s goal was to study the effects of **LSD** on individuals in real-life scenarios, primarily as a means of gaining insight into interrogation and control methods. In these safehouses, men would be dosed without their consent or knowledge. White and his team often observed from behind mirrored glass, taking notes on the men’s reactions to the drugs. The experiments were conducted in "comfortably furnished" rooms to replicate a natural environment, complete with beds, lamps, and chairs to keep the subjects relaxed—at least at first.

> *"Gottlieb wanted to systematize the study of how sex, especially in combination with drugs, could loosen men's tongues.”*
>
> - Stephen Kinzer, Excerpt from his book Poisoner in Chief

The experience of these men was surreal, to say the least.

Many became disoriented, some spiraled into paranoia, and others appeared to lose complete touch with reality. [Some test subjects](https://www.fordlibrarymuseum.gov/library/document/0005/7288238.pdf) had become ill for hours or days afterward. At least one had to be hospitalized.

While White and his CIA colleagues took meticulous notes on these reactions, they did little to help the unwitting subjects, often allowing them to leave and manage the aftereffects on their own.

### **Impact of Operation Midnight Climax**

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/8c0d1e9d-384a-4ab4-b5c0-00e487255d56_821x437.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!YEEK!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8c0d1e9d-384a-4ab4-b5c0-00e487255d56_821x437.png)

Aug 1977 Article. San Francisco Examiner.

Operation Midnight Climax continued for several years, with CIA records indicating that **hundreds of civilians were exposed to LSD** in these “safehouses.” However, most participants never learned the truth about what had happened to them. The operation wasn’t halted due to ethical concerns but because it was found out by [a staffer](https://www.washingtonpost.com/wp-dyn/content/article/2005/06/15/AR2005061502685.html) in the office of CIA's Inspector General John S. Earman conducting an audit.

**The agency then shifted its focus to other methods.**

## Testing on Prisoners and Hospital Patients

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/41e09c02-4e81-475e-a292-091db2f9cbdb_278x181.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!akhY!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F41e09c02-4e81-475e-a292-091db2f9cbdb_278x181.jpeg)

While Operation Midnight Climax was conducted in public settings, the CIA also focused on more controlled environments like **prisons** and **mental health facilities**. These institutions offered the agency a unique advantage: vulnerable subjects who could be easily manipulated, often with promises of reduced sentences or special privileges. However, prisoners and patients were rarely given enough information to understand the risks involved.

### Prison Experiments: “Volunteers” with Limited Choices

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/4253d1be-6dea-4a33-805c-2468266af93f_1200x800.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Au3x!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4253d1be-6dea-4a33-805c-2468266af93f_1200x800.jpeg)

James 'Whitey' Bulger

In prisons, inmates were often coerced into participating in experiments with promises of lighter sentences or privileges, such as access to television or better food. In one example, inmates (including [Whitey Bulger](https://www.cbsnews.com/boston/news/i-team-whitey-bulger-volunteered-for-lsd-testing-while-in-prison-in-1950s/)) at the **Federal Penitentiary in Atlanta** were dosed with LSD and other psychoactive drugs under the pretense that it was part of a “rehabilitation program.”

In his journal, Whitey even recounted the experience:

In the notebook -- undated, but apparently written in the years after he was released from prison -- Whitey described "horrible LSD experiences followed by thoughts of suicide and deep depression." Yet he was determined to keep it all to himself so he would not be "committed for life."

At one point, Whitey wrote that he developed a "morbid fear of LSD" and felt if he had any more of it, "it would push me over the edge." He was afraid that "if I mentioned hearing voices" or the "seeming movement of calendar in cell, etc., that I'd be committed for life and never see the outside again."

### Hospital Testing: Patients as Unwitting Subjects

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/ca246d33-a332-49bf-bf93-9bf54ce169bb_1200x900.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!DTaM!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fca246d33-a332-49bf-bf93-9bf54ce169bb_1200x900.jpeg)

[Mental health facilities](https://web.archive.org/web/20210330005628/https://www.nytimes.com/1977/08/04/archives/80-institutions-used-in-cia-mind-studies-admiral-turner-tells.html) were key testing grounds for MK Ultra, where the CIA could easily access individuals with existing psychological vulnerabilities. This setup allowed researchers to **mask the effects of experimental drugs** as legitimate psychiatric treatment, often leaving patients unaware of their role in secret government experiments.

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/cee076b9-08e6-44ab-ab33-b98bfdaf6454_637x152.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!NE_D!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fcee076b9-08e6-44ab-ab33-b98bfdaf6454_637x152.png)

Excerpt from [1977 Senate Hearing](https://www.intelligence.senate.gov/sites/default/files/hearings/95mkultra.pdf)

One example took place at the **New York State Psychiatric Institute** in the 1950s. Here, patients diagnosed with mild anxiety or depression were unwittingly given doses of **LSD and psilocybin** as part of research into the effects of psychedelics on mental health. The drugs were administered without clear disclosure, as doctors presented the experiments as therapeutic interventions. One former patient later testified that he had “trusted the doctors to help,” but instead found himself in “a state of constant terror,” experiencing hallucinations and paranoia that lingered long after treatment.

And [then there’s the case of tennis pro, Harold Blauer](https://archive.org/details/ciadoctorshumanr0000ross/page/81/mode/1up?q=tennis). Dr. Paul Hoch was a doctor at New York State Psychiatric Institute, recruited by Sidney Gottlieb to be a facilitator of the MKULTRA experiments.

Harold Blauer went to see Dr. Hoch [seeking treatment for depression](https://nypost.com/2019/09/16/the-mad-scientist-behind-americas-mind-control-quest-with-lsd/) following a divorce. In late 1952, Blauer was [injected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harold_Blauer) **six times over the course of a month**, with an unidentified drug.

Blauer complained of hallucinations, but “Hoch insisted that he proceed.” Little did Blauer know, it was mescaline - the active ingredient in peyote.

Then, the following January, Hoch and his team pushed the envelope to far.

> “On Jan. 8, 1953, Blauer was given a dose 14 times greater than previous ones. The protocol notes that he protested when he was injected at 9:53 a.m. Six minutes later he was flailing wildly. At 10:01 his body stiffened. He was pronounced dead at 12:15.”

His family was told that it was a reaction to the drug - which was allegedly for therapeutic or diagnostic purposes. They didn’t learn the truth until years later, after reaching a settlement of $18,000 with the state (roughly $200,000 today). After the truth was uncovered, the state of New York did give the Blauer family an [additional $700k](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1987-05-06-mn-2486-story.html).

Strangely enough, [on the same day as Blauer](https://analyticalsciencejournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/dta.2292), another patient at NYSPI received an injection of MDA. However, she was administered only 150 mg instead of the intended 450 mg that Blauer received. They actually stopped due to her severe reaction to the substance. The injection was halted when only one-third of the dose had been administered.

[![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/4414c53e-a0c6-4483-a9f3-7f881cf15ffb_220x286.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!JxKA!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F4414c53e-a0c6-4483-a9f3-7f881cf15ffb_220x286.jpeg)

Some people did know what they were doing.

[Ken Kesey](https://web.archive.org/web/20210428174421/https://www.nytimes.com/2001/11/11/nyregion/ken-kesey-author-of-cuckoo-s-nest-who-defined-the-psychedelic-era-dies-at-66.html?pagewanted=1), author of [One Flew Over the Cuckoo's Nest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Flew_Over_the_Cuckoo%27s_Nest_(novel)), is said to have volunteered for MKUltra experiments involving LSD and other psychedelic drugs at the Veterans Administration Hospital in Menlo Park while he was a student at nearby Stanford University. **He was paid $75.**

**And this sort of thing [went on in dozens of places](https://archive.org/details/location-of-human-experiments/page/83/mode/1up?q=%22Spring+Grove+State+Hospital%22+mk+ultra):**

- **Spring Grove State Hospital | Baltimore, Maryland** - The test subjects were given enormous amounts of LSD over an extended period — 100 micrograms per day for two weeks, which was increased by a further 100 micrograms daily thereafter to combat rising tolerance levels.
- **Spring Grove Clinic | Catonsville, Maryland**- A series of LSD studies performed from 1963 to 1976 on patients with psychotic illnesses. Many of the patients who took part in these experiments federally funded by the NIMH were admitted against their own wishes. Over the course of the experiments, over 700 people were treated with LSD
- **Lexington Addiction Research Center | Lexington, Kentucky** - Dubbed “[The Narcotics Farm](https://www.psychedelicsinrecovery.org/the-narcotics-farm-of-lexington-a-pioneering-institution-in-addiction-treatment-psychedelic-research-and-its-controversial-ties-to-mkultra/),” they administered LSD and other psychoactive drugs to inmates and patients, sometimes without consent. To add insult to injury, this institution designed to help with addictions would “reward” the volunteers with the drug of their choice. So, for patients going through withdrawls, *were they really volunteers?*

As a matter of fact, [80+ public and private institutions](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP99-00498R000100110072-2.pdf) were associated with MKULTRA, including 44 colleges o r universities as well as hospitals, prisons, and pharmaceutical companies.
