### **The General Order That Opened Pandora’s Box**

The bureaucratic name—**[General Order Establishing the Counter Espionage Branch of the Intelligence Branch of the Intelligence Service of OSS](https://irp.fas.org/ops/ci/docs/ci2/2ch3_a.htm#ossceb)** (*woof*)—didn’t do justice to what it unleashed.

Let’s break down what the order actually authorized X-2 to do:

- **Collect intel** on espionage and subversive activity from every “authorized” source.
- **Process and analyze** that intel to take “appropriate action.”
- **Protect OSS operations** by detecting and neutralizing foreign penetration efforts.
- **Cooperate with allied agencies**, sharing real-time alerts of enemy activity.
- **Maintain subversive personality lists**—essentially kill or capture rosters—for use by theater commanders and other U.S. agencies.

**And that was just the surface.**

X-2 would operate under its own separate **channels**, **maintain private files**, and establish its own **liaison network** inside OSS walls. It didn’t report to the whole—**it was a nation within a nation**, loyal not to oversight, **but to outcome**.

The order also sketched out what a “*[Strategic Intelligence Service with Subversive Attributes](https://irp.fas.org/ops/ci/docs/ci2/2ch3_a.htm)*” might look like:

- **Secret means of information gathering**
- **Morale and physical subversion** as core functions

  - **Morale subversion** is the use of **propaganda**, **disinformation**, psychological operations (**psyops**), and **cultural influence** to weaken the confidence, unity, and will of a target population or political group.
  - **Physical subversion** involves direct, often **covert actions**—like **sabotage**, **assassinations**, **kidnappings**, or **arming insurgents**—to destabilize or overthrow governments, institutions, or movements.
- **Independent communications**, including pouch and code

  - “Pouch and code” essentially means **secure communication methods**. “Pouch” refers to a physical diplomatic mail system—sealed, hand-delivered containers (often via diplomatic couriers) that are legally protected from inspection. “Code”, of course, means encrypted.
- **Unvouchered funds and separate budgets**

  - **Unvouchered funds** are **secret, off-the-books government funds** that do not require standard accounting or public disclosure. They're used for operations where **plausible deniability is essential**—bribery, black ops, asset payments, covert logistics, and more.
  - **Separate budgets** mean these **funds are kept in distinct financial silos**, often under the direct control of senior intelligence officials, **bypassing** traditional oversight channels like Congress or Parliament.
  - **Unvouchered funds** + **separate budgets** = money that moves in the dark, for actions no one is supposed to see.
- **Diplomatic cover and passport privileges**

  - **Diplomatic cover** means an intelligence officer is assigned to an embassy or consulate under a false title—like “cultural attaché” or “trade advisor”—granting them the legal protections of a diplomat. If caught spying, they can't be arrested—only declared *persona non grata* and expelled.
  - **Passport privileges** refer to the ability to travel internationally under multiple or false identities using official or forged government-issued passports. This allows agents to move across borders discreetly, avoid detection, and operate in hostile or restricted regions.

That doesn’t sound like defense.   
It sounds like **statecraft through sabotage**.
