Fueled by Cold War paranoia, the CIA’s primary objective with MK Ultra was to develop effective **tools for psychological control** that could be used against potential enemies. The agency aimed to understand how thoughts, memories, and perceptions could be altered and whether it was possible to **reprogram individuals** under certain conditions. This pursuit went beyond the ordinary scope of intelligence work, delving into the uncharted territories of mind manipulation.

With the goal of using **mind control** for **military and intelligence purposes**, MK Ultra explored ways to induce compliance, alter loyalties, and even extract information from uncooperative subjects. This ambition to master psychological warfare fueled experiments in **drug administration**, **hypnosis**, and **physical conditioning**.
