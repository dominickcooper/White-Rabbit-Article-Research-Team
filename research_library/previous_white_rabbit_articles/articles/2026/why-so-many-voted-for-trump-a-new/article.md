### The Rise of MAGA

**Trump’s Appeal Beyond Party Lines**

Donald Trump’s support didn’t fit the usual mold. While political analysts tried to categorize him as a traditional Republican (**or worse**, in most cases), the reality was more nuanced. Trump supporters were not just **Republicans; they were individuals** frustrated with political elites on both sides of the aisle. Trump gave voice to people who felt alienated, promising to put **"America First"** and tackle issues that others had ignored.

**Dissatisfaction with the Establishment**

The phrase “drain the swamp” became more than a campaign slogan; it represented the public’s resentment towards **Washington’s entrenched establishment**.

A [Pew Research study in 2020](https://www.pewresearch.org/global/2020/02/27/attitudes-toward-elected-officials-voting-and-the-state/) revealed that 65% of Americans felt **elected officials did not care** about the needs of ordinary citizens.

By 2024, **that number** had [jumped](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2024/04/30/more-than-80-of-americans-believe-elected-officials-dont-care-what-people-like-them-think/) to **85%**.

Trump spoke directly to these frustrations, highlighting how the system prioritized elites and special interests over average Americans.

### Challenging the Political Status Quo

**Moving Away from Corporate-Driven Policies**

Historically, Republicans have aligned with corporate interests, but Trump’s rhetoric often focused on **everyday Americans**. He opposed policies that primarily benefited **big corporations** while leaving small businesses and workers behind.

For instance, the **Tax Cuts and Jobs Act** ([TCJA](https://www.investopedia.com/taxes/trumps-tax-reform-plan-explained/)) of 2017 reduced the **corporate** **tax** **rate** from 35% to 21%, intending to stimulate economic growth and job creation. Additionally, the TCJA introduced a 20% [deduction](http://Center for Budget and Policy Priorities) for **pass-through businesses**, which include many small enterprises, thereby reducing their taxable income.

Within [3 months](https://www.investors.com/politics/commentary/how-trump-regulatory-rollback-boosts-small-businesses/) of his 2018 inauguration, President Trump slashed over **[1,500 planned regulatory actions](https://www.marketplace.org/2018/01/19/economy/how-many-regulations-has-trump-actually-killed)**. His administration repealed **22 regulations** for each new rule issued, cutting regulatory costs by **[more than $8 billion](https://www.politico.com/agenda/story/2018/01/20/trumps-regulatory-experiment-year-one-000620)**.

These deregulatory efforts alleviated burdens on small businesses and reflect a shift from traditional Republican approaches, emphasizing support for small businesses and workers over large corporations.

In his words, "I will fight for you with every breath in my body — and I will never, ever let you down."

### Trump and Working-Class Values

**Economic Growth through Targeted Tax Relief**

Trump’s current economic policies focus on directly benefiting working Americans by reducing tax burdens on everyday earnings. For example, his proposal to **eliminate taxes on tips** aims to let service workers keep more of their hard-earned money, boosting disposable income and spending power for millions.

Additionally, Trump has advocated for **eliminating taxes on Social Security income**, a move designed to provide seniors with increased financial stability and reduce the financial strain on retirees. Here are [additional examples](https://www.donaldjtrump.com/platform) of Trump’s current proposals aimed at promoting **economic growth**:

1. **Tax Cuts for Small Businesses:**  
   Trump has proposed further tax cuts specifically for **small businesses** to help them thrive and expand. By lowering tax rates and simplifying the tax filing process, he aims to empower small business owners to invest back into their businesses and create more jobs.
2. **Elimination of the Death Tax:**  
   Trump plans to **repeal the estate tax**, also known as the "death tax." This move is intended to allow families to pass down businesses and properties to future generations without the financial strain of a large tax bill, encouraging long-term investment and economic stability.
3. **Cutting Bureaucratic Red Tape:**  
   Trump has continued to emphasize **deregulation across multiple sectors** to reduce bureaucratic barriers that he argues stifle business growth. His administration set a precedent by cutting 22 regulations for each new one implemented, a trend he aims to continue to help industries and small businesses grow with fewer restrictions.
4. **Manufacturing Incentives:**  
   To revitalize domestic production, Trump proposes offering **tax incentives for manufacturers** who produce goods in the U.S. This initiative aims to bring manufacturing jobs back to American soil, reduce dependency on foreign suppliers, and enhance national economic resilience.
5. **Tax-Free Savings Accounts for Childcare and Elder Care:**  
   Trump has also suggested creating **tax-free savings accounts** for families to use on child care and elder care expenses. By giving families a tax-advantaged way to save, he hopes to make it easier for working parents and caregivers to manage costs, thereby boosting workforce participation.
6. **Reduction of the Capital Gains Tax:**  
   A proposal to **reduce the capital gains tax rate** is also part of Trump’s economic growth strategy. Lowering this tax would encourage investment in stocks, real estate, and small businesses, aiming to spur economic activity and encourage wealth generation.
7. **Tax Credits for Onshoring Jobs:**  
   Trump has suggested offering **tax credits for companies that relocate jobs to the United States**. This initiative is designed to incentivize companies to bring back jobs that have been outsourced, further strengthening the domestic job market and supporting American workers.

These policies collectively aim to **stimulate economic growth, empower American workers, and support small businesses and families** by reducing tax burdens and regulatory constraints.

**Border Security and Self-Reliance**

A major focus of Trump’s administration was **border security**. For Trump, this was not only about national security but also about **protecting American workers** from wage competition. His supporters, particularly in working-class communities, saw this as a measure to preserve jobs and encourage self-reliance.

### A Fresh Appeal to Minority Voters

**Values of Family, Faith, and Economic Empowerment**

### The Broader Coalition of Americans

**Appeal Across Generations**

Trump’s movement reached across generational lines, resonating with **Baby Boomers, Gen X, Millennials, and even Gen Z**. While younger generations have historically leaned left, many Gen X and Millennial voters found in Trump a leader who stood firmly against **government overreach** and voiced their concerns about **censorship** and **lack of representation** in government.

Trump also expanded his appeal by connecting directly with younger audiences through interviews on platforms with widespread reach. He appeared on **Joe Rogan’s podcast**, **Theo Von’s show**, and even engaged with online streamer **Adin Ross** to speak to a new wave of voters.

These appearances showcased his willingness to reach a diverse audience outside traditional media channels, creating a coalition that defied conventional political boundaries. For younger Americans, his strong stance on **free speech** and **government transparency** aligned with their frustrations about censorship, helping to bring a new generation into his movement.

### FAQs

**Why did so many Americans vote for Trump?**  
Many voters saw Trump as a departure from traditional politicians, focusing on American workers and reducing foreign entanglements.

**What is Trump’s "America First" philosophy?**  
It prioritizes American interests, particularly in terms of job creation, security, and economic growth.

**How did Trump appeal to minority voters?**  
Trump emphasized family, faith, and economic opportunity, values that resonated with many Latinos and Hispanics.

**Did Trump challenge traditional Republican policies?**  
Yes, Trump’s policies often diverged from the corporate-focused strategies of past administrations, emphasizing the American worker.

**Is this movement solely about Trump?**  
Not entirely. This movement is a coalition of Americans who feel underserved by the political system and seek meaningful change.
