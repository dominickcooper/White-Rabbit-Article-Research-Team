## BACKGROUND ON THE MANSON MURDERS

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!lCGU!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9f08d874-51ca-4d28-b9c7-f1f4825eba97_1647x1236.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!lCGU!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9f08d874-51ca-4d28-b9c7-f1f4825eba97_1647x1236.jpeg)

Young Charles Manson

[Charles Milles Manson](https://allthatsinteresting.com/charles-manson-cia) was born in **Cincinnati, Ohio, in 1934**, to a **16-year-old mother**, Kathleen Maddox, whose life was marked by instability, petty crime, and a revolving door of partners.

*Well, we’re **not off to a good start.***

His early years were spent bouncing through reform schools, juvenile facilities, and federal institutions. Interestingly, these types of environments often intersected with **[psychological experimentation programs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experimentation_on_prisoners) and [government-funded](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/health/medical-slideshow-code) behavior modification research.**

Manson’s father was listed as **[Colonel Walker Scott](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/116001100/colonel_walker_henderson-scott)**, a man Manson rarely spoke about.

**For clarity:** “Colonel” was his actual first name. **Not a military rank.**

However, public records later indicated that Scott did have military ties: a fact that has sparked speculation that **Manson’s lineage and early life** may have brushed against **the periphery of U.S. military or intelligence circles**.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!eTQs!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F785f3220-26d8-4e63-a74c-adb81cd48e50_1828x1199.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!eTQs!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F785f3220-26d8-4e63-a74c-adb81cd48e50_1828x1199.jpeg)

**But this not the case.**

**[His father](https://www.mansonblog.com/2013/02/colonel-walker-scott.html)** was just a POS who abandoned Charlesand his mother, on top of being an abusive alcoholic.

By the time Manson reached his 30s, he had spent **over half his life in institutional settings**, from reform schools to federal penitentiaries like [McNeil Island](https://www.knkx.org/news/2019-02-19/mcneil-inmates-endured-hepatitis-testing-in-exchange-for-earlier-release-and-acrobatics) and [Terminal Island](https://www.laconservancy.org/japanese-american-history-at-terminal-island/) — both of which ALSO had connections to psychological behavior studies and **parole experiments under federal supervision**.

Manson himself [once said](https://www.mansondirect.org/transa.html), *“I have spent my life in jail, and without parents.”*

While imprisoned, Manson learned to manipulate, charm, and **psychologically dominate**. After his release in 1967 (the **“[Summer of Love](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summer_of_Love)”**) Manson drifted to **San Francisco’s Haight-Ashbury district**, a hub for LSD, counterculture, and, curiously…

**CIA-backed research through the Haight-Ashbury Free Medical Clinic**.

There, Manson would build his cult — a *family* of young drifters, dropouts, and dreamers, whom he molded into instruments of his will.

### **The Murders: Tate–LaBianca, Summer 1969**

On the nights of **August 8th and 9th, 1969**, Los Angeles became the epicenter of a nightmare that would permanently scar the cultural landscape.

At **10050 Cielo Drive**, the home of actress **Sharon Tate** and director **Roman Polanski**, members of Manson’s so-called “Family” — **Tex Watson**, **Susan Atkins**, **Patricia Krenwinkel**, and **Linda Kasabian** — broke in and unleashed unspeakable violence. Tate, eight months pregnant, was murdered alongside **Jay Sebring**, **Abigail Folger**, **Wojciech Frykowski**, and **Steven Parent**.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!3wam!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fc94f49f8-2868-45db-8711-3196d8e52f99_6339x2400.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!3wam!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fc94f49f8-2868-45db-8711-3196d8e52f99_6339x2400.jpeg)

Sharon Tate was murdered in August of 1969. She was married to Roman Polanski who had released his film “Rosemary’s Baby” only a year earlier.

The next night, the killers struck again, this time at the home of **Leno and Rosemary LaBianca**, brutally stabbing both to death. On the walls, the murderers smeared words like **“Healter Skelter”** and **“Rise”** in blood — cryptic messages that fueled national panic.

The murders were portrayed as random, psychotic acts of a cult gone mad. But even then, investigators were puzzled:

*Why were these young, middle-class Americans willing to kill so savagely for a man who barely lifted a finger?*

The deeper question — and one central to the CIA theory — is how Manson managed to exercise such **absolute psychological control** over his followers. How could ordinary people be convinced to kill, smile, and [sing about it afterward](https://x.com/historyinmemes/status/1788534128486309974).

### **The “Helter Skelter” Narrative and Its Official Explanation**

According to prosecutor **Vincent Bugliosi**, Manson’s motive was simple yet deranged: he believed in an imminent **apocalyptic race war** that he called *Helter Skelter* — a term he borrowed from a Beatles song.

In Bugliosi’s account, Manson thought the murders would ignite this war, which would ultimately allow him and his followers to emerge as the rulers of a new social order.

That explanation became canon — immortalized in Bugliosi’s best-selling book *[Helter Skelter](https://www.google.com/books/edition/Helter_Skelter/Fiyw_LeL51MC?hl=en&gbpv=1&printsec=frontcover)* and the subsequent film. It painted Manson as a delusional cult leader driven by racial paranoia and psychedelic madness.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GKGR!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fb36edce2-768e-4b5f-b1ab-a52fb9098db6_533x281.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GKGR!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fb36edce2-768e-4b5f-b1ab-a52fb9098db6_533x281.jpeg)

But not everyone bought it.

Writers, researchers, and former law enforcement officers have long argued that the *Helter Skelter* theory **oversimplified and obscured the true origins of Manson’s psychological influence**.

**Tom O’Neill’s groundbreaking book** ***[Chaos: Charles Manson, the CIA, and the Secret History of the Sixties](https://www.amazon.com/Chaos-Charles-Manson-History-Sixties/dp/0316477540/ref=asc_df_0316477540?tag=bingshoppinga-20&linkCode=df0&hvadid=80470622177442&hvnetw=o&hvqmt=e&hvbmt=be&hvdev=c&hvlocint=&hvlocphy=61981&hvtargid=pla-4584070153625634&psc=1)*** revealed evidence suggesting **Manson’s parole officer, Roger Smith**, was connected to **Haight-Ashbury’s drug research network**, which in turn was monitored by figures tied to **Dr. Louis Jolyon West** — a key psychiatrist involved in MKUltra.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!-sXL!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F05bab4ef-36fd-481d-90bb-6c6d82148d5c_228x342.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!-sXL!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F05bab4ef-36fd-481d-90bb-6c6d82148d5c_228x342.jpeg)

### **Jolly’s Bio**

[Dr. Louis Jolyon “Jolly” West](https://thecinemaholic.com/dr-louis-jolly-west/) was born in 1924 in Madison, Wisconsin, and grew up to become one of the most prominent psychiatrists of his generation. After earning his M.D. from the **University of Minnesota**, he joined the **U.S. Army Air Force**, serving as a psychiatrist during World War II.”

After the war, West quickly rose through the ranks of academic psychiatry. By the 1950s, he was the **head of psychiatry at the University of Oklahoma**, where he began conducting research on **LSD, hypnosis, and behavior modification**. These subjects would soon place him at the **epicenter of the CIA’s secret mind-control project — MKUltra**.
