## Continuity of Operations Plan (COOP)

A **Continuity of Operations Plan (COOP)** ensures that essential government functions can continue during a crisis, such as a natural disaster, attack, or other major disruption. These plans often include measures to **relocate government officials**, establish **alternative** **communication** channels, and implement **emergency** **protocols** to maintain order.

**Example**: After the 9/11 attacks, U.S. government agencies activated their COOP protocols, [relocating key officials](https://www.nbcnews.com/storyline/9-11-anniversary/secrets-9-11-new-details-chaos-nukes-emerge-n645711) to secure locations like **Mount Weather**, an underground facility designed to ensure the continuity of government operations in case of a national emergency. President Bush at the time was aboard Air Force One [for 8 hours](https://www.politico.com/magazine/story/2016/09/were-the-only-plane-in-the-sky-214230#:~:text=For%20the%20next%20eight%20hours,traversed%20the%20eastern%20United%20States.), another measure of COOP.

## Soft Coup

A **soft coup** refers to the gradual, non-violent overthrow or subversion of a government. Unlike traditional coups that involve military force, a soft coup is typically orchestrated through political maneuvering, manipulation of public opinion, or bureaucratic influence, allowing unelected entities to take control or exert influence over a country's leadership.

**Example**: The [events in](https://brownpoliticalreview.org/2017/05/coup-overthrew-dilma/) **[Brazil in 2016](https://brownpoliticalreview.org/2017/05/coup-overthrew-dilma/)**, where President **Dilma Rousseff** was impeached, has the signs of a soft coup. Dilma Rousseff's impeachment was driven by corrupt politicians who wanted to halt investigations, such as **[Operation Car Wash](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2017/jun/01/brazil-operation-car-wash-is-this-the-biggest-corruption-scandal-in-history)**, which threatened their power.

Leaked transcripts revealed that the goal of her removal was to end corruption probes, and not based on the fiscal wrongdoing she was accused of. Additionally, media outlets dominated by Brazil’s elite swayed public opinion against her, and Michel Temer's government immediately reversed key progressive policies after her removal.

## Stay-Behind Networks

**Stay-behind networks** refer to secret groups that were established during the Cold War by NATO and intelligence agencies in European countries. These groups were created to resist a possible Soviet invasion by carrying out sabotage, guerrilla warfare, and intelligence activities in the occupied territories.

**Example**: **Operation Gladio**, which operated in countries like Italy, Belgium, and Turkey, involved these stay-behind networks. Though intended to combat communism, Gladio was linked to far-right terrorism, including bombings and assassinations that some believe were part of a broader strategy of tension to justify authoritarian measures.

## National Security State

A **national security state** is a government that prioritizes military and intelligence operations over civil liberties in the name of protecting national security. In such a state, surveillance, secrecy, and the concentration of power in security agencies become central features of governance.

**Example**: The **United States after 9/11** has been described as evolving into a [national security state](https://ciss.princeton.edu/events/911-fbi-and-national-security), with the passage of the **Patriot Act** and the expansion of the **Department of Homeland Security**. These developments increased government surveillance capabilities and placed greater emphasis on counterterrorism, often at the expense of individual privacy and rights.

## Declassification

**Declassification** is the process of removing the secrecy status of government documents, making them available to the public. Governments declassify information to increase transparency, though crucial details related to the [deep state](https://thewhiterabbitreport.substack.com/p/how-the-deep-state-shapes-us-foreign) remain hidden despite these efforts.

**Example**: The **[JFK Assassination Records](https://www.archives.gov/press/press-releases/2023/nr23-43)** have undergone multiple rounds of declassification, yet many documents remain classified or heavily redacted, fueling speculation about the [involvement of secretive government entities](https://thewhiterabbitreport.substack.com/p/has-the-deep-state-ever-been-exposed) in the assassination.

## Compartmentalization

**Compartmentalization** refers to the practice of dividing information into distinct sections, each of which is accessible only to individuals who need to know specific details. This method is often used in intelligence agencies to prevent the spread of sensitive information, making it difficult for anyone to see the complete picture of an operation or policy.

**Example**: The **[Manhattan Project](https://www.nps.gov/mapr/faqs.htm#:~:text=Work%20on%20the%20Manhattan%20Project,of%20the%20facilities%20they%20built.)**, which developed the atomic bomb during World War II, was a highly compartmentalized operation. Only a few individuals had access to all the information, ensuring that secrecy was maintained despite the large number of people involved.

**[Project Hexagon](https://www.ctinsider.com/connecticutmagazine/news-people/article/The-story-of-the-Hexagon-a-Danbury-built-spy-17046062.php)**, also known as **KH-9 Hexagon**, was a top-secret U.S. reconnaissance satellite program during the Cold War. It was also highly compartmentalized, with only a few individuals having access to the full scope of the project. The program developed advanced film-return spy satellites to gather critical intelligence on Soviet military activities.

Secrecy was maintained through strict compartmentalization, ensuring that most of those involved in the project only knew about specific tasks rather than the full mission, which continued successfully for over a decade.

## The Iron Triangle

The **Iron Triangle** describes the relationship between **government agencies**, **legislators**, and **interest groups**, particularly in areas like defense. These three entities work together to shape policy, often prioritizing their own interests over the public good.

**Example**: An example of how **Iron Triangles** operate can be seen in the relationship between **pharmaceutical companies**, the **U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)**, and **Congress**. Pharmaceutical companies often exert significant influence over FDA decisions, potentially leading to the approval of drugs with harmful side effects. Congress, in turn, supports these companies by passing favorable legislation, largely influenced by lobbying efforts.

The close cooperation between these three entities—the industry, the regulatory agency, and lawmakers—results in prioritizing corporate interests over public safety, showcasing the dangers of the **Iron Triangle**.

## Fusion Centers

**Fusion Centers** are collaborative efforts that bring together various levels of government, including federal, state, and local agencies, to share intelligence and coordinate responses to potential security threats. While designed to improve national security, fusion centers have been criticized for overreach and for collecting information on law-abiding citizens.

**Example**: Fusion centers were established across the U.S. after the **9/11 attacks** to facilitate better communication between agencies. However, a [2012 Senate report](https://cdn.govexec.com/media/gbc/docs/pdfs_edit/100312cc1.pdf) criticized them for [producing little useful intelligence](https://foreignpolicy.com/2012/10/03/senate-report-says-national-intelligence-fusion-centers-have-been-useless/) and for overstepping by monitoring activists and protesters, including the **[Occupy Wall Street movement](https://www.justiceonline.org/litigation/occupy-movement-crackdwn/)**.

If you read [Part 1](https://thewhiterabbitreport.substack.com/p/deep-state-vocabulary-false-flags), you’ll find it interesting that **DHS Secretary Janet Napolitano** called the fusion centers "a critical part of our nation’s homeland security capabilities."

## Total Information Awareness (TIA)

**Total Information Awareness (TIA)** was a [controversial program](https://www.aclu.org/documents/qa-pentagons-total-information-awareness-program) launched by the U.S. government in 2003 to collect and analyze massive amounts of data on individuals in order to predict and prevent terrorist activities. The program faced backlash over privacy concerns, and although it was officially canceled, many believe its activities continued under different guises.

**Total Information Awareness** was the brainchild of **John Poindexter**, the Reagan administration official who got his conviction in the Iran-Contra scandal [overturned](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/politics/1991/11/16/appeals-court-reverses-poindexter-conviction/e212e1ec-5924-4336-ac3c-7f1bfe0bd517/) on appeal. 

**Example**: The **[PRISM program](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2013/jun/06/us-tech-giants-nsa-data)**, revealed by **Edward Snowden** in 2013, is a [continuation of the TIA initiative,](https://www.msnbc.com/all-in/prism-there-was-total-information-awar-msna64853) as it involved the NSA collecting private data from tech companies like **Google** and **Facebook**, sparking widespread concerns about government surveillance.

## Martial Law

**Martial law** refers to the temporary imposition of direct military control over civilian populations, typically in response to an emergency or during wartime. Under martial law, civil rights are suspended, and military authorities take over the functions of government.

## Rendition

**Rendition** is the practice of transferring individuals, often suspected terrorists, from one country to another, usually to a country with fewer legal protections, for detention and interrogation. It is often associated with extrajudicial actions and human rights abuses.

**Example**: **[Maher Arar](https://amnesty.ca/legal-brief/case-maher-arar/)**, a Syrian-born Canadian citizen, was detained by **U.S. authorities** in 2002 while transiting through **JFK Airport**. Suspected of having ties to **al-Qaeda**, Arar was sent to **Syria** as part of an extraordinary rendition. In Syria, he was detained and tortured for nearly a year before being released without charges.

The Canadian government issued a formal apology and compensation to Arar for its role in his wrongful detention.

**[Khalid El-Masri](https://www.coe.int/en/web/impact-convention-human-rights/-/compensation-and-official-apology-for-victim-of-cia-torture-and-secret-rendition-)**[,](https://www.coe.int/en/web/impact-convention-human-rights/-/compensation-and-official-apology-for-victim-of-cia-torture-and-secret-rendition-) a German citizen of Lebanese descent, was mistakenly identified as a terrorist and **abducted by the CIA** in 2003. He was flown to a secret detention facility in **Afghanistan** and held for months, where he endured **harsh interrogations**.

He was eventually released.

**[Binyam Mohamed](https://www.amnesty.org/ar/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/eur450012009en.pdf)**, a UK resident of Ethiopian descent, was **arrested in Pakistan** in 2002 and **subjected to rendition by the CIA**. He was transferred to **Morocco**, where he was **tortured for 18 months**, and then moved to a CIA "**black** **site**" in Afghanistan before being sent to **Guantánamo Bay**.

After years of detention without trial, Mohamed was released in 2009.

## Parallel Construction

**Parallel construction** is a law enforcement technique where evidence obtained illegally or through surveillance is re-packaged and presented as if it were obtained through legitimate means. This allows authorities to use sensitive or illegally gathered intelligence without disclosing its source in court.

**Example**: One of the most well-known instances of parallel construction involves the **DEA's Special Operations Division (SOD)**. [According to reports](https://www.reuters.com/article/world/uk/exclusive-us-directs-agents-to-cover-up-program-used-to-investigate-americans-idUSBRE97409S/), SOD would receive intelligence from sources such as **NSA surveillance, wiretaps, and informants**.

However, to avoid disclosing the true source of the information, the DEA would use **parallel construction** to build a separate, lawful case that could be presented in court. For example, if the NSA provided information about a drug deal, DEA agents might conduct a "routine traffic stop" and then "discover" the drugs, so the defense could not challenge the origins of the intelligence.
