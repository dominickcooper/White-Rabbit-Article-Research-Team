By the early 1970s, Courant’s computing labs were humming with **[DARPA-linked research](https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/tr/pdf/ADA115440.pdf)**, numerical modeling, **cryptography-adjacent math**—the kind of quiet work intelligence agencies covet.
