- **[GRILL FLAME](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP96-00788R001000410001-6.pdf)**
- **[CENTER LANE](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP96-00789R002100180002-8.pdf)**

From a **[Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA) overview](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP96-00789R002100240001-2.pdf)**, Project Sun Streak is described as follows:

> “Project Sun Streak deals with the use of psychoenergetics in the collection of intelligence information.”

The DIA defines **psychoenergetics** as a discipline divided into two broad classes:

1. **Mental effects on the physical world**
2. **Purely mental collection of information**

The document further defines psychoenergetics as:

> “The process by which an individual may psychically interact with objects, locations, organisms, or events.”

It breaks this down into:

- **Psychokinesis** — physical actions performed by mental powers
- **ESP / Telepathy / Remote Viewing** — perceptions that cannot be explained by known sensory means

## The Ark of the Covenant Problem

At some point, programs like Sun Streak crossed an invisible line.

They weren’t just tracking missiles or hostages anymore.  
They were tasked with targets that blurred **intelligence and mythology**.

Among the documented taskings:  
**Search efforts [related to the Ark of the Covenant](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP96-00789R001300180002-7.pdf).**

[![Ark of the Covenant | Indiana Jones Wiki | Fandom](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!2h0D!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F36ac7b11-5674-489d-bf69-597ad7d8dc02_620x418.webp "Ark of the Covenant | Indiana Jones Wiki | Fandom")](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!2h0D!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F36ac7b11-5674-489d-bf69-597ad7d8dc02_620x418.webp)

That detail is often dismissed as fringe.

**But it shouldn’t be.**

Because it tells us something critical:  
The intelligence community wasn’t limiting remote viewing to *plausible* targets.

They were testing the **edges of reality itself**.

And, if Project Sun Streak certainly did deal with **manipulating TIME**…

*Who’s to say that the didn’t already know the Ark really did exist?*

> “The target is fashioned of wood, gold and silver…The target is hidden underground, dark and wet were all aspects of the location of the target…Individuals opening the container by prying or striking are destroyed by the container’s protectors through the use of a power unknown to us.”

**Spoiler Alert:*****Allegedly,** they never found the Ark of the Covenant.*

Also, this experiment was conducted in 1988.  
The first Indiana Jones movie came out in 1981.

**Keep an open mind,**   
but don’t let your brain fall out.

## What This All Adds Up To

Project Sun Streak wasn’t canceled because it failed.

It was canceled after:

- Nearly **25 years of funding**
- Roughly **$20 million spent** (over $100 million in today’s dollars)
- Successful operational taskingsRepeated confirmations unsettling enough to demand secrecy
- And research paths that raised ethical and political liabilities no one wanted exposed

If nothing worked, none of this would have happened.

And if it worked *too well*…

That might explain why it ended.  
**If it did at all.**
