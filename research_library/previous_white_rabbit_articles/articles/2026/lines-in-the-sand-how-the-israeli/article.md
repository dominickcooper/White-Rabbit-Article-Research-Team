In **Part 1**, we traced the road to crisis: the rise of **Zionism** as a political movement, **Britain’s imperial meddling**, and how promises made during World War I—to both **Jews and Arabs**—set the stage for a land divided and a people betrayed.

[LINK HERE](https://open.substack.com/pub/thewhiterabbitreport/p/origins-israeli-palestine-conflict)

Under the **British Mandate**, Zionist organizations built a parallel state with institutions, land purchases, and even armed militias. Meanwhile, **Palestinians resisted** through general strikes and uprisings, only to be met with **brutal British repression**. The 1939 **White Paper** tried to course-correct—limiting Jewish immigration and proposing joint independence—but by then, the tension had boiled over. Jewish militias had turned against both Arabs and the British. The region was a tinderbox.

Then came **World War II**.
