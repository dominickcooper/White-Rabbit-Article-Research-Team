**[DARPA’s Wireless Brain-Computer Interface](https://spectrum.ieee.org/darpa-funds-ambitious-neurotech-program)** has moved from science fiction into military reality. The same agency that funded decades of “behavioral modification” programs now leads the race toward **mind-machine fusion.**

Publicly, these interfaces promise to [heal the blind](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7901898/), [restore mobility](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-82252-7), or unlock new forms of communication.

But beneath the polished press releases lies a troubling truth: this technology mirrors the same objectives once pursued under MKULTRA—control, influence, and manipulation of human thought.

In other words, DARPA didn’t end MKULTRA.  
**It upgraded it.**

And thanks to new advances in A.I., nanomaterials, and frequency-based neural access, the tools they have now make the old program look primitive.
