## **Part 1 Recap – Who Were the Real Men Behind the Movie?**

In **Part 1**, we peeled back the fictional façade of *The Good Shepherd* to expose the real-life figures behind the film. While Edward Wilson served as the stoic protagonist, he was a composite—an amalgam of shadowy men whose hands shaped post-war history. The character drew most heavily from **James Jesus Angleton**, the obsessive and deeply paranoid head of CIA counterintelligence, known for seeing moles where others saw friends. We also examined OSS origins and how early wartime relationships seeded something far more expansive and enduring.
