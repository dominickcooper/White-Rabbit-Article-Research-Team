### Reason One - Conditions That Changed the Rules

[![A Presidential Election During the Time of COVID-19 | Baker Institute](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0ijS!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F79bc8e88-a5da-48f4-8644-dcc11dc0dcf4_3000x2000.jpeg "A Presidential Election During the Time of COVID-19 | Baker Institute")](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0ijS!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F79bc8e88-a5da-48f4-8644-dcc11dc0dcf4_3000x2000.jpeg)

The 2020 presidential election did not occur under normal circumstances. That alone does not imply misconduct. But it does mean that the procedures governing the vote departed (often dramatically) from historical precedent. Understanding those departures is essential before any evaluation of outcomes can take place.

#### COVID-Era Emergency Protocols

In response to COVID-19, states invoked emergency authorities that reshaped election administration in real time. Polling **locations were consolidated**, **staffing was reduced**, observation **procedures were altered**, and **deadlines were extended**. Signature standards and ballot-handling protocols were modified **in the name of public health.**

These changes were often implemented by **executive** or **administrative action** rather than through the traditional legislative process.

#### Unprecedented Mail-In Ballot Volume

At the same time, the scale of mail-in voting expanded dramatically. While absentee voting had long existed, 2020 saw **tens of millions of ballots** mailed nationwide, in some cases ***[without](https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/millions-mail-in-ballots/)*** **[being explicitly requested](https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/millions-mail-in-ballots/)**.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!7HUB!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F43b390bf-7c23-42e6-a572-e07e1e9e0a6a_811x468.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!7HUB!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F43b390bf-7c23-42e6-a572-e07e1e9e0a6a_811x468.png)

Even [Snopes](https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/millions-mail-in-ballots/) had a hard time dancing around Trump’s claim of “millions of mail-in ballots were sent to people who never asked for them.” Turns out they were right.

**Eligibility requirements were broadened**, and mail-in voting shifted from a limited alternative to a **primary method** of participation. Election systems (verification, tracking, curing, and tabulation) were forced to operate at **volumes they had never previously encountered**, fundamentally altering how and when votes were counted.

**This shift disrupted long-standing reporting patterns.**

Compounding these changes was **the way election rules were modified**.

In multiple states, **adjustments to deadlines, signature verification, observer access, and ballot acceptance** were enacted through **governors’ orders**, **court rulings**, or **election officials** rather than legislatures.

These changes were often challenged, not on policy grounds alone, but on **constitutional authority**.

The courts frequently declined to rule on the merits, citing timing or procedural constraints.

What remains indisputable is that the rules governing federal elections were altered rapidly, inconsistently, and close to Election Day creating **conditions without clear historical precedent.**

- He pulled **74 million votes** (a gain of 11 million from 2016) won **2,497 counties**, took **18 of 19 bellwether counties**, carried **Florida, Ohio, and Iowa**, and Republicans *won* House seats.

*Wow! Almost exactly like Obama!*

**Historically speaking**, that combination is almost a sure indicator of reelection. County dominance, bellwethers, down-ballot strength.

The usual signals all flashed **green for election.**

Then there’s **Biden 2020**.

- No traditional campaign. Minimal rallies. Sparse ground presence.
- Yet somehow he posted **81 million votes**, the most in U.S. history, while winning just **477 counties**.
- He captured **only 1 of the 19 bellwether counties**, lost **Florida, Ohio, and Iowa**, and Democrats *lost* House seats.

**These are sworn statements.** You don’t have to accept them as gospel but dismissing them outright requires **pretending they don’t exist.**

*Oh boy*.

- Some Milwaukee wards reported **turnout rates above 87%**, a threshold election analysts often flag as statistically abnormal.

Finally, zoom out.  
Across **Michigan and Pennsylvania**, Biden’s margins in mail-in ballots were **several times larger** than in other swing states. In Michigan, Republicans reportedly requested more absentee ballots than Democrats yet Biden led absentee returns by **38 points**.

*Huh*…

For that math to work, he would have needed virtually **every Democrat**, **every independent**, and a sizable slice of **Republicans** (*yeah right*). This is a scenario that clashes with both exit polls and historical behavior.
