It was a move that sent shockwaves through Washington. To the CIA, it was more than another Soviet expansion; **it was the perfect battlefield.** America could bleed its rival dry without firing a single shot under its own flag. Afghanistan became the pawn in a silent war, and **[Operation Cyclone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Cyclone)** was the opening move.

The plan was simple but ruthless: flood the region with weapons, training, and cash to turn Afghan resistance fighters (**[the Mujahideen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afghan_mujahideen)**) into Moscow’s nightmare.
