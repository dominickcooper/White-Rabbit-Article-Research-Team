Vaccines are everywhere—from your baby’s first doctor visit to routine boosters as adults. They’re touted as life-savers, hailed as one of **modern medicine’s biggest breakthroughs**. But here’s the big question: *Are all vaccines really necessary?* Or are we taking shots in the dark, trusting a system that might need a second look?

The journey of vaccines started small, with just a few essential immunizations. Over time, the schedule grew, adding more and more shots, each claiming to shield us from diseases. Yet, as the list of recommendations expanded, so did concerns. Today, with dozens of shots on the roster—and debates raging louder than ever—it's fair to ask: *Are we prioritizing public health, or are we overreaching?*
