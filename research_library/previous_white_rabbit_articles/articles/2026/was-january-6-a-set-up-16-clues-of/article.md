## 3. THE SPEED OF THE BREACH

If you’ve ever seen a crowd storm a secured building, you know it’s not easy. Security barriers, checkpoints, and armed guards typically keep chaos at bay. Yet on **January 6,** the U.S. Capitol—one of the most protected buildings in the world—fell in a matter of minutes.

**Here’s [the timeline](https://www.jan-6.com/january-6-timeline) that raises eyebrows:**

- **1:10 PM:** Trump concludes his speech:   
  *“...we’re going to walk down Pennsylvania Avenue...to the Capitol...to try and give them the kind of pride and boldness that they need to take back our country."*
- **1:12 PM:** Back up police arrive to the scene.
- **1:17 PM:** Rioter advance on the West side stalls
- **1:26 PM:** Lawmakers evacuated from Senate Chamber.
- **1:50 PM:** Windows are shattered by [Dominic Pezzola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominic_Pezzola), and entry points open up.
- **2:09 PM:** The crowd breaks the police line on the NW steps and quickly ascends to the NW terrace.

[![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!AQ5B!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2b21336f-9cba-4217-b19e-dbd56ce666f7_501x327.avif)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!AQ5B!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F2b21336f-9cba-4217-b19e-dbd56ce666f7_501x327.avif)

From the first push (*windows shattering*) to the full breach? **Just 19 minutes.** That’s faster than most food deliveries.

For a building with **layers of security**—from fencing to surveillance to armed officers —such a swift collapse feels…off. Was it simply a case of being overwhelmed, or did someone open the gates, metaphorically (or literally)?

Critics argue that **the speed suggests coordination**. Videos show barriers being moved aside with minimal resistance, almost as if there were an “invisible green light.” Some officers were even filmed stepping aside, raising questions about orders from higher up.

Could a ragtag group of protesters really outmaneuver the **entire Capitol security apparatus** that quickly? Or was the stage set for them to succeed?

Add this to the list of oddities: **no full lockdown was triggered** until after the breach ([2:16pm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timeline_of_the_January_6_United_States_Capitol_attack)). Compare that to other federal buildings where even a minor disturbance triggers an immediate security clampdown.

- October 3, 2013 – U.S. Capitol Lockdown ([Car Chase and Shooting](https://www.washingtonpost.com/sf/style/2014/11/26/how-miriam-careys-u-turn-at-a-white-house-checkpoint-led-to-her-death/))  
  **4 MINUTES**  
  A woman, Miriam Carey, attempted to breach a White House vehicle barrier at around 2:16 p.m., leading to a high-speed car chase toward the Capitol. She was shot by police near the Capitol at approximately 2:20 p.m., prompting a lockdown of the Capitol and surrounding federal buildings.
- March 28, 2016 – U.S. Capitol Lockdown ([Shooting at Visitor Center](https://www.tennessean.com/story/news/2016/04/01/faith-frustration-mark-life-of-antioch-man-shot-in-dc/82434564/))  
  **1-2 MINUTES**

  A man, Larry Russell Dawson, entered the Capitol Visitor Center around 2:39 p.m. and drew a pellet gun, prompting a Capitol Police officer to shoot him. The Capitol complex was placed on lockdown to ensure safety.
- August 22 2017 – White House Lockdown ([Suspicious Package](https://www.washingtonpost.com/local/public-safety/white-house-placed-on-lockdown-for-suspicious-package-investigation/2017/08/22/f40278a8-875e-11e7-961d-2f373b3977ee_story.html))  
  **5 MINUTES**A suspicious package was reported near the White House fence line, leading to a partial lockdown of the White House and nearby federal buildings.
- July 8, 2016 – U.S. Capitol Lockdown ([Suspicious Person](https://www.politico.com/story/2016/07/capitol-lockdown-225287))  
  **5-7 MINUTES**A suspicious person was reported around 9:00 a.m.., leading to a lockdown of the of the Capitol for about 30 minutes. A lock down “out of an abundance of caution.”

Was this the result of **incompetence, bad planning, or something more deliberate?** When the most secure halls of power fall in under 20 minutes, it’s hard not to ask: **who wanted it to happen this way?**
