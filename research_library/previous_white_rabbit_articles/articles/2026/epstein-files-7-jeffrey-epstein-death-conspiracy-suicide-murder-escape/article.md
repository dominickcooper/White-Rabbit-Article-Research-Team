### Autopsy Aftermath

[![Jeffrey Epstein Death: Autopsy Doctor Explains Delayed Suicide Ruling -  Business Insider](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/88e1f5fd-cfaa-447c-bd7d-63d4fe952485_800x587.jpeg "Jeffrey Epstein Death: Autopsy Doctor Explains Delayed Suicide Ruling -  Business Insider")](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!DgHp!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F88e1f5fd-cfaa-447c-bd7d-63d4fe952485_800x587.jpeg)

So things are already *fishy* in the “suicide” narrative.

**Then comes the autopsy.**

**Dr. Michael Baden** (a forensic pathologist) is hired by Epstein’s brother, Mark. Baden and other colleagues (like Dr. Peter Cummings) notice some **more** unusual things:

- Epstein had three fractures: bilateral (both sides) of the thyroid cartilage cornua + fracture of the left greater cornu of the hyoid bone, all with surrounding soft-tissue hemorrhages.

  - Baden argued these require **significant compressive force** more typical of **manual or ligature strangulation** (especially from behind) than the relatively passive downward pull of a short-drop hanging from a bunk bed.
  - [He stated he had reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_of_Jeffrey_Epstein) “over a thousand jail hangings/suicides in New York City prisons over the past 40–50 years” and “never seen” three such fractures in a suicidal hanging.
  - [Baden and others noted the pattern](https://www.nytimes.com/2019/10/30/nyregion/jeffrey-epstein-homicide-autopsy-michael-baden.html)—multiple **rigid-structure fractures** with hemorrhage—is more consistent with **active crushing pressure** in homicidal strangulation than the variable forces in suspension hanging .
- The furrow was described as narrow (1/8” to 1/2” wide) and had specific features (noncircumferential, ascending/peaking on the sides, right-sided bifurcation anterior to the ear, central anterior position over the thyroid cartilage).

  - Baden claimed the **wound/furrow was much thinner** than the wide strip of bedsheet actually used as the ligature.
  - There was blood on the neck but **none on the bedsheet itself**.
  - He noted the **furrow’s central neck position** (not higher under the mandibles/jawline) is atypical for suicidal hanging and more consistent with a thinner ligature (e.g., cord or wire) applied in strangulation.
  - **This mismatch** suggests the bedsheet may not have produced the fatal injury, implying a different ligature or staged scene.
- The autopsy noted plethoric face/head, prominent petechiae (face, eyebrows, conjunctivae, lips, tongue), and confluent hemorrhages.

  - [Baden argued](https://www.miamiherald.com/news/state/florida/article236809668.html) certain patterns of conjunctival/eye hemorrhages are more common in strangulation than in typical hangings, where venous congestion can occur but is often less pronounced in short-drop suspensions.
- No anterior strap-muscle hemorrhages (only one small posterior paraspinal hemorrhage) and no vertebral fractures were seen as more compatible with strangulation than a dynamic hanging.

  - Baden questioned why Epstein would use a bedsheet when stronger/longer materials (e.g., wires/tubing from his sleep-apnea machine) were available in the cell.

**[Baden concluded](https://www.ndtv.com/world-news/strangled-not-hanged-doctor-who-observed-jeffrey-epstein-autopsy-makes-big-claim-11002210):**

“**The evidence points to homicide rather than suicide**” and called for further investigation, saying the findings were “much more consistent with a crushing injury caused by homicidal strangulation than by hanging by suicide.”
