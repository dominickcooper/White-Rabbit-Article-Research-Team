# Editorial correction note for WR-000032

Canonical article: https://thewhiterabbitreport.substack.com/p/from-gladio-to-guyana-jonestown-and

The canonical article was checked during repository reconciliation on September 20,
2026. It still describes the International Police Academy as a “CIA front” in the Dan
Mitrione paragraph and summary. The canonical archive copy therefore retains that
published wording.

Future editorial work should use the more precise distinction developed during the
USAID/Office of Public Safety research:

> Calling the International Police Academy (IPA) a CIA front captures part of the
> reality, but it blurs an important distinction. IPA was a real U.S. government police
> academy run through the Office of Public Safety and AID. The separate International
> Police Services operation was the documented CIA proprietary. The CIA used the broader
> OPS/IPA system for liaison, trainee assessment, information exchange, intelligence
> relationship-building, and pathways into selected CIA-controlled follow-on training.

Condensed replacement considered for the article:

> Not a CIA front. A CIA backdoor.

This note preserves the correction without silently converting the published-article
archive into an unpublished revised edition. Any future publication change should be
verified against the canonical URL and then synchronized normally.
