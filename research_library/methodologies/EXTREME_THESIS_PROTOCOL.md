# Extreme-Thesis Protocol

This protocol governs investigations whose strongest coherent theory alleges covert,
deniable, compartmented, conspiratorial, outsourced, or otherwise intentionally hidden
conduct. It is a research method, not permission to publish a predetermined conclusion.
The point is to test the strongest serious thesis without quietly weakening it before the
evidence hunt, then reduce it as far as the record requires.

## Source-corpus authority

Anything the author deliberately places in an article `sources/` directory, a series
`shared_sources/` directory, or another location explicitly identified as author-provided
is admissible evidence. It may be used for facts, quotations, statistics, dates,
testimony, relationships, and claims without independent recovery of the source or its
underlying records. The folder is the author's evidentiary corpus, not a queue awaiting
Codex approval.

Admissibility does not erase provenance or make every item equally strong. Preserve the
source type, author or witness, page/chapter/location, date, and exact scope. A scholar's
report of a named witness supports “the witness told the scholar X”; it does not become
“agency records prove X.” A missing archival document never automatically excludes the
supplied claim. Record what the supplied source says, what underlying source it cites,
whether that source was recovered, and what independent corroboration was or was not
found.

## Required order

When this protocol activates, work in this order:

1. State the strongest logically coherent thesis worth testing. Do not soften it first.
2. Break the thesis into testable propositions.
3. Predict the observable footprint that should exist if each proposition is true.
4. Mine author-supplied, inherited series, and prior-project sources; then search
   aggressively for direct and circumstantial proof.
5. Search historical precedent and derive modern search signatures from past mechanisms.
6. Follow people, organizations, money, programs, contracts, intermediaries, footnotes,
   bibliographies, document numbers, hearings, cases, cables, grants, and archival IDs.
7. Build cumulative connection chains. A supported edge may become the premise for the
   next research question without becoming proof of the whole chain.
8. Assemble the strongest evidence-convergence case, with dependencies visible.
9. Seek disconfirming evidence and the strongest ordinary or alternative explanation.
10. Run Zebra Protocol only after the strongest case and contrary record are assembled.
11. Reduce the thesis clause by clause.
12. Complete Story Decision, then write the strongest surviving version.

Zebra Protocol is adjudication, not preemptive moderation. A HORSE or CONTRADICTED result
is a successful investigation when it best fits the evidence.

## Evidence convergence and stepping stones

Do not reset the investigation to zero after every edge. An established fact or strong
inference may become a premise for the next question. Every edge retains its own label,
and no edge silently inherits the strength of another.

Do not reason: “A does not prove X; B does not prove X; C does not prove X; therefore
there is no meaningful evidence for X.” Ask what A + B + C show together. Independent
streams can increase weight; citations that repeat the same witness, document, dataset,
or reporting chain are one stream and must not be double-counted.

Research artifacts may use these conclusion labels:

- DIRECTLY DOCUMENTED
- CORROBORATED STRONG INFERENCE
- HIGH-DIAGNOSTIC PATTERN
- PLAUSIBLE CONNECTION
- LEAD
- SPECULATION
- CONTRADICTED

A strong inference is a valid article-level conclusion when the premises, bridges,
dependencies, and meaningful boundary are shown. A weak edge stays weak even when the
complete chain is important.

## Documentary-footprint test

Do not demand a single document describing an entire hidden operation. Ask what records a
distributed or deniable system should leave: role division, personnel overlap, common
recipients, funding sequences, statutory workarounds, contractor layers, intelligence
access, concealed sponsorship, synchronized timing, common objectives, interagency
coordination, successor relationships, information sharing, offshore entities, or
functional migration. Evaluate the combination and its plausible alternatives.

## Books and secondary works

Author-supplied books, monographs, dissertations, memoirs, and investigative works are
usable evidence and major lead generators, not mere context. For consequential claims,
preserve page/chapter and attribution; inspect footnotes and bibliographies; record the
underlying citation; try to acquire it; and search recovered records for names, programs,
organizations, document identifiers, grants, contracts, and new connections. Failure to
recover an underlying item changes the provenance note, not the supplied work's
admissibility. Do not reject a supplied work merely because its interpretation is
controversial.

## Historical precedent

Ask whether the actor or system has used the alleged mechanism before. If so, document the
mechanism, identify its observable footprint, and search the current case for analogous
footprints. Precedent changes research strategy and prior plausibility; it does not prove
recurrence. Label analogy, institutional descent, personnel continuity, functional
migration, and direct continuity separately.

## Required artifacts

Use `research/CONNECTION_CHAINS.md` for cumulative chains, `research/BOOK_LEADS.md` for
book-derived claims and targets, and `research/BIBLIOGRAPHY_TRACE.csv` for citation
recovery. When this protocol activates, also create:

- `research/EXTREME_THESIS_LEDGER.md`
- `research/THESIS_REDUCTION.md`
- `research/ZEBRA_ANALYSIS.md`

The ledger records the maximum thesis, propositions, predicted observables, supporting
and disconfirming evidence, dependencies, and next targets. Thesis reduction must show:

ORIGINAL MAXIMUM THESIS → DIRECTLY PROVEN CLAUSES → STRONGLY INFERRED CLAUSES →
PLAUSIBLE CLAUSES → FAILED / CONTRADICTED CLAUSES → FINAL SURVIVING THESIS.

The final thesis is the strongest version the evidence supports, not the safest version.

## Publication boundary

Methodology labels belong mainly in research artifacts. Publication prose should unfold
as an investigation: show the receipts, accumulate them, state the inference, give the
meaningful boundary once, and keep moving. Do not turn the article into a protocol report.
