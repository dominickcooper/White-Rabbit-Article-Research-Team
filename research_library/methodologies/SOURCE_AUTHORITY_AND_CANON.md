# Source Authority, Canon, and Forward Reasoning

This is the permanent authority for source status in White Rabbit investigations. It
governs Codex research, connection building, story decisions, factual audits, editorial
audits, and publication prose. Preserve provenance internally without turning provenance
into a brake on investigation or a recurring disclaimer in the article.

## Editorial priority is distinct from evidentiary provenance

For choosing **which investigation to conduct**, use this priority:

1. the author's explicit brief or instruction;
2. the central thesis and evidence emerging from the author-selected source corpus;
3. established published White Rabbit canon; and
4. newly discovered external research.

The three authority levels below still govern provenance and claim treatment. Editorial
priority does not make every supplied allegation a primary fact. It prevents external
research from replacing the author's selected investigation with a more conventional,
easier, or safer adjacent story.

Before external research, complete `research/SOURCE_THESIS.md`. Read all supplied material
to the extent its format permits, characterize competing claims, identify the strongest
coherent source-derived investigation, and lock it as the editorial anchor.

## Thesis Lock

Later research may strengthen, refine, narrow, challenge, or disprove individual claims.
It may not silently substitute a different central investigation. If substantive contrary
evidence requires a fundamental change, record ORIGINAL THESIS, CONTRADICTORY EVIDENCE,
FAILED CLAIMS, PRESERVED RESEARCH and PROPOSED DIRECTION, then stop at **AUTHOR THESIS
DECISION REQUIRED**. Missing corroboration alone does not authorize substitution. Thesis
Lock never suppresses contrary evidence or requires publication of a contradicted claim.

## Three levels of source authority

### Level 1 — Published White Rabbit canon

Anything stated as a fact, finding, quotation, statistic, connection, conclusion, or
established premise in a **published** White Rabbit Report or Substack article is project
canon. It may be used as the starting premise for later White Rabbit research without
independent re-verification and without being re-proven before the investigation can move
forward. Published White Rabbit articles are also voice canon: study representative work
for rhythm, narrator presence, questions, humor, blunt transitions, accumulation, and the
way a receipt opens the next rabbit hole.

Do not spend a new article's research budget rebuilding an accepted edge. Recheck it only
when the author requests a recheck, new evidence genuinely contradicts it, the published
article explicitly left it unresolved or speculative, or the exact wording of the earlier
conclusion materially controls the new claim.

Canon retrieval and voice retrieval are separate. Canon retrieval returns actual
published findings, testimony, relationships, timelines and source context that can become
premises and generate next-hop searches. Voice retrieval returns filtered authored prose
for narration and structure; it is not factual support. A title or internal link alone is
not proof that canon affected the investigation.

Canon is not frozen forever. When genuine contrary evidence appears, preserve all four:

- EXISTING CANON;
- NEW CONTRADICTORY EVIDENCE;
- WHY THEY CONFLICT; and
- WHETHER AUTHOR REVIEW IS NEEDED.

Do not silently rewrite, erase, or automatically downgrade existing canon. An external
denial, absence from one database, or failure to locate a command memorandum is not by
itself contradictory evidence.

### Level 2 — Author-supplied source corpus

Anything deliberately placed by the author in an article `sources/` folder, a series
`shared_sources/` folder, or another explicitly author-provided source location is
accepted factual source material. Codex may use its facts, dates, statistics, quotations,
testimony, names, relationships, events, allegations as allegations, and documentary
descriptions without a second verification gate.

Independent corroboration is encouraged because it can strengthen the record, resolve a
conflict, or open a new connection. It is not permission to use Level 2 material. Preserve
source type, witness or author, page/chapter/document location, and exact scope. Never
change testimony into a memorandum, a secondary reconstruction into a contemporaneous
record, or an allegation into a documented act.

### Level 3 — Newly discovered external material

Material Codex independently discovers during the current run follows the ordinary
evidence and provenance workflow. Compare sources, verify consequential claims where
possible, recover originals, distinguish independent from derivative streams, and record
conflicts. Do not apply this Level 3 gate retroactively to Level 1 or Level 2.

## Accepted testimony becomes a premise

When Level 1 or Level 2 material says that a named source testified, stated, recalled, or
told an author that Y happened, ordinarily accept Y as true for investigative reasoning.
Assume good-faith truth unless actual contrary evidence appears. Preserve ACCEPTED
TESTIMONY as its internal source type; do not relabel it DOCUMENTED RECORD.

At first reader-facing use, attribution may be useful: “According to X, Y happened.” After
that, Y may operate naturally inside the established chain without repeating the same
attribution at every step. A missing signed order, confession, declassification, or second
witness does not reset the premise to zero.

The required movement is:

TESTIMONY → ACCEPTED PREMISE → NEXT PERSON / ORGANIZATION / PROGRAM / PLACE / EVENT →
NEXT DOCUMENT → NEW CONNECTION.

Corroborating evidence may strengthen the testimony and should also be inspected for the
new branch it opens. Do not spend B, C, D, and E solely purchasing permission to use A
when A is already an accepted premise.

## Internal epistemic labels

Use these provenance/status labels in research artifacts where they help:

- DOCUMENTED RECORD
- ACCEPTED TESTIMONY
- PUBLISHED WHITE RABBIT CANON
- CORROBORATED INFERENCE
- PLAUSIBLE CONNECTION
- SPECULATION
- CONTRADICTED

The labels preserve source identity and reasoning status. They are not mandatory phrases
for publication prose and must not create an automatic caveat after every finding.

## Connections move forward

An accepted edge normally creates a new research question. Follow, where relevant:

- PERSON → EMPLOYER / FAMILY / INVESTOR / BOARD / INTELLIGENCE / CONTRACT / FOUNDATION /
  BANK / POLITICAL FIGURE / ORGANIZED CRIME;
- COMPANY → CONTRACTOR / SUBCONTRACTOR / INVESTOR / SHARED COUNSEL;
- PROGRAM → PERSONNEL / CONTRACTOR / RECIPIENT;
- RECIPIENT → OTHER PROGRAM; and
- TESTIMONY → NAMED PERSON / PROGRAM / PLACE / EVENT.

Every edge retains provenance. The investigation does not return to zero at the next
node. Broken or genuinely contradicted edges stay visible.

When two or more independent paths converge on the same person, company, bank,
institution, intermediary, location, financing channel or method, investigate the shared
node. Preserve chronology and dependency groups, then test coordination, common
infrastructure, access, ordinary commercial overlap, or coincidence. Shared contacts alone
do not establish common command.

Select roughly three to five high-value rabbit holes per article, adjusted to scope.
Completion requires a pursuit record: searches and identifiers tried, records actually
read, people/institutions/money/logistics followed, connections or contradictions found,
and a DEVELOPED, CONTRADICTED, EXHAUSTED, DEFERRED or BLOCKED disposition. One failed exact
search is never enough for EXHAUSTED.

## Cumulative inference and historical priors

Never evaluate independent A, B, C, and D in isolation and reset the hypothesis after
each. Ask what they indicate together when timing, mechanism, repeated actors, strategic
benefit, and expected signatures align. A circumstantial case can become strong without
one smoking-gun document. Do not double-count citations that derive from the same witness,
record, dataset, or reporting chain.

Historically established mechanisms change the research prior. Front organizations,
proprietaries, unwitting participants, contractor layers, police or narcotics
relationships, cultural or student groups, media, financial intermediaries, covert
recruitment, and offshore structures may become search templates when project canon has
already established their use. Precedent does not prove recurrence; it means the present
hypothesis does not begin from zero.

## Contradiction and disconfirmation

The thesis can fail, but disconfirmation must come from actual contrary evidence. Seek it
after building the strongest case. When accepted sources conflict, record the competing
claims, provenance, chronology, materiality, and whether author review is required. Change
the thesis when the conflict materially warrants it. Do not manufacture balance from a
missing command memo, a routine institutional denial, a database omission, or the presence
of legitimate motives that can coexist with covert or improper ones.

## Research artifacts and publication prose

Research artifacts should be explicit about source level, provenance, confidence,
dependencies, contradiction, and inference. Publication prose should normally move:

FACT → QUESTION → IMPLICATION → NEXT RECEIPT.

State the conclusion the cumulative chain supports. Put a material boundary once, at the
point where it changes meaning, then continue. Do not translate every internal label into
lawyerly prose or narrate the audit to the reader.
