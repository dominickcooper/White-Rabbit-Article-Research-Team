# Zebra Protocol

Zebra Protocol adjudicates a developed case. It never lowers the ambition of the initial
research question and must not run before the strongest case, cumulative connection
chains, and disconfirming evidence have been assembled.

## Categories

### HORSE — ordinary or emergent explanation

Actors pursue public missions or independent interests. Overlap is substantially
incidental, routine, competitive, incoherent, or better explained by ordinary
coordination than deliberate assembly.

### ZEBRA — deliberately networked statecraft

Different overt and/or covert institutions are intentionally assigned or used for
complementary roles around a broader strategic objective. Zebra does not require one
commander, a universal organization chart, a single target list, every participant's
knowledge of the whole, or a master memorandum.

### BLACK ZEBRA — concealed or deniable compartment

A Zebra arrangement includes covered personnel, secret tasking, cutouts, clandestine
funding, concealed sponsorship, false attribution, proprietaries, shell entities, or
another materially hidden compartment. This analytical label is not automatically a
legal conclusion about covert action.

### HYBRID — ordinary activity plus deliberate networked statecraft

Substantial Horse activity and substantial Zebra activity coexist. Hybrid is not a
consolation category for a case lacking centralized command.

### UNRESOLVED

The available record cannot distinguish ordinary convergence from deliberate assembly.

## Adjudication test

For each proposition or bounded event, ask:

1. What would the ordinary/emergent account predict?
2. What would deliberate role differentiation predict?
3. Which predicted signatures are present and which are absent?
4. Are the evidence streams independent or derivative?
5. What evidence contradicts each account?
6. Is there a concealed compartment, and what specifically establishes it?
7. Which category best explains the complete record, at what confidence?

Judge bounded propositions, events, programs, or periods before generalizing to an entire
institution or ecosystem. Horse can win. A controversial theory can be contradicted,
remain unresolved, or survive as a corroborated strong inference. Record what evidence
would change the judgment.

## Workflow position

The permanent order is:

EXTREME THESIS → EVIDENCE BUILD → CONNECTION CHAINS → DISCONFIRMATION → ZEBRA
ADJUDICATION → THESIS REDUCTION → STORY DECISION

Historical precedent informs predicted signatures but never substitutes for evidence of
the current case. Absence of a smoking-gun memo is one observation, not an automatic Horse
finding. Equally, proximity, thematic similarity, or one weak edge is not an automatic
Zebra finding.
