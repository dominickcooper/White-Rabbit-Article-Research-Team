# White Rabbit style

Write a long-form investigative newsletter for Substack, normally 2,000–3,500 words;
go longer when evidence earns it. Be conversational, intelligent, skeptical and
curiosity-driven. Use active voice, useful rhetorical questions and strong transitions.
Most paragraphs have 3–5 sentences; reserve one-line paragraphs for rhetorical impact.
Bold important names, agencies, companies, claims, numbers and discoveries. Use italics
for conversational emphasis and blockquotes only for genuine, accurately sourced quotes.

Open with a strange document, contradiction, unexplained event, quotation, mystery or
provocative question. Never generic SEO filler. Let documents and competing explanations
shape the narrative; do not turn a predetermined suspicion into the conclusion.

The author is a visible investigator when first person clarifies the path of discovery,
why a connection caught attention or where interpretation begins. Prefer concrete actors
and verbs to abstract policy language. Vary paragraph and sentence rhythm; do not make
the article orderly enough to erase discovery. Read the author-voice and anti-AI
authorities for the required post-draft passes.

Use FACT → QUESTION → JUDGMENT → IMPLICATION organically: what is documented, what it
makes us ask, what the evidence most likely means, and why it matters. Do not label
every paragraph or force this sequence into every section. Preserve supported causal
chains and enough context to understand the judgment. End with an earned implication.

Include useful visual notes as `[IMAGE: description | ALT: alt text]`, and place
`[[SUBSCRIBE]]` and `[[SHARE]]` strategically. Markers are editorial placement notes,
not actual uploaded images or functional Substack buttons.
Maintain full purpose, source, exact placement, caption, alt text and evidence-status
metadata in the dossier. Images and emphasis are argumentative and pacing choices,
not decoration.

Include exactly five useful FAQs as ### questions under ## FAQ. Include the exact
heading `## YOU MAY BE INTERESTED IN THESE ARTICLES` with relevant verified archive
links. When none are relevant/available, explain the limitation in audit.md rather
than inventing links. The number of FAQs may change only through an intentional
update to both permanent standards and the configuration.
