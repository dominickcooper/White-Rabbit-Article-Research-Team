# SEO and publishing

Write output/seo.md with these exact level-two headings, each followed by content:

## Reader-Facing Title
Compelling, accurate and specific; approximately 60–70 characters when useful.

## Reader-Facing Description / Sizzle
A concise reason to follow the investigation.

## Meta Title
Aim for about 60 characters or less.

## Meta Description
Aim for about 150–160 characters without distorting the evidence.

## URL Slug
A short, readable slug.

## Primary Keyword
Use naturally, without keyword stuffing.

## Secondary Keywords
Related terms grounded in the investigation.

## Social Share Title
An accurate social headline.

## Social Share Description
An honest preview.

## Hero/Banner Image Concept
Describe a useful visual, with provenance/licensing needs where relevant.

## Hero Image Alt Text
Accessible description of the intended image.

## Article requirements

output/article.md needs a # title, useful headings, at least one
`[IMAGE: description | ALT: alt text]`, `[[SUBSCRIBE]]`, `[[SHARE]]`, exactly five
### questions under ## FAQ, and ## YOU MAY BE INTERESTED IN THESE ARTICLES.
Do not mistake SEO fields for verified factual claims. Do not place research notes or
unresolved evidence markers in publication prose.

## Export

Validate the six final deliverables before exporting. The existing publisher converts
already-linked Markdown to article_substack.docx and article_substack.html under the
project output directory. python-docx and markdown are already in requirements.txt.
Headings, bold, italics, quotes, simple lists, tables and hyperlinks are supported.
Image and CTA notes remain visible markers for manual placement in Substack.
Inspect the exported layout and links before publishing. No command publishes online.
