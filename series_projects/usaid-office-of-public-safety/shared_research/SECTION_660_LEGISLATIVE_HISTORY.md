## Original exceptions at enactment

1. Assistance under Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act §515(c).
2. DEA or FBI authority concerning crimes of a nature unlawful under U.S. law.
3. Foreign Assistance Act §482 narcotics-control assistance.
4. Pre-enactment personnel contracts, but not renewals or extensions entered after enactment.

## Later exceptions

Later democratic-country, judicial/prosecutorial, human-rights, antiterrorism and other exceptions must be dated and analyzed in Parts 6–7. They were not part of the December 1974 text.
