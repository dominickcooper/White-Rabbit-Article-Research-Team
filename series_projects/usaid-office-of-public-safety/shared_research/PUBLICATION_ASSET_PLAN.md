# Publication Asset Plan

## Production rules and asset count

This plan specifies **51 assets**: seven article heroes, 43 inline visuals, and one master-series graphic. The inline set contains seven archival photographs, fourteen document exhibits, and twenty-two custom maps/diagrams/timelines. Every relationship graphic must be checked against `SERIES_CANONICAL_FACTS.md`; generated artwork is atmosphere, never evidence.

Use a shared palette of midnight navy, charcoal, aged ivory, muted federal green and one restrained red accent. Add a small file-folder tab in the upper-left safe zone reading `OPS FILES / 01` through `07`, set at roughly 4% of canvas width. Use the same tab proportions and condensed sans-serif face throughout. A faint map grid or redaction strip may recur at under 15% opacity. Keep all headlines out of artwork. Heroes must preserve the central 60% for square crops and the middle 80% vertically for shallow banners.

Rights labels used below: **USG** = presumptive U.S. government work, verify the item page for exceptions; **PD** = public domain candidate; **WC** = Wikimedia Commons, verify the named license; **FU** = fair-use editorial excerpt candidate; **VR** = verify rights before use; **GEN** = newly generated White Rabbit Report illustration.
