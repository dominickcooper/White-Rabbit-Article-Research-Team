# Final series verdicts

## IPA / IPS canonical classification

The International Police Academy was an overt OPS/AID academy with a genuine police-training mission and documented CIA operational integration and access. International Police Services was the separate CIA proprietary used for selected follow-on training and assessment. The supported analytical shorthand is that IPA was not the proprietary; it was the access point—a CIA backdoor into an overt foreign-police program.

| Claim | Verdict | Basis |

| USAID was/is the CIA | Rejected | Documented interfaces, proprietary and compartmented cooperation do not establish institutional identity |
