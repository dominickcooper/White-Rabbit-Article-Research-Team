## Part 6 resolution

- Criminal investigation was legally reauthorized through §534(b)(3) and ICITAP in 1986.
- Counterterrorism was legally reauthorized through ATA beginning in 1983.
- FBI remained a parallel training channel; ILEA began in 1995 with functional similarity to IPA but no documented descent.
- GAO documented a fragmented State/Justice/Defense architecture reaching 125 countries in fiscal 1990.
