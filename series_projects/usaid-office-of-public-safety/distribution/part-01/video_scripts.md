The committee reported that FY1958 AID police programs in 21 countries cost about $14 million. The committee reported that FY1958 Defense police programs cost $5.8 million in five countries. The committee recommended substantially increasing the global police-assistance program. The committee proposed protecting police funds from competition with economic-development projects. One proposed funding option was an AID internal-defense budget line for FY1964. The sequence matters more than a slogan. A policy problem became an interagency design, then an operating assignment. The documents support a national-security architecture. They do not support collapsing every participating agency into one institution. Read Part 1 and examine the records yourself. Read the sequence slowly: the funding proposal, the agency boundaries, the training plan and the deadline are pieces of one administrative design. The tension is visible because the conventional development rationale and the stated internal-defense rationale occupy the same file. That is why the exact wording matters.
