### FINAL RESERVE COPY

The July 1962 police-assistance memo proposed AID leadership, with exceptions. How would you trace those exceptions?

In February, Kennedy told AID that police assistance could look marginal under economic-growth criteria but had a different justification: internal security and resistance to Communist-supported insurgency.

In June, NSAM 162 called for country plans combining military, police, intelligence and psychological measures. In July, the police-assistance committee recommended AID coordination under State policy guidance.

Recommendation 4 proposed giving AID operating and funding responsibility, except for covert aspects and programs selected for Defense. That is a specific administrative distinction, not evidence that every police program was covert.

My reading is that the recommendation establishes a boundary but does not identify all the activities beyond it. The next useful evidence would be records assigning or reporting the excluded work, rather than another broad claim that AID and CIA were identical.
