## Source-sensitive editorial decisions

1. July recommendations are proposals. Do not call the July committee's recommendations a presidential order or claim implementation. The original claim 016 uses “assigned”; rewrites deliberately say “proposed.”
2. The police office was to provide a repository of technical knowledge based on research. The flagship preserves this function instead of asserting that the office conducted all the research itself.
3. NSAM 162 requires available qualified personnel with paramilitary skills. It does not prove particular deployments, completed training, or a CIA command chain over AID.
4. The FY1958 figures are reported retrospectively under the memo's label “AID programs.” Rewrites attribute that label; they do not claim the later agency existed in FY1958. Use approximate $14m and exact reported $5.8m; no modern-dollar conversion or summed country coverage.
5. Recommendation 8 offers a proposed FY1964 line item and an alternative military-assistance funding route. Neither proves that the proposed line item was enacted.
6. The roster contains the Bureau of the Budget as well as the six bodies in the brief. This is supported by the existing 024 excerpt (“BOB Member”) and the spelled-out Bureau of the Budget reference in recommendation 8.
7. Preserve both recommendation 4 exceptions. The carve-out does not establish that all AID programs were covert or identify every excluded activity.
8. The bridge imports no withheld Part 2 outcome, identity, training mechanism or recruitment finding. No later-part visual should be attached.
