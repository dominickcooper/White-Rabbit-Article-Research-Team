## NEW FINDINGS

### 1. Autonomy and strategic utility can coexist

Wilford and Saunders establish a historically important mechanism: CIA-funded associations could contain unwitting members, genuine voluntary energy, internal conflict, and meaningful autonomy while still advancing a sponsor's strategic purpose. This is a high-diagnostic precedent, not automatic proof about modern grantees.

### 2. Syria is the strongest modern stress test

State records document opposition councils, the Assistance Coordination Unit, independent media, civil defense, interim justice, public safety, and Free Syrian Police. GAO documents combined State-USAID teams, military support, colocation, and civil-military working groups. A congressional exhibit and acknowledged public record document the separate CIA/partner-state armed program. Strategic complementarity is a CORROBORATED STRONG INFERENCE; USAID command of Timber Sycamore is CONTRADICTED/UNSUPPORTED.

### 3. Ukraine supports environmental influence, not operational direction

USAID's pre-2014 strategy explicitly favored democratic and European alignment; the wider ecosystem built civic, media, election, party, and governance capacity. State openly engaged government and opposition and discussed preferred political configurations. Material shaping of the environment is a CORROBORATED STRONG INFERENCE. Operational direction of Maidan remains UNSUPPORTED.

### 4. Venezuela made its pressure architecture public

Recognition, sanctions, opposition support, transition institutions, military terms, elections, sanction relief, and IFI support were explicitly linked. The core is DIRECTLY DOCUMENTED Zebra statecraft; outcome control and concealed CIA direction are not established.

### 5. Haiti separates rupture causation from vacuum use

The Core Group publicly favored Ariel Henry during a contested succession; Henry then took office. External succession influence is a CORROBORATED STRONG INFERENCE. U.S./USAID responsibility for Moïse's assassination is UNSUPPORTED.

### 6. Bangladesh became denser but stayed unresolved

The CEPPS ecosystem and U.S. pressure are documented; Yunus has long, unusually warm U.S. relationships; immediate post-transition aid followed. These are a HIGH-DIAGNOSTIC PATTERN. The disputed “destabilize” language failed authentication, and no protest-direction or successor-selection bridge was recovered.

### 7. OCCRP resolves a false binary

Funders deliberately purchased investigative capacity and tracked downstream official/civic action. OCCRP's disclosed grant clause preserves editorial control. Strategic utility is DIRECTLY DOCUMENTED at the capability level. Individual-story tasking is UNSUPPORTED.

### 8. Domestic-bleedback claims did not close

Tides foreign grants, NewsGuard's DOD research award, and cross-border information organizations are real. Direct USAID pass-through to BLM-linked entities/FJP, USAID direction of PolitiFact, and government direction of NewsGuard's later domestic ratings were not recovered. Direct claims are CONTRADICTED/UNSUPPORTED; indirect capacity subsidy remains PLAUSIBLE pending accounting evidence.

## RESPONSIBILITY ASSESSMENT

- **Institutional responsibility:** high where agencies publish objectives, choose implementers, assign roles, and monitor desired effects.
- **Operational responsibility:** only where an agency or partner directs implementation; not transferable across adjacent programs.
- **Causal responsibility for ruptures:** varies sharply. Strong for explicit transition pressure in Venezuela; inferential for environment shaping in Ukraine; absent for Sri Lanka and the Myanmar coup; unresolved for Bangladesh.
- **Recipient responsibility:** local actors retain agency. Their autonomy is analytically compatible with sponsor utility and blocks claims of simple puppetry.
- **Intelligence responsibility:** direct only in documented compartments such as IPS/TWO-FOLD/Timber Sycamore; not imputed to all aid or media programs.

## CONTRARY EVIDENCE

- Global aid presence creates a high overlap base rate.
- Bureaucratic rivalry, weak monitoring, local initiative, and program failure are common.
- Sri Lanka's crisis is strongly explained by domestic policy error and external shocks.
- Myanmar's coup defeated U.S.-supported civilian rule.
- Afghanistan's integrated U.S. system collapsed contrary to its purpose.
- OCCRP's editorial-control clause and recipient denials are substantive limits on control claims.
- Many modern programs have genuine humanitarian or public purposes even when they also occupy strategic terrain.

## COMPETING EXPLANATIONS

1. **Routine division of government labor:** explains much interagency co-presence; insufficient where documents assign common end states or conceal attribution.
2. **Recipient opportunism:** local actors seek resources for their own aims; often true and compatible with sponsor strategy.
3. **Developmental purpose:** many programs genuinely deliver services or rights; dual purpose must be demonstrated, not assumed.
4. **Crisis adaptation:** agencies redirect after rupture rather than causing it; decisive in Myanmar and important elsewhere.
5. **Strategic network:** wins only where role allocation, target, sequence, or downstream-effect evidence joins the nodes.
