# Thesis Reduction — Part 7

## Original maximum thesis

Politically sensitive U.S. foreign operations can be distributed among USAID, State, DOD, DOJ, intelligence, law enforcement, democracy institutes, OTI, contractors, NGOs, media, anti-corruption networks, civil society, and foreign partners; in some countries the tools may be complementary parts of regime pressure, realignment, stabilization, or transition.

## Directly proven

- The United States deliberately assigns different foreign-policy functions to different agencies and implementers.
- USAID/State democracy and transition programs intentionally build political, civic, media, governance, and information capacity.
- Contractors and grantees provide implementation, scale, specialized technology, and distance.
- Syria's overt opposition/stabilization effort was coordinated across State, USAID, and DOD, while a separate CIA/partner-state arming program existed.
- Venezuela's recognition, sanctions, transition design, aid, security terms, election plan, and international-finance offer were complementary instruments aimed at political transition.
- Haiti's Core Group, including the United States, materially influenced which claimant governed after Moïse's assassination.
- OCCRP received state support to build investigative capacity whose performance included downstream official/civic action, while preserving editorial control.
- Section 660 ended OPS but police, security, information, and influence functions migrated into a larger distributed architecture.

## Proven by corroborated strong inference

- In Syria, the overt and covert tracks were strategically complementary at the level of pressure on Assad, even though no public joint-tasking memo links USAID to Timber Sycamore.
- Before Maidan, U.S. programs materially shaped Ukraine's pro-democratic and pro-European political environment; they did not merely occupy the same geography.
- A government can deliberately fund an independent information network for statecraft-relevant consequences without assigning each investigation.
- Repeated cultivation of local institutions and elites creates durable access and influence that may matter during a rupture.

## High-diagnostic but incomplete

- Bangladesh's long democracy network, pre-election pressure, longstanding U.S. support for Yunus, and immediate post-transition partnership form a consequential pattern; the uprising-to-successor operational bridge is missing.
- Ukraine's diplomatic preferences during Maidan show attempted influence over the transition's composition; direction of the uprising remains unproved.
- Domestic foreign-policy awards can create fungible organizational capacity, but the traced examples do not prove named domestic advocacy was subsidized by restricted foreign grants.

## Plausible

- Outside powers sometimes exploit a political vacuum they did not create.
- Contractor and NGO layers can make political responsibility harder to see even without a covert relationship.
- Personnel, tools, or overhead developed on foreign contracts may later matter domestically; this requires organization-level accounting.

## Weakened

- A single orchestrator is necessary for networked statecraft. The record more often shows compatible missions, negotiated roles, rivalry, and uneven control.
- Formal independence makes a grantee strategically irrelevant. Historical and modern records contradict that binary, but independence still constrains claims of control.

## Contradicted

- USAID is simply the CIA under another name.
- USAID ran Timber Sycamore.
- The United States operationally directed Maidan on the reviewed record.
- USAID caused Moïse's assassination.
- USAID engineered Sri Lanka's 2022 uprising.
- The U.S. caused Myanmar's coup or intended Afghanistan's collapse.
- USAID money was traced through Tides to BLM-linked entities or Fair and Just Prosecution.
- State/USAID assigned OCCRP's individual stories.

## What became stronger than expected

The Syria record contains an unusually explicit overt architecture: opposition councils, independent media, police/public safety, combined State-USAID teams, DOD support, sanctions, and a parallel covert armed track. The Venezuela record is even more explicit about coordinated transition pressure because the design was published.

## What collapsed

The universal proxy theory collapsed, as did event-causation claims for Sri Lanka, the Myanmar coup, Afghanistan's fall, Haiti's assassination, and the alleged direct domestic grant pass-throughs.

## Final surviving thesis

What survived OPS was not a single office, secret chain of command, or seamless CIA succession. It was a reproducible governing method: Washington can separate money, legitimacy, diplomacy, security, intelligence, implementation, and information across public and private institutions, then combine selected functions around a strategic objective. Some systems are openly coordinated; some contain a concealed compartment; many programs remain ordinary aid. The method is proven in bounded cases, the Syria convergence makes overt/covert complementarity a strong inference, and the control cases show why overlap alone is never enough.
