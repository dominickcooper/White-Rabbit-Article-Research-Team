# Story Decision

## Research threshold

The research is substantially complete for publication. All eight flagship cases have independent primary/oversight streams; fifteen secondary cases have been screened; seventeen non-rupture comparisons are included; stateside claims were tested at award/recipient level; OCCRP was tested against both grant design and editorial-independence evidence. Remaining gaps are recorded in the rabbit-hole queue and are not load-bearing to the decision.

## Governing story

The old Part 7 asked what survived *inside USAID*. The stronger story is what the global map reveals when “USAID presence” is treated as the beginning rather than the conclusion of an investigation.

The answer is a selective architecture:

> Modern U.S. foreign assistance is a large ordinary aid ecosystem containing a smaller set of deliberately networked statecraft programs. In those programs, USAID commonly supplies funding, access and implementation; State supplies policy and diplomacy; security and intelligence agencies supply other capabilities; contractors, NGOs and media networks extend reach. A few theaters add a concealed compartment. The architecture is real, but it does not make every protest a U.S. operation or every grantee a cutout.

## Why this is the story

- It survives the control cases. Sri Lanka, Tunisia, Ethiopia and the Myanmar coup demonstrate that presence plus rupture is not causation.
- It explains the strongest positive cases without demanding a nonexistent master chart. Syria, Venezuela, Georgia and post-coup Myanmar show explicit complementary roles.
- It handles covert evidence precisely. Timber Sycamore and ZunZuneo are bounded Black Zebra compartments, not licenses to blacken the whole ecosystem.
- It makes OCCRP intelligible: independent information production can be an intentionally funded instrument of statecraft without government ownership of editorial decisions.
- It yields a falsifiable domestic conclusion: Tides' foreign grants do not establish USAID funding of BLM or FJP.
- It completes the series. The durable continuity is doctrinal and functional—role differentiation and migration—not an unbroken OPS organization.

## Benz assessment

Mike Benz's broad proposition is **strengthened in architecture and weakened in universality and event causation**. He is substantially right that funding, policy, operations, intermediaries and information can be deliberately divided; wrong or unproven when that architecture is treated as sufficient proof that a specific protest, leadership change or domestic organization was controlled by USAID/CIA.

## Structure decision

1. Open on the pin-map trap and the amended Zebra test.
2. Establish the base rate: more than 100 countries and thousands of partners.
3. Use Syria to prove the complete overt-plus-covert architecture.
4. Contrast Ukraine and Venezuela: influence architecture versus event causation; explicit transition toolkit.
5. Use Haiti, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Myanmar and Georgia as the category-changing test sequence.
6. Follow the information: OCCRP, editorial independence and intelligence benefit without ownership.
7. Test domestic bleedback: Tides/BLM/FJP, NewsGuard, Poynter and CIR.
8. End on 2025 administrative migration from USAID to State and the series-level implication.

## Claims excluded or narrowed

- Exclude the unauthenticated Bangladesh “destabilize” quotation.
- Do not say USAID ran Timber Sycamore.
- Do not say USAID caused Moïse's assassination, Maidan, Sri Lanka's collapse, Myanmar's coup or Afghanistan's fall.
- Do not describe grant recipients as intelligence assets without tasking/ownership evidence.
- Do not treat Tides-wide infrastructure as a direct BLM/FJP subsidy.
- Do not use historical OPS precedent as proof of any modern case.

## Publication decision

Proceed with a full ground-up rewrite. The article adds enough new value to justify the finale: a global comparative screen, an explicit causal protocol, a bounded finding of modern deliberately networked statecraft, genuine controls, domestic negative findings and a documented 2025 institutional handoff.
