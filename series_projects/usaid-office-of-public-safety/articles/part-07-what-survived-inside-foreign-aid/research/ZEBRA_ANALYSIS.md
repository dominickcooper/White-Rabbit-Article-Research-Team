# Part 7 Amended Zebra Protocol Analysis

## Executive adjudication

The modern foreign-assistance ecosystem is **HYBRID**. Most USAID work is ordinary development, humanitarian relief, institutional support or routine diplomatic coordination. A smaller but consequential set of programs is **ZEBRA**: U.S. policy documents deliberately assign funding, diplomacy, governance, media, sanctions, security and operational responsibilities to different nodes around a common strategic objective. A still smaller set contains **BLACK ZEBRA** compartments: concealed sponsorship or covert operational activity inside the larger network.

This conclusion is not a compromise category. The map produces clean Horse cases, clean Zebra cases, bounded Black Zebra cases and unresolved cases. What it rejects is the claim that the entire aid ecosystem is centrally orchestrated—or that absence of a single commander makes deliberate networked statecraft impossible.

## Protocol

- **HORSE:** agencies pursue public missions; overlap is incidental, emergent or routine coordination.
- **ZEBRA:** role differentiation is deliberately used to produce a broader strategic effect. Central command, CIA ownership and universal participant knowledge are not required.
- **BLACK ZEBRA:** a deliberately concealed, deniable or clandestine compartment exists inside the broader network.
- **HYBRID:** substantial ordinary/emergent activity and substantial deliberate networked statecraft coexist.
- **UNRESOLVED:** predicted evidence is insufficient or materially conflicting.

Every proposition was tested through four questions: Is the overlap incidental? Is it ordinary coordination? Were roles intentionally divided around a common strategic objective? Is there a covert or deniable compartment?

## The base-rate correction

USAID-funded organizations operated in more than 100 countries. The inspector general reported more than 4,000 partner organizations across more than 100 countries, while USAID described more than 80 missions. A world map of aid pins will therefore overlap many protests, coups, wars and elections by chance. The 40-country screen contains 23 selected rupture cases and 17 comparison countries; it is purposeful, not a random sample, and cannot estimate an overthrow rate.

The correct evidentiary ladder is presence → relevant program → timing → common strategic target → explicit or strongly corroborated role allocation → event-level operational bridge → covert compartment. Most internet claims stop at the first or second rung.

## Flagship cases

### Syria — Hybrid with a Black Zebra compartment

**Horse prediction:** aid, diplomacy and military programs would pursue related but independent mandates, with routine embassy coordination and no common political target.

**Zebra prediction:** public documents would assign governance, political, stabilization and armed-support roles to separate institutions around pressure on Assad and construction of a viable opposition.

**Black Zebra prediction:** independently documented concealed arming, vetting, training, funding or cutouts would sit beside the overt programs.

The Zebra signatures are present. State described hundreds of millions of dollars for opposition local councils, activists, independent media and nonlethal support. Budget and policy documents said the work should increase opposition credibility and cohesion. The 2018 Stabilization Assistance Review explicitly divided responsibilities among State, USAID and DOD and described stabilization as political. Separate public records describe assistance to vetted armed opposition.

The Black Zebra signature is also present but bounded. The Washington Post reported the CIA's covert Timber Sycamore program; a New York Times account was reproduced in a congressional hearing exhibit, describing a White House-authorized arrangement in which CIA trained rebels while Saudi intelligence supplied money and weapons. That does not make USAID the covert program's owner. It establishes an additional concealed compartment aimed at an overlapping strategic theater.

**Adjudication:** HYBRID WITH BLACK ZEBRA. The conventional explanation—separate responses to civil war, ISIS, humanitarian catastrophe and stabilization—remains real. The record nevertheless shows deliberate role division, not merely emergent convergence.

### Ukraine — Hybrid, with a later reported intelligence compartment

The 2012–2016 USAID strategy explicitly proposed using civil society and targeted diplomatic engagement to maintain pressure on the Ukrainian government, encourage European norms and expand political competition. State publicly described frequent engagement with government and civil society and large cumulative democracy investments. That is Zebra at the strategy level: assistance and diplomacy were complementary by design.

The decisive missing signature is an event-level operational bridge to Maidan. Documents show a pro-European influence architecture, not a U.S. command chain for protesters, police violence, elite defections or Yanukovych's flight. Later security assistance and the reported CIA–Ukraine partnership cannot be back-projected into proof that CIA or USAID ran the 2013–2014 uprising.

After Russia's 2022 invasion, the role division becomes overt and high-confidence: USAID and State supported governance and resilience; DOD trained and armed forces; diplomacy and sanctions targeted Russia; intelligence cooperation supported defense. That is deliberately networked statecraft responding to an invasion, not evidence that Washington created the invasion.

**Adjudication:** HYBRID. Zebra is established for strategic influence and post-invasion support. Maidan operational control is not.

### Venezuela — Hybrid

Public documents call the approach “whole of government.” The United States recognized Juan Guaidó, imposed sanctions, funded democratic actors, coordinated humanitarian aid and published a transition framework integrating governance, elections, security and economic relief. A USAID inspector-general report found that NSC and State directives to USAID were sometimes undocumented and that USAID coordinated assistance with the interim government under a $200 million bilateral agreement.

This is strong Zebra evidence because the role division and common transition objective are explicit. It is Hybrid because humanitarian relief had a genuine purpose, Maduro retained power, and GAO attributed Venezuela's economic decline to a combination of government mismanagement, oil prices and sanctions. No credible Black Zebra compartment was established in the reviewed record.

### Haiti — Hybrid, with a hard negative finding

After President Jovenel Moïse's assassination, the Core Group publicly encouraged Ariel Henry to form a government. State briefings show officials had engaged both Henry and acting prime minister Claude Joseph and then defended the resulting succession against reporters' charge that outside powers had chosen a side. Long-running USAID governance and election programs and State law-enforcement assistance formed the surrounding institutional ecosystem.

The leadership-transition connection is therefore real: foreign diplomatic recognition helped determine which claimant became internationally legitimate. But the most important negative finding is equally clear: no reviewed evidence shows USAID planned, funded, knew of or participated in the assassination. External leverage after a rupture is not causation of the rupture.

### Sri Lanka — Horse and the strongest dissolved coincidence

USAID's strategy aligned governance work with a Free and Open Indo-Pacific. Its portfolio included multimillion-dollar awards to NDI, IRI, IFES, MSI, IREX and Chemonics, and GAO counted at least $115.2 million in democracy assistance in fiscal years 2018–2023. President Gotabaya Rajapaksa resigned after mass protest.

Yet the predicted Zebra signatures aimed at removal are missing: no event-specific role allocation, synchronized pressure plan, protest tasking, covert sponsorship or leadership-transition mechanism appeared. State's human-rights report describes an economic collapse marked by debt, depleted reserves, shortages and public anger. This is the case that looks suspicious on a pin map and dissolves under causal reconstruction.

**Adjudication:** HORSE.

### Bangladesh — Unresolved

USAID's country strategy explicitly sought political pluralism and work with parties, citizens, civil-society groups and youth. A prior election-support evaluation maps the CEPPS implementers—IFES, IRI and NDI—across polling, parties, media, election administration and civil society. State also applied election-related visa pressure before the 2024 upheaval.

Those facts establish a deliberate democracy-influence network. They do not establish that the network organized the student quota protests, directed the escalation, or caused Sheikh Hasina's departure. The disputed line attributed to a USAID document about “destabilizing” Bangladesh was excluded because it could not be authenticated in an underlying primary source.

**Adjudication:** UNRESOLVED at the event-causation level; ZEBRA at the long-run democracy strategy level.

### Myanmar — Hybrid

The February 2021 coup is a Horse case: Myanmar's military overturned an election and removed a civilian government that U.S. programs supported. State then created an interagency process, redirected assistance away from government beneficiaries, continued direct programs, imposed sanctions and supported media, civil society and the National Unity Government's capacity.

The event and response therefore classify differently. The coup was not an intended U.S. strategic effect. The response was deliberately assembled statecraft.

**Adjudication:** HYBRID.

### Georgia — Zebra

USAID's inspector general found that Georgia's country strategy integrated the Countering Malign Kremlin Influence framework. GAO reported more than $224 million in democracy assistance in fiscal years 2018–2023. During the 2024 crisis, State reviewed and paused assistance benefiting the government, imposed visa restrictions and announced sanctions while assistance continued to support civil society and media.

The signatures are explicit strategy, common adversary definition, differentiated assistance and diplomatic/economic pressure, and synchronized adaptation after government action. A covert compartment is not necessary and none was found.

**Adjudication:** ZEBRA.

## Media, anti-corruption and the information question

OCCRP is the strongest modern example of intelligence benefit without formal ownership—but “intelligence” must be used precisely. A State solicitation for the Global Anti-Corruption Consortium sought investigative reporting that produced legally actionable information and downstream action by governments, international organizations, prosecutors and asset-recovery bodies. A USAID evaluation of an ICFJ award and its OCCRP subgrant linked investigative networks to resignations, indictments and governance consequences. State separately credited a USAID-supported network and an OCCRP partner with securing and publishing documents from Ukraine's departing Yanukovych government.

That is deliberately networked statecraft at the architecture and outcome level. Government funds independent investigators because their information can activate public, legal, sanctions and asset-recovery systems. But the State solicitation expressly denied government editorial influence, and OCCRP says donors do not choose or preview stories. Those are not boilerplate details to discard; they are negative evidence against the Black Zebra hypothesis of a secretly tasked newsroom.

**Adjudication:** ZEBRA, NOT BLACK ZEBRA. Independent journalism can be deliberately funded as part of an accountability toolkit without becoming a government-owned publication.

## Stateside bleedback

### Tides, Black Lives Matter and Fair and Just Prosecution

Tides says it received $24.5 million in USAID grants from October 2016 through July 2024, plus an earlier $1.5 million Open Government Partnership grant, and made $680,807 in related subawards. Tides separately fiscally sponsored Black Lives Matter and Fair and Just Prosecution. Shared corporate infrastructure is therefore real.

The direct-funding claim failed. Tides states that no USAID funds went to BLM, FJP, Greenpeace or domestic programs. FJP says it receives no USAID or public-agency funding. Award-level evidence tracing restricted USAID dollars into either domestic project was not found. The most that can be said is that government grants paid fees and supported the same fiscal sponsor's general operating apparatus; the record does not show how much, if any, benefit reached those projects.

**Adjudication:** HORSE with indirect organizational overlap. No direct USAID→BLM/FJP funding path established.

### NewsGuard

USAspending records a $749,387 Air Force contract for “Misinformation Fingerprints,” and a federal court decision records a September 2021–December 2022 performance period and no later contracts. The technology had obvious dual-use potential in a commercial information-rating ecosystem. Missing are government tasking of domestic political ratings, continuing contract control or a traced USAID channel.

**Adjudication:** UNRESOLVED for bleedback; the federal R&D contract itself is documented.

### Poynter/PolitiFact and CIR

No authenticated direct USAID award to PolitiFact was found. USAID materials sometimes cite Poynter resources, while Poynter and PolitiFact publish their funders. Organizational overlap between global and domestic fact-checking exists; a USAID-funded domestic tasking chain does not.

The Centre for Information Resilience discloses U.S. State Department funding for foreign investigations and claims editorial independence. No USAID award or domestic political operation was traced in the reviewed record. Its foreign projects fit Zebra at the program level—public money intentionally develops information capacity around strategic crises—but not Black Zebra.

## Evidence independence

The strongest genuinely independent convergence occurs when policy design, funding/award documentation, oversight findings and recipient disclosures align without copying one another. Syria has public policy records plus OIG material plus independent reporting on the concealed compartment. OCCRP has a State solicitation, USAID evaluation and recipient editorial policy. Venezuela has State strategy, USAID OIG findings and GAO economic analysis.

Streams are not independent merely because they appear on different websites. State press releases and USAID strategy papers can reflect one executive-branch policy process. NDI, IRI and IFES under CEPPS are separate implementers but often share one cooperative agreement and are not three independent proofs of causation. News stories that all cite the same anonymous official count as one stream. Recipient claims of editorial independence are relevant negative evidence, not dispositive self-certification.

## Personnel, contractors and handoffs

The modern pattern is stronger in organizational role continuity than in individual careers. Recurring intermediaries include Chemonics, MSI, IREX, NDI, IRI, IFES, ICFJ/OCCRP and Tides. Their recurrence shows a reusable implementation market with country access, compliance systems and specialized expertise. It does not by itself show a secret cadre or common command.

The cleanest handoffs are functional and legal: government-directed funding moving from host-state institutions to civil society after democratic erosion; remaining foreign-assistance awards moving from USAID administration to State after July 1, 2025; and earlier series examples in which police or security functions moved across statutory and institutional boundaries. Personnel-level continuity remains comparatively weak and is a major limit on the broadest theory.

## Benz propositions: final verdicts

1. **USAID as an interface among State, CIA and DOD:** strengthened in bounded country programs; overstated as a universal institutional identity.
2. **Foreign assistance as attribution/oversight mechanism:** plausible and sometimes documented through intermediary and reporting designs; not a general explanation for aid.
3. **Deliberate division of funding, policy and operations:** strongly supported in Syria, Venezuela, stabilization policy, CMKI and post-coup responses.
4. **Political influence through civil society:** strongly supported at strategy level; event causation remains case-specific.
5. **Contractors/NGOs as intermediary layers:** strongly supported; deniability cannot be inferred from outsourcing alone.
6. **Civilian programs generate useful information:** strongly supported, especially in anti-corruption journalism.
7. **Intelligence benefit without formal ownership:** supported as open-source/actionable information; secret intelligence tasking usually not shown.
8. **Post-restriction functional migration:** strongly supported historically and again by the 2025 USAID-to-State transfer, but migration can be lawful and overt.
9. **ZunZuneo:** Black Zebra on concealed attribution, not proof of CIA ownership.
10. **ICITAP and CIA/DEA coordination:** Zebra with bounded covert features from Parts 5–6; separate legal authorities matter.
11. **Afghanistan:** Hybrid; deliberate integrated wartime architecture plus vast ordinary aid and an unintended collapse.
12. **Media/anti-corruption networks:** Zebra when program design seeks downstream official action; not Black Zebra without editorial tasking.
13. **Domestic bleedback:** weakened. Shared intermediaries and dual-use tools exist, but direct funding/tasking claims failed in the tested cases.
14. **Historical precedent:** useful for predictions, never a substitute for modern evidence.

## Overall fit

The best-fitting explanation is not “USAID caused regime change,” and it is not “USAID was just doing aid.” It is a selective architecture of deliberately networked statecraft embedded inside a much larger ordinary aid system. USAID most often supplied money, access, implementation and public legitimacy; State supplied policy and diplomacy; DOD and law-enforcement agencies supplied security capabilities; sanctions and intelligence channels sometimes operated beside them; contractors, NGOs and media networks extended reach. Only a few cases add a documented concealed compartment.

The amended protocol changes the answer because it recognizes deliberate distribution without demanding a master command chart. It also prevents that insight from swallowing every coincidence. The global ecosystem is Hybrid. Syria contains Black Zebra. Georgia and the OCCRP architecture are overt Zebra. Sri Lanka is Horse. Bangladesh and several domestic-bleedback claims remain unresolved. That differentiated result best fits the total pattern.
