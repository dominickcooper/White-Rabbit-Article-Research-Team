# Zebra Analysis — Part 7 Ground-Up Revision

## Decision

**Overall: HYBRID.** The universal “USAID is a CIA proxy” thesis fails. A stronger bounded proposition survives: U.S. strategy repeatedly distributes complementary functions across civilian, diplomatic, military, law-enforcement, intelligence, contractor, NGO, and media nodes. The arrangement is a Zebra when role allocation toward a shared objective is explicit; a Black Zebra only when a concealed compartment is independently established.

## Base-rate correction

USAID and State operate globally, so co-presence around crises is expected. Geographic overlap is weak. High-diagnostic evidence includes explicit role assignment, named interagency teams, conditional pressure architecture, concealed sponsorship, common recipients, close sequencing, or records tying program outputs to official action.

## Case adjudications

### Syria — HYBRID / bounded BLACK ZEBRA

The overt architecture is unusually explicit: the United States funded opposition councils, service delivery, independent media, civil defense, public safety, and Free Syrian Police. GAO records combined State-USAID teams, DOD support, and civil-military working groups. A separate CIA/partner-state program armed and trained anti-Assad rebels. Common strategic direction and theater make complementarity a corroborated strong inference. A public record assigning USAID to Timber Sycamore was not found.

### Ukraine (2013–14) — HYBRID

Long-running aid intentionally favored democratic and European integration, strengthened civil society, parties, elections, media, and governance, and was paired with open embassy engagement. That materially shaped the environment. The leap to operational direction of Maidan fails because protest initiation, tactical control, and event-specific U.S. command are not documented; local mobilization and state violence supply substantial causes.

### Venezuela — HYBRID / open ZEBRA core

Recognition, sanctions, opposition support, humanitarian pressure, a detailed transitional executive, security conditions, elections, and promised international financial support were openly combined to produce a transition. That is a documented Zebra toolkit. Local authoritarianism, repression, and economic collapse remain major causes, and no concealed compartment was recovered.

### Haiti — HYBRID

The Core Group's public intervention materially favored Ariel Henry in an ambiguous succession. The assassination remains a separate causal question. No evidence links USAID or the U.S. government to organizing the killing.

### Bangladesh — UNRESOLVED

The network is real: parties, polling, elections, youth, civil society, media, visa pressure, and a successor with unusually durable Western relationships. The expected event bridge—tasking, operational coordination, or succession agreement—was not recovered. The authenticated record does not support the disputed “destabilize” quotation.

### OCCRP — ZEBRA, not BLACK ZEBRA

Government funders intentionally built investigative capacity and measured effects through official/civic action. OCCRP retained editorial control. Both can be true. No evidence shows State or USAID assigning individual stories.

### Domestic bleedback — HORSE to UNRESOLVED

Tides, NewsGuard, Poynter, and CIR have real public or foreign-policy funding relationships. The named direct-pass-through claims failed: USAID dollars were not traced to BLM-linked entities, Fair and Just Prosecution, PolitiFact's domestic ratings, or CIR domestic operations. Fungible capacity is plausible but requires accounting proof.

### Sri Lanka — HORSE

The IMF's pre-rupture record documents tax cuts, debt, reserve depletion, tourism collapse, COVID shocks, inflation, and foreign-exchange shortages. Democracy-program co-presence does not beat that causal chain, and no deliberate role allocation toward Rajapaksa's removal was found.

### Myanmar — HORSE for the coup; ZEBRA for the response

The military coup defeated the elected government the United States supported. Post-coup sanctions, diplomatic pressure, and redirected civil-society/media aid were coordinated, but they were a response to the rupture, not its cause.

### Afghanistan — HYBRID control

Development, counterinsurgency, counternarcotics, diplomacy, contractors, and intelligence were deliberately networked. The republic's collapse was an unintended defeat for that architecture. This is strong evidence against theories that infer control from integration.

## Alternative explanation

The ordinary explanation—different agencies performing their legal mandates amid common foreign-policy priorities—accounts for much of the record. It fails only in bounded cases where documents show roles deliberately fitted together or sponsorship intentionally concealed.

## Final rule

Do not ask only whether CIA “controlled” an entity. Ask what the government funded, what effects it specified, which other instruments surrounded the program, and whether the case contains an explicit or high-diagnostic bridge. Autonomy can coexist with strategic utility. Co-presence cannot substitute for a bridge.
