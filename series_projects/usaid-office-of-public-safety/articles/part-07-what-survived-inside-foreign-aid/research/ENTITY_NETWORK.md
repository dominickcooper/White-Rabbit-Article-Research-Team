## Country target / recipient nodes

- **Syria:** opposition local councils, Syrian Coalition, activists, independent media, vetted armed opposition, Syrian Democratic Forces and stabilization communities. These are not interchangeable recipients; the network used political, civil and armed nodes for different purposes.
- **Ukraine:** civil society, election bodies, independent media, anti-corruption actors, government institutions and—after invasion—security forces.
- **Venezuela:** democratic organizations, Guaidó-aligned interim institutions, humanitarian recipients and regional partners.
- **Haiti:** election institutions, civil society, police/justice institutions and competing executive claimants.
- **Georgia:** government entities, civil society, media and election programs. Funding could move away from government while other nodes remained active.
- **Myanmar:** pre-coup government beneficiaries, post-coup civil society/media, humanitarian channels and the National Unity Government.

## Handoff mechanisms

1. **Funding handoff:** congressional appropriations → USAID/State → prime implementer → subgrantee/local partner.
2. **Policy/operations split:** State/NSC define objective → USAID/contractors implement civilian programs → DOD/DOJ/intelligence perform distinct security roles.
3. **Recipient migration:** assistance shifts from a government to civil society when antidemocratic actions make state channels untenable.
4. **Authority migration:** police/security or foreign-assistance functions move to an agency with a different statute after restriction or closure.
5. **2025 administrative migration:** remaining foreign-assistance awards moved from USAID administration to State, preserving some functions after the agency stopped administering assistance.

## Personnel finding

No modern personnel chain comparable to the old OPS/IPS record was established across the flagship cases. The recurring pattern is organizational and contractual, not a demonstrated cadre of individuals rotating through USAID, CIA, contractors and opposition groups. This negative finding materially narrows the theory.
