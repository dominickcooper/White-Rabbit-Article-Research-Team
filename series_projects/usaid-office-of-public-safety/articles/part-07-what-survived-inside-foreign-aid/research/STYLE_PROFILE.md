# Style Profile — Part 7

## Gold-example synthesis

- Narrator appears only to locate an investigation, document surprise, or make an inference; never to perform caution.
- Sections open with an actor, receipt, or contradiction—not an abstract thesis summary.
- Paragraph rhythm alternates dense documentary blocks with short turns and callbacks.
- Reveals arrive in sequence: receipt, odd detail, connection, boundary, next door.
- Questions are used at genuine forks, not as decoration.
- Quotations remain short and carry provenance in the sentence.
- Uncertainty is compressed to the edge that matters, then the narrative moves.
- Conclusions return to the opening object and enlarge it into an institutional pattern.

## Part 7 application

- Use Syria's divided system as the cold open.
- Let the books change the question before country analysis begins.
- Reserve research vocabulary (Zebra, adjudication, confidence) for artifacts, not publication prose.
- State strong inference plainly after showing its chain.
- Give the Horse case real narrative space so disconfirmation feels earned.
- Avoid repeated “this does not prove.” Name the missing edge once.
- Use the phrase “the grammar survived” as the finale callback, not as a repeated slogan.
