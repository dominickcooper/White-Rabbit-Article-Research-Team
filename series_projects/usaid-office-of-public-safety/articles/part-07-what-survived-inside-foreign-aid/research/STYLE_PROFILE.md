# Part 7 Style Profile

## Model

Use the permanent White Rabbit voice canon and approved Gold examples, especially the investigative rhythm of Part 5: document, realization, next document, connection. The finale should read as an investigator stress-testing a provocative map rather than a policy report or a prosecution brief.

## Narrative movement

1. Open on the accusation-by-pin-map problem.
2. Establish base rates and the amended Zebra categories quickly.
3. Use Syria as the full overt/covert proof case.
4. Test ambiguity through Ukraine and Venezuela.
5. Let Haiti, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Myanmar and Georgia change the category rather than merely add examples.
6. Treat OCCRP as the conceptual reveal: strategic information benefit can coexist with editorial independence.
7. Let the stateside test weaken the most inflammatory funding claims.
8. End on the 2025 USAID-to-State handoff and the series-level distinction between institution and method.

## Voice requirements

- Keep the investigator visible through concrete research decisions and negative findings.
- Lead with documents; interpret after the reader can see the mechanism.
- Use exact verbs: funded, awarded, redirected, recognized, sanctioned, trained, coordinated, tasked and controlled are not synonyms.
- Preserve local causation and recipient agency.
- State strong evidence with force; localize caveats beside the claim they limit.
- Keep most explanatory paragraphs to three to five sentences and reserve one-line paragraphs for major turns.

## Avoid

- Treating USAID presence as causal evidence.
- Calling every grantee or contractor a cutout.
- Saying USAID ran Timber Sycamore.
- Back-projecting a post-2014 CIA–Ukraine relationship into proof of Maidan control.
- Implying USAID caused Moïse's assassination or Myanmar's coup.
- Treating Tides-wide infrastructure as direct funding to BLM/FJP.
- Describing OCCRP as government-controlled without editorial-tasking evidence.
- Promising a Part 8; this is the finale.

## Formatting

- 4,000–7,000 words when evidence supports it.
- Descriptive H2s and selective H3 country turns.
- Exactly five FAQs.
- Three relationship-heavy image markers: world map, Syria network and stateside intermediary map.
- One subscribe marker and one share marker at earned pauses.
- Verified White Rabbit links in the required related-articles section.
- Exact source phrases mirrored in `output/sources.csv`.
