## Rockefeller Commission archival map

The Library of Congress finding aid identifies the most likely underlying evidence set:

- **Baker–Weidner File, boxes CL 1–CL 3:** federal-agency coordination and CIA aid to other
  agencies, explicitly including the federal portion of chapter 17.
- **Top Secret testimony, box TS 5:** 17 February 1975 testimony of Howard J. Osborn
  (misspelled “Osborne” in the finding aid), John W. Debelius, Raymond G. Rocca and Newton
  Scott Miler.
- **Colby files/testimony:** boxes CL 5, CL 9, CL 22, CL 26 and testimony appendices in CL 29,
  with Top Secret cross-references.

These are retrieval coordinates, not substantive evidence until the folders are examined.
