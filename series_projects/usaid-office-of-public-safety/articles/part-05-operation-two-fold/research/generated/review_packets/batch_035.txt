Phoenix Comes to Thanh Phong

Crisscrossed with waterways and rice paddies, Kien Hoa Province

in Kien Hoa Province accounted for more than five percent of the 
insurgency’s total leadership. 


In Operation Speedy Express, the US Army’s Ninth Infantry
