1* On December 12, 1966 I had a conference with Police Major Pramual 
VANIGBANDHU Central Bureau Of Narcotics regarding the increased number 
of Thai fishing boats transporting opium, morphine base and heroin from 
Thai ports into Hong Kong and Singapore* According to reliable information 
the modus operandi of these traffickers is as follow. After leaving Thai 
ports, usually at night to elude Customs surveillance, these boats proceed 
to a prearranged place outside the Hong Kong territorial water or Singapore. 
There, they would place opium and other contraband in the water and anchor it. 
(This contraband is previously placed in soldered containers). A day or two 
after the Thai boat had left the contraband at sea, a Hong kong fishing boat 
or junk knowing in advance where the contraband was left would proceed to the 
location, hook the contraband usually in tin cans and drag it under water 
into a safe port for distribution to the recipients, 

2. This method of smuggling narcotics out of Thailand have been very 
sucessful, aid traffickers are using this system more frequently knowing 
that Thai Customs and Police are very poorly equiped to intercept any 
shipment on it's way to Hong Kong or Singapore. 

3. During this conference Major Pramual who is the spokesman for General 
Nitya Bhanumas suggested that our office get in touch with the Admiral Commander 
of the OS 7th Fleet and have him order the US units operating in the Gulf of 
Siam and the South China Sea stop all Thai fishing boats and search them under 
the pretense that they might be carrying firearms, and if any of these boats 
are found to have narcotics to hold then and notify us. Members of the Thai 
Felice will then resndez vous with the OS unit seise the narcotics and arrest 
the smugglers.

6. I was further told by Mr Sullivan who is also very well up to date ©n 
on the traffic in Laos, that working in Laos would be a very difficult task 
because of the involvement of many high Laotian personalities. Mr Sullivan 
knew of laboratories operating in Louangprabang and Houei Sai. Mr Sullivan

7. Mr Sullivan further told me that if during my investigation in Laos I

the necessary approaches to the right peoples, and cooperation could maybe 
obtained on Governmental basis. 

8. I pointed out to Mr Sullivan the interest that the US Bureau Of Narcotics 
had regarding any laboratories operating in Laos, and further that all heroin 
or morphine base processed in Laos was exported to different areas of the world. 
Because in Laos there is no heroin nor morphine users. I also told Mr Sullivan 
that a large part of these narcotics is reaching the United States via Hong Kong 
Singapore, and different parts of Europe,
