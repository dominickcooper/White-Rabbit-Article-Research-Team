EVIDENCE OF PLANNING 


As stated In the body cf the Memorandum, the 
coming together of more than 60 persons from all over 
the United States and Cuba to a small village in upstate 
New York on a working day {Thursday, November M ) is 
by itself persuasive evidence that the Apalachin meeting 
was planned and not coincide rice, as claimed. indeed, 
the location of the Barbara heme in a r. out of rhe way 
and hard to reach area indicates at least that road 
instructions must hove been given. 

There is much additional specific evidence of 
planning as follows: 

On November 5, Barbara Sr. ordered over 
$400 worth of meat from Armour & Company for 
delivery November i3, including 20 boxes o? 
special cut steaks. This is the first known 
time an order was given for delivery at a 
later date. 

On November 6 , Bufalino came to Apalachin 
for a meeting with Barbara. 

On Monday, November II, the Barbara maid 
was instructed to clean the house for what 
appeared to her to be an obvious special event. 

Also on Novemver II, DeSimone and Scozzari 
flew from Los Angeles to New York and checked 
in at the Hotel New Yorker. 


(Attachment C ) 


On Tuesday, November 12, Barbara made 
reservations at the Arlington Hotel, to be 
charged to Canada Dry. These were later 
occupied by Larocca and M. Genovese. 

Also on November 12, Colletti from Colorado 
and Zito from Illinois checked in at the Riela- 
Bonanrto Airport Motel in Newark. 

On Wednesday morning November 13, 

Barbara, Sr. went to Binghamton Airport with 
another man and checked the TWA schedule. 

He also reconfirmed the reservations of 7 or 8 
other men at the Sawtelle Travel Service, 
bring ing the men with him. 

Also on November 13, Barbara, Jr. made 
reservations at the Parkway Motelfor a Canada 
Dry convention, faking the keys and refusing 
to identify the expected guests. Six men (who 
must first have seen Barbara, Jr. and obtained 
the keys) came, two with an Ohio car who 
refused to register saying "Joe would take care 
of it. " 

Beginning Wednesday afternoon November 
13, people began congregating in the area 
from all over. At least seven different motel 
and hotel groups have been identified 
(Attachment H). 

A preliminary meeting was held Wednesday 
afternoon and evening November 13, at the 
Barbara home. The Turrigiano, LaDuca, 
and Evola cars were there, and at least the 
following persons probably participated, in 
addition to the Barbaras: Carl i si, Cucciara, 
DeMarco, Evola, LcDucq, Lombardozzi, 
Riccobcno, Scalish and Turrigiano 

Another group met the evening of November 
13 at the Del Motel, joining Chiri and possibly 
Cucciara, Lombardozzi and Riccobono there.

Some guests remained overnight at the 
Barbara home, November 13, the maid making 
up four extra beds the following morning 
(November M) and observing six strangers 
at breakfast. 

The rest of the guests began arriving early 
Thursday, November 14. 5-6 men arrived on 

Mohawk Airlines (using aliases) and were picked 
up by Barbara, Jr. and driven to the house. 

The others arrived by cor or taxi, many in 
groups. (Attachment H). 

After learning of the roadblock, many of 
the attendants left by different means, either 
with other groups by car, on foot, or through

While this is evidence of consciousness of 
some kind of wrongdoing, it also is evidence 
that these persons did not come on a social 
call with the original groups just to visit a 
sick friend or after making a wrong turn on 
the road. 

Many of the attendants had travel in- 
structions, business cards and telephone 
numbers of other conferees, or had given the 
Barbara telephone number as an airline flight 
contact, also indicating that the meeting had 
been planned in advance. 

Four of the co-conspirators allege they 
were invited to a party for a sick man. At 
least these alibis are evidence that a meeting 
had been arranged in advance, which most 
attendants deny.
