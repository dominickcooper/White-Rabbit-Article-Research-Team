1. Reference is made to my memorandum report # 2864, dated September 2, 1966. 
Specific reference made to paragraph # 9 regarding the seizure of 250 kilos of 
opium made by the Vietnamese Military Security Sendees. 

2. to Tuesday September 6, 1966 I traveled to Saigon, Vietnam, and upon ray arrival 
I checked with the Saigon Municipal Police, The Vietnamese National Police, the Viet- 
namese Customs Agency, the US Customs Advisers USAID, C.I.A., QSI, and CID. None of 
the above mentioned agencies have heard of any opium seizure made in Saigon in the 
past month. (I did not check with the Vietnamese MSS not wanting to show that I had 
any doubt in Mr TAK's information), (it is very possible that MSS had seized the 250 
kilos of opium and are keeping quiet in order not to arouse suspicion of the VietCong 
organization in Saigon). 

3* to September 8, 1966 I confered with Mr Lucide C.I.A. in Saigon. I reported 
to Mr Lucide the information I obtained in Bangkok, and told him that the Vietnamese 
had now 2 tons of opium and wanted our cooperation. Mr Lucide replied that his Agent 
that we introduced to MSS was never contacted in regard to cooperation, and as long 
as MSS do not contact his Agency the US Ambassador will not approve the operation. 


4. to September 9, 1966 I returned to Bangkok and arranged to meet with Mr TAM. 

On September 12, 1966 I met Mr TAM and told him that during a short visit to Saigon, 

I attempted to check on the 250 kilos of opium seized but could not trace it. Mr 
TAM replied "please believe what I have told you, and very soon I’ll be able to 
prove it to you". At this point I decided that it was not in our interest to continue 
to promise transportation that we can not furnish, and told Mr TAM that I could not 
provide an aircraft to transport the 2 tons of opium to Saigon, I then suggested to 
Mr TAM to arrange for the seizure to be made in Bangkok, and also attempted to find 
out the location where the opium was in Bangkok. Mr TAM refused to do this, and stated 
that they (the Vietnamese) will transport the 2 tons of opium to Saigon the way they 
transported the 250 kilos, (by boat). (cent 1 !
