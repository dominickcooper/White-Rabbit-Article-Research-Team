1. Reference is made to my memorandum report dated August 14, 1968, 

case file number as above, subject: Interview of Louis DOUHERET con- 
ducted on August 8, 1968, at the Federal Penitentiary, Atlanta, Georgia, 
in which I reported the offer of cooperation made by DOUHERET and 
his conditions for such a cooperation.

2. On August 9, 1968, Customs Agent Albert W. Seeley and I briefed 
Acting District Supervisor Bernard A. Theisen Jr., Acting Enforcement 
Assistant George R. Halpin, Assistant U.S. Attorney Andrew Lawler, 

Customs Agent Edward T. Coyne, Group Leader, Joseph Dino, Narcotic 
Agents Thomas J. Dugan and Norris Durham on the results of the inter- 
views conducted at the Federal Penitentiary, Atlanta, Georgia, on August 
6,7,8, 1968, particularly, the interview of DOUHERET. 

i 

3. It was subsequently decided that DOUHERET' s full cooperation 
is desirable in that this cooperation will lead to the development of 
a far reaching conspiracy involving Joseph ORSINI, Fernand ORSINI, 

Arnold ROMANO, Dominick ROMANO, Frank CAMPANA and others. 

I 

4. On August 15, 1968, Assistant U.S. Attorney Andrew Lawler and 
I conferred with Assistant U.S. Attorney William Tendy, in charge of 
the narcotics section at the Office of the District Attorney, SDNY. 

Mr. Tendy concurred with the decision made to utilize DOUHERET' s 
testimony in the development of a conspiracy. Mr. Tendy, therefore, 
decided to make arrangements for DOUHERET' s transfer to a different 
penitentiary, in order to insure his security. Mr. Tendy further 
decided, that, without making any promises as to the ultimate result, 
he would eventually initiate steps to have DOUHERET' s sentence re- 
considered by the proper authority. Mr. Tendy authorized me to inform 
DOUHERET of the decision reached. 


5. On August 19, 1968, Customs Agent Seeley and I, while at the 

Federal Penitentiary, Atlanta, Georgia, on another matter, interviewed 
DOUHERET who reiterated his offer to cooperate and requested a
