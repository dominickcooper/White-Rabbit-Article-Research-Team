MEMORANDUM FOR H. R. HALDEMAN 
SUBJECT: PERSONNEL RECRUITMENT 


Donald E. Santarelli, 32, Associate Deputy Attorney General, 

Office of Criminal Justice 
Department of Justice 

Don has been indefatigable in preparing and advocating the entire 
District of Columbia crime bill for the Administration. He is 
absolutely loyal, a man with the highest integrity and true brilliance. 

He has received the unique honor of being appointed to the University 
of Virginia Board of Visitors at the age of 32. I was hoping that 
some Presidential recognition of this honor could be indicated to him, 
perhaps by letter. He is thoroughly devoted to the President and to 
the success of this Administration. His resume and background of 
his recent appointment to the Board of Visitors are at Tab A. 

John Dean , 31, Associate Deputy Attorney General. for Legislation 
Department of Justice 

John has responsibility for the overall Administration crime pro- 
gram in the Congressional relations area. He worked with Senator 
Hruska before joining the Justice Department and he has been one 
of my closest confidants in developing Congressional strategy. He 
served as an outstanding negotiator with the New Mobilization 
leadership prior to the last march on Washington and, in my judgment, 
he was responsible for much of the positive publicity we received. 

His resume is attached at Tab B. 

Gene Knorr , -30, Deputy Special Assistant to the Secretary for 
Congressional Relations 
Department of the Treasury 

Gene has worked with me in liaison with the Hill in fire?arms legisla- 
tion and Swiss Bank Accounts. He! is bright, loyal, and while not

Dave Miller , 28, Special Assistant to the Attorney General 
Department of Justice 

Dave was a White House Fellow last year and is now involved in special
