# Research dossier: After the Office of Public Safety

## WHAT THIS PART ADDS

Readers learn that no direct OPS successor has been documented; by 1990 a legally differentiated, poorly coordinated architecture reached 125 countries at a known minimum cost of $117 million.

## EARLIER FINDINGS REQUIRED FOR CONTEXT

OPS closed; §660 restricted FAA funding but originally preserved narcotics and specified FBI/DEA activity. GAO found immediate compliance plus indirect pathways.

## NEW FINDINGS

- FY1990 portfolios: INM $45m, ATA ~$10m, ICITAP/Justice portfolio ~$20m, DOD ~$42m; overlap in 46 countries.
- ICITAP arose in January 1986 after §534(b)(3) expanded judicial/prosecutorial-control investigative assistance; AID transferred funds to DOJ.
- ICITAP began with human-rights prosecutions/investigative capacity, then expanded to full police development in Panama.
- FBI's preexisting Academy trained about 100 international officers annually; more than 1,100 from 89 countries over the prior decade.
- ATA served 49 countries in FY1990.
- ILEA Budapest opened in 1995 from a post-Cold War U.S.-Hungarian initiative; no IPA transfer found.
- No agency qualifies as documented institutional descent on reviewed evidence.

## NEW SOURCES

GAO NSIAD-92-118; DOJ ICITAP FAQ/milestones; State ILEA history; FBI ILEA history; laws collected in GAO appendix I.

## NEW PEOPLE / ORGANIZATIONS

ICITAP; INM; ATA; ILEA; OPDAT at boundary only.

## EVIDENCE-WEIGHTED CONCLUSIONS

Foreign-police assistance survived through original exceptions and later congressional reauthorization. Its post-OPS form was fragmented and mission-specific. Functional continuity is documented; institutional descent is not.

## RESPONSIBILITY ASSESSMENT

Congress deliberately reopened categories. State, Justice and Defense managed separate programs; fragmented data and coordination limited government-wide accountability.

## CONTRARY EVIDENCE

New missions, legal authorities, judicial integration, human-rights purposes and oversight distinguish later programs from OPS. Similarity alone is insufficient.

## COMPETING EXPLANATIONS

Secret OPS continuation fails for lack of transfer evidence. Pure discontinuity fails because functions and some personnel persisted. Fragmented reauthorization best fits the total record.

## CONNECTIONS TO MASTER DOSSIER

Closes the security-agency succession question and preserves the separate finale question of what remained inside foreign aid.

## QUESTIONS RESOLVED

ICITAP's legal origin, 1990 scale, agency division, ILEA classification and strongest personnel bridge.

## QUESTIONS CARRIED FORWARD

Names/careers of five narcotics advisers; transferred manuals/files; complete Justice own-funds total; later oversight effectiveness.
