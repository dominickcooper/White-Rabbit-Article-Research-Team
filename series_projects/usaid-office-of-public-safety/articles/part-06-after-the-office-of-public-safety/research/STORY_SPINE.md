# Part 6 Story Spine

## Central mystery

When the Office of Public Safety disappeared, did its strategic effects merely re-emerge through unrelated bureaucracies—or did the United States deliberately divide complementary roles among new institutions?

## Opening receipt

Mike Benz’s provocation: “When it’s too dirty for the CIA, you give it to USAID.” Use it as a hypothesis, then immediately replace the simplistic CIA-proxy question with a structural one: who funded, who guided, who operated, who implemented, who received, and who gained access?

## Reader’s initial model

Parts 1–5 showed a centralized and partly concealed architecture. The reader initially expects either a renamed successor or total discontinuity after Section 660.

## Reveal ladder

### 1. The prior architecture was deliberate

Recall the overt AID center, Engle’s covered CIA status, IPS proprietary training, Police Group liaison, participant-information routing and TWO-FOLD’s covert BNDD recruitment. These are historical Black Zebra compartments inside a wider interagency system.

### 2. Congress genuinely killed OPS

Preserve the institutional rupture. Section 660 ended the visible office while retaining and later expanding bounded legal routes.

### 3. ICITAP reveals overt network design

GAO’s funding transfer and the institutional history show deliberate role differentiation: AID money, State policy, Justice operations, FBI expertise, contractor implementation and foreign recipients. ICITAP is overt networked statecraft—not OPS renamed and not a covert program.

### 4. Kriskovich crosses the assigned roles

Use his FBI → Caribbean course → AID-funded/DOD-supported Salvadoran work → ICITAP → Bosnia path as the human bridge. He proves transmission across seams, not CIA command.

### 5. GAO reveals scale and uneven integration

$117 million, 125 countries, 46 with multiple programs. Separate the two findings: selected programs had deliberate role allocation; the whole aggregate lacked unified policy and accounting. Deliberate networking and bureaucratic disorder coexisted.

### 6. Name the network

Congress supplied authorities; State policy; AID and other accounts money; Justice/DEA/FBI operations and expertise; DOD support; embassies coordination; contractors implementation; host governments institutions; intelligence agencies liaison and information access where documented.

### 7. Public purpose is compatible with Zebra

Professional police, lawful courts, development and agricultural assistance were real public purposes. Networked statecraft does not require false purposes or universal secrecy.

### 8. Intelligence access was designed, not merely incidental

Source registries, station-chief notification, regional centers, Police Group liaison and earlier information routing form deliberate information architectures. Formal ownership was unnecessary because access itself could be an intended product.

### 9. Contractors can be ordinary or attribution-sensitive

Bosnia shows overt contractor implementation and accountability gaps. ZunZuneo shows the narrower concealed version.

### 10. ZunZuneo provides the concealed modern compartment

OTI authority, Creative implementation, offshore structures, concealed sponsorship and attempted segmentation. Preserve OIG’s limits while explaining why concealment is analytically distinct from overt ICITAP networking.

### 11. Afghanistan provides overt integrated strategy

Development, counternarcotics, stabilization and counterinsurgency were deliberately combined. Contradictions and unintended poppy effects occurred inside that planned structure; hidden narcotics intent is unnecessary and unsupported.

### 12. Keep the boundaries

Bangladesh’s disputed sentence and Naruvu’s fictional scenario cannot carry operational claims. Media/OCCRP and domestic bleedback remain unresolved and outside the main thesis.

### 13. Benz’s strongest supported theory

The record repeatedly shows strategic functions divided among overt aid, diplomacy, law enforcement, military support, contractors and intelligence channels. Sometimes overt, sometimes concealed, often with genuine public purposes. The recurring feature was deliberate role differentiation.

### 14. Payoff

OPS was easy to see because it concentrated the functions. The successor architecture was harder to see because it distributed them. In several documented cases, distribution was the design. Congress killed the office. The network became the organization.

Provocation → historical recognition → apparent closure → funding receipt → human bridge → scale shock → role map → designed information access → overt/hidden distinction → modern tests → disciplined limits → stronger realization.
