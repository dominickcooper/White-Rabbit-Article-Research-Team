# ZEBRA ANALYSIS — AMENDED PROTOCOL

## Scope and non-mutation notice

This is a from-scratch rerun of the Zebra Protocol against the preserved Part 6 USAID/Office of Public Safety corpus and the established Parts 1–5 record.

It does **not** modify or reinterpret the article as publication prose. `article.md`, `STORY_DECISION.md`, and `STORY_SPINE.md` remain untouched pending human review of this analytical output.

Companion artifact: `ZEBRA_MATRIX.csv`.

## Correction to the first implementation

The first implementation made central direction the implicit dividing line between Zebra and Hybrid. That was conceptually wrong.

A network can be deliberately assembled without having one commander, one organization chart, one universal target list, or one participant who sees the whole. Role differentiation may itself be the operating design.

The amended diagnostic is:

> **Was role differentiation deliberately used to produce a broader strategic effect?**

The relevant contrast is therefore not centralized control versus decentralization. It is:

- **emergent strategic effect**, produced mainly by ordinary bureaucratic convergence; versus
- **deliberately assembled strategic effect**, produced by intentionally complementary roles.

## Amended model definitions

### HORSE — primarily ordinary bureaucratic convergence

Agencies and private actors pursue their own public missions. Their activities overlap because they address the same country, crisis, or policy problem. Any wider strategic effect is substantially incidental, emergent, or explained by normal coordination.

HORSE signatures include:

- separately authorized public missions;
- no evidence that role separation was used toward a shared broader objective;
- similar work arising independently;
- incidental information spillover;
- routine contracting for capacity or expertise;
- ordinary embassy or interagency deconfliction;
- duplication, rivalry, and incoherence outweighing integrated planning;
- effects not reasonably attributable to deliberate assembly.

### ZEBRA — deliberately networked statecraft

Different overt and/or covert institutions are intentionally assigned or used for complementary roles around a broader strategic objective.

Nodes may include USAID, State, CIA, DOD, DOJ, DEA/FBI, contractors, NGOs, media organizations, civil society, foreign governments, and local political organizations.

ZEBRA does **not** require:

- one central commander;
- CIA ownership;
- a common organization chart;
- a master target list spanning every program;
- one universal command memorandum;
- every participant knowing the whole strategy;
- every program being covert;
- every public purpose being false.

ZEBRA signatures include:

- explicit interagency role allocation;
- complementary funding, policy, and operational responsibility;
- country strategies assigning different tools toward a common objective;
- functional migration after restriction;
- civilian authorities deliberately used for security objectives;
- intermediaries used where organizational distance or attribution matters;
- information products intentionally useful to other nodes;
- synchronized programs focused on a common strategic target;
- embassy, NSC, or interagency planning;
- doctrine describing a combined toolkit;
- demonstrated historical reuse of the same architecture.

### BLACK ZEBRA — covert or concealed compartment

BLACK ZEBRA is a subtype of ZEBRA, not a separate theory of the entire system. It applies when a deliberately networked arrangement includes a covert, deniable, clandestine, or concealed compartment.

BLACK ZEBRA signatures include:

- covered personnel;
- proprietaries;
- shell companies;
- concealed sponsorship;
- covert recruitment;
- secret tasking;
- intelligence requirements;
- clandestine funding;
- cutouts;
- false attribution.

A BLACK ZEBRA judgment is analytical. It does not automatically make a legal determination that a program constituted “covert action.”

### HYBRID — substantial Horse and substantial Zebra activity

HYBRID applies when the evidence shows both:

1. substantial ordinary or emergent activity; and
2. substantial deliberately networked statecraft;

with no basis for claiming that the entire ecosystem is consciously orchestrated.

HYBRID is not a fallback for missing central command. If roles were deliberately differentiated around a strategic objective, that portion is Zebra even when nobody commanded the whole network.

### UNRESOLVED

The corpus lacks the records needed to distinguish incidental convergence from deliberate assembly.

## Revised overall adjudication

### Core post-OPS architecture: ZEBRA

The amended protocol changes the classification of the core Part 6 architecture.

The following are not merely emergent similarities:

- AID transferred money to DOJ;
- State supplied policy guidance;
- Justice operated ICITAP;
- FBI personnel supplied expertise;
- Congress created mission-specific legal routes;
- DOD, DEA, FBI, State, Justice, AID, contractors, and host governments performed complementary functions;
- post-conflict and counternarcotics strategies deliberately combined civilian and security tools;
- intelligence and law-enforcement agencies created formal information-sharing mechanisms.

Those arrangements qualify as **deliberately networked statecraft** even though they lacked one commander and contained real public purposes.

### Whole corpus/ecosystem: HYBRID

The entire research universe remains **HYBRID**, but for a different and narrower reason than in the first analysis.

It remains Hybrid because the corpus also contains substantial Horse activity:

- genuine post-conflict policing needs;
- normal agency competence and jurisdiction;
- ordinary contracting;
- poor coordination and duplication;
- Afghanistan programs producing unintended or contradictory effects;
- unproved media-network and domestic-bleedback claims;
- programs and outcomes that cannot be shown to belong to a common strategic design.

Thus the corrected overall judgment is:

> **The core historical and post-restriction architecture is Zebra; the full ecosystem is Hybrid because deliberate networked statecraft coexists with substantial ordinary, emergent, and unintegrated activity. Black Zebra compartments are documented inside the wider network.**

This is a material change from the first implementation. Previously, absence of central command pushed many propositions into Hybrid. Under the amended protocol, explicit role differentiation is affirmative Zebra evidence.

## Evidence-stream independence

The independence rules remain essential.

### Genuinely independent high-value streams

1. **Presidential/NSC/State/AID architecture records from Parts 1–2** — prove overt interagency design and recognition of separate covert activity.
2. **CIA personnel, proprietary, liaison, and training records** — prove Engle’s covered status, IPS, Police Group liaison, participant assessment, and information routing.
3. **Statutes and congressional action** — prove the restriction, exceptions, and later legal authorities.
4. **GAO oversight** — proves funding handoffs, aggregate scale, incomplete accounting, and coordination problems.
5. **Rockefeller/TWO-FOLD and separate CIA–BNDD/DEA records** — prove covert recruitment, personnel transfer, source coordination, and selective functional preservation.
6. **DOJ/NDU institutional history** — reconstructs ICITAP’s division of labor and personnel bridge, though some claims depend on agency interviews.
7. **USAID OIG ZunZuneo review** — independently establishes the contractor, grant, offshore, concealment, and political-information architecture.
8. **GAO and SIGAR Afghanistan oversight** — independently document explicit integration and policy conflict, though both rely partly on agency program data.

### Partially independent streams

- The Senate ZunZuneo hearing is institutionally separate from OIG but often discusses the same program record.
- Human Rights Watch’s Bosnia report contains distinct interviews, CID material, and tribunal findings, but the underlying records were not all independently acquired.
- Current DOJ pages corroborate program chronology but are institutional self-description.

### Non-independent or weak streams

- The Benz interview and derivative reports count as one lead stream.
- Four CIA Reading Room files that resolve to the same generic landing-page shell do not constitute four sources.
- Repetition of OCCRP or Bangladesh claims in commentary does not substitute for the missing award, report, or authenticated original.

## Four-question test by proposition

## P01 — USAID as an interface among State, CIA, and DOD

### 1. Is the overlap incidental?

Some post-OPS country overlap is incidental. Agencies often operate in the same crisis because their public jurisdictions converge.

### 2. Is it ordinary interagency coordination?

Yes, in part. State-USAID planning and civil-military coordination can be ordinary foreign-policy administration.

### 3. Were roles intentionally divided around a common strategic objective?

Yes in documented historical and selected later settings. Parts 1–2 establish an AID-led interagency internal-defense architecture. Afghanistan and stabilization records deliberately combine development, diplomacy, and military security. ICITAP and the Salvadoran SIU show complementary civilian and security roles.

### 4. Is there a covert/deniable compartment?

Yes historically: Engle’s cover, IPS, concealed follow-on training, and Police Group liaison. The post-OPS corpus does not establish an equivalent standing CIA-USAID compartment.

### Adjudication

**HYBRID, high confidence.** Zebra architecture is documented, including historical Black Zebra, but the broad post-OPS CIA-USAID-DOD interface remains only partly established.

## P02 — Foreign assistance as an attribution and oversight mechanism

### 1. Incidental?

Some organizational distance is an ordinary consequence of appropriations and contracting.

### 2. Ordinary coordination?

Yes. Foreign-assistance accounts create statutory controls, reporting, congressional notification, and inspector-general review.

### 3. Intentional role division?

Yes. Section 660’s account boundary mattered operationally; AID financed Justice; State directed policy; contractors implemented programs. Civilian authorities were intentionally used within broader security and political objectives.

### 4. Black Zebra compartment?

Yes in specific cases. ZunZuneo used concealed sponsorship and offshore entities. OPS used cover and a proprietary. This cannot be generalized to all aid.

### Adjudication

**HYBRID, high confidence.** Foreign assistance can simultaneously create oversight and attribution distance.

## P03 — Deliberate division of funding, policy, and operations

### 1. Incidental?

No. The ICITAP split was an actual administrative arrangement, not a coincidental accumulation of programs.

### 2. Ordinary coordination?

Partly. Agency competence explains why Justice supplied law-enforcement operations and State supplied foreign-policy guidance.

### 3. Intentional role division?

Yes. AID funded, State guided, Justice operated, and host governments received. The institutional history describes a conscious compromise shaped by legal authority, capacity, and the political reputation of OPS.

### 4. Black Zebra compartment?

Not established for ICITAP.

### Adjudication

**ZEBRA, very high confidence.** This moves from Hybrid. Overt, lawful, and legitimate role allocation still constitutes deliberately networked statecraft.

## P04 — Political influence through civil society

### 1. Incidental?

Some political effects are inherent in civic participation, journalism, and election work without implying strategic orchestration.

### 2. Ordinary coordination?

Yes. Overt democracy and transition programs normally coordinate with diplomatic objectives.

### 3. Intentional role division?

Partly documented. OTI and democracy programs deliberately build political and civic capacity. ZunZuneo intentionally assembled a politically useful audience. The broader civil-society proposition remains underdocumented.

### 4. Black Zebra compartment?

ZunZuneo qualifies. The Bangladesh destabilization claim does not, because the decisive language is unauthenticated.

### Adjudication

**HYBRID, medium confidence.** Deliberate political network-building exists, but the corpus cannot characterize the full civil-society field.

## P05 — Contractors and NGOs as intermediary layers

### 1. Incidental?

Much outsourcing reflects ordinary staffing, expertise, and local-access requirements.

### 2. Ordinary coordination?

Yes. Contract and grant management are routine implementation tools.

### 3. Intentional role division?

Yes. Creative, DynCorp, and ICITAP contractors were deliberately assigned operational functions inside broader programs. Organizational distance and flexibility were part of what contractors supplied.

### 4. Black Zebra compartment?

Creative’s ZunZuneo structure qualifies because attribution was intentionally obscured. DynCorp’s Bosnia role does not become Black Zebra merely because accountability was weak.

### Adjudication

**HYBRID, high confidence.** Ordinary outsourcing and attribution-sensitive intermediation both materially exist.

## P06 — Information generated by ostensibly civilian programs

### 1. Incidental?

Program monitoring, surveys, subscriber records, and journalism naturally generate information.

### 2. Ordinary coordination?

Agencies may lawfully share useful information without converting the originating program into an intelligence operation.

### 3. Intentional role division?

Yes in specific cases. OPS/IPA deliberately supplied participant names, biographies, and orientation assessments. ZunZuneo deliberately attempted political segmentation. Anti-corruption journalism intentionally generates actionable public information, but strategic government use is not sufficiently documented.

### 4. Black Zebra compartment?

OPS/IPS qualifies. ZunZuneo’s concealed sponsor plus political-data effort is a partial Black Zebra signature.

### Adjudication

**HYBRID, high confidence.** Intentional information production is documented; routine post-OPS intelligence dissemination is not.

## P07 — Intelligence benefit without formal ownership

### 1. Incidental?

Some benefit can arise incidentally from open reporting or partner relationships.

### 2. Ordinary coordination?

Source deconfliction and liaison are legitimate features of counternarcotics work.

### 3. Intentional role division?

Yes. Source registries, station-chief notification, regional information centers, and historical OPS liaison intentionally positioned different institutions to produce and exploit information toward shared security objectives.

### 4. Black Zebra compartment?

Yes in OPS/IPS and TWO-FOLD. No Black Zebra finding is needed for the broader proposition.

### Adjudication

**ZEBRA, very high confidence.** This moves from Hybrid. Formal ownership is irrelevant where intelligence benefit is an intended product of role allocation.

## P08 — Post-restriction functional migration

### 1. Incidental?

Persistent public demand explains why similar capabilities reappeared, but not the documented handoffs.

### 2. Ordinary coordination?

Congress openly created exceptions and agencies lawfully used them.

### 3. Intentional role division?

Yes. Narcotics authorities, AID-to-DOJ financing, Justice operation, State guidance, post-conflict exceptions, personnel bridges, and contractor implementation intentionally reassigned functions after OPS became unavailable.

### 4. Black Zebra compartment?

TWO-FOLD’s selective foreign continuation and historical OPS-to-DEA mechanisms contain Black Zebra elements. ICITAP’s migration was overt.

### Adjudication

**ZEBRA, very high confidence.** This moves from Hybrid. The absence of an en bloc successor does not make the migration emergent.

## P09 — ZunZuneo

### 1. Incidental?

Audience growth and user data can arise from ordinary platform operation, but sponsor concealment did not.

### 2. Ordinary coordination?

OTI legitimately conducts political-transition programming in closed information environments.

### 3. Intentional role division?

Yes. OTI, Creative, grant recipients, offshore entities, content acquisition, and audience-segmentation work were deliberately combined toward a political-influence objective.

### 4. Black Zebra compartment?

Yes. Concealed U.S. sponsorship, offshore corporate structures, indirect ownership, and attribution management satisfy the analytical definition.

### Adjudication

**BLACK ZEBRA, high confidence.** This moves from Hybrid. The classification does not assert that OIG made a legal covert-action finding; it did not.

## P10 — ICITAP

### 1. Incidental?

No. The AID/State/Justice arrangement was deliberately constructed.

### 2. Ordinary coordination?

Yes, and it served real investigative and post-conflict needs.

### 3. Intentional role division?

Yes. Legal authority, foreign-assistance money, diplomatic policy, Justice operations, FBI expertise, contractors, and foreign recipients were assembled around a shared objective.

### 4. Black Zebra compartment?

None located.

### Adjudication

**ZEBRA, very high confidence.** This moves from Hybrid. ICITAP is overt Zebra, not OPS renamed and not a Black Zebra program.

## P11 — CIA/DEA coordination

### 1. Incidental?

No. The strongest relationships were formal and intentional.

### 2. Ordinary coordination?

Yes in part. Narcotics threats created legitimate need for intelligence, source coordination, and liaison.

### 3. Intentional role division?

Yes. CIA supplied training, recruitment, foreign tradecraft, intelligence support, and liaison while BNDD/DEA supplied law-enforcement authority, foreign offices, sources, and operational reach.

### 4. Black Zebra compartment?

Yes. TWO-FOLD’s covert recruitment, proprietary cover, covered agents, and selective secret continuation meet the standard.

### Adjudication

**BLACK ZEBRA, very high confidence** for the compartment; **ZEBRA** for the wider CIA–DEA relationship. This moves from Hybrid.

## P12 — Afghanistan

### 1. Incidental?

Some overlap and many adverse effects were emergent products of one conflict environment, poor knowledge, and contradictory incentives.

### 2. Ordinary coordination?

Yes. Wartime country teams and PRTs normally coordinate development and security.

### 3. Intentional role division?

Yes. U.S. strategy expressly aligned counternarcotics with counterinsurgency, expanded DOD’s role, shifted eradication priorities, and used agriculture and alternative development inside a combined stabilization strategy.

### 4. Black Zebra compartment?

None established in the retained Afghanistan evidence.

### Adjudication

**ZEBRA, high confidence.** This moves from Hybrid. Deliberate integration is documented even though many results were incoherent, failed, or unintended.

## P13 — Media and anti-corruption networks

### 1. Incidental?

Possibly. Independent reporting naturally produces political, legal, and financial consequences.

### 2. Ordinary coordination?

Possibly. Funders may openly support anticorruption without directing stories.

### 3. Intentional role division?

Not yet established. A Zebra finding would require grants, approval rights, target-selection records, prosecutor coordination, or strategy documents assigning journalism a role in a broader campaign.

### 4. Black Zebra compartment?

No authenticated evidence.

### Adjudication

**UNRESOLVED, low confidence.** This changes from Horse/Hybrid-open. The proposition cannot be adjudicated from outcome metrics and an interview lead alone.

## P14 — Domestic “bleedback”

### 1. Incidental?

This currently fits best. Information crosses borders, organizations operate in multiple markets, and separately funded capacities can have spillover effects.

### 2. Ordinary coordination?

Smith-Mundt modernization and normal legal access do not themselves prove domestic operations.

### 3. Intentional role division?

No evidence in this corpus traces foreign-aid-funded capacity, data, methods, or personnel into a deliberately assigned domestic political role.

### 4. Black Zebra compartment?

None documented.

### Adjudication

**HORSE, medium confidence.** This does not move. Deliberate domestic reuse remains a research hypothesis.

## P15 — Historical precedents from Parts 1–5

### 1. Incidental?

No. The strongest architecture was explicitly designed.

### 2. Ordinary coordination?

The overt portions were formal interagency internal-defense and counternarcotics policy.

### 3. Intentional role division?

Yes. NSAMs allocated overt AID, military, diplomatic, and separately recognized covert functions. Later CIA–BNDD/DEA arrangements divided intelligence and enforcement roles.

### 4. Black Zebra compartment?

Yes. Engle’s cover, IPS proprietary status, concealed training, Police Group liaison, TWO-FOLD recruitment, and proprietary cover are direct signatures.

### Adjudication

**BLACK ZEBRA, very high confidence** for the covert compartments inside a wider Zebra architecture. This moves from Hybrid.

## Final amended judgment

The corrected definitions materially change the analysis.

The core finding is no longer merely that a fragmented ecosystem produced strategic effects. It is that several of the most consequential effects were produced through **deliberate role differentiation**:

- overt aid and covert access in OPS;
- intelligence and law-enforcement roles in CIA–BNDD/DEA arrangements;
- funding, policy, and operations in ICITAP;
- civilian development and military stabilization in Afghanistan;
- government authority, contractor execution, offshore distance, and concealed attribution in ZunZuneo.

Those are Zebra arrangements even when public missions were real, authority was dispersed, participants lacked full knowledge, and no agency commanded the whole.

At the same time, the protocol does not justify declaring the entire USAID ecosystem Zebra. Ordinary programs, institutional rivalry, failure, duplication, incidental spillover, and unproved modern allegations remain substantial. The total ecosystem is therefore Hybrid—but its load-bearing Part 6 architecture is now adjudicated primarily as **Zebra with documented Black Zebra compartments**.
