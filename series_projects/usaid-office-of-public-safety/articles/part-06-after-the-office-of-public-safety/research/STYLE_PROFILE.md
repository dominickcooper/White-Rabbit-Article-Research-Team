# Part 6 Style Profile

## Model

Use the permanent White Rabbit voice canon and the registered Gold articles, especially Part 5. The piece should feel like an investigator following a paper trail rather than a policy memo summarizing a claims table.

## Narrative movement

1. Open on the apparent institutional death: OPS closes under Section 660.
2. Put the reader inside the discovery that the statute controlled one funding channel, not all federal police relationships.
3. Follow the paper trail into AID's 1986 transfer to DOJ, then introduce ICITAP's unusual administrative structure.
4. Use David Kriskovich as the human-scale bridge through the bureaucratic architecture.
5. Widen from one man/program to the measurable ecosystem: State narcotics and antiterrorism, DEA/FBI, DOD, ILEA, post-conflict missions and contractors.
6. Test the sinister version directly: CIA continuity, contractor misconduct, and modern influence programs. State what the records do and do not show.
7. Land on the payoff: reform abolished the visible office but produced a distributed system whose accountability problem was fragmentation.

## Voice requirements

- Visible investigator: “The next document changed the question” is acceptable; claims-ledger prose is not.
- Documents first, interpretation second.
- Short paragraphs, mostly three to five sentences.
- Distinguish `funded`, `administered`, `guided`, `contracted`, `trained`, `coordinated`, and `controlled`.
- Localize caveats next to the claim they limit.
- Preserve credible conventional explanations: post-conflict policing, transnational crime, narcotics, human rights and weak local institutions were real policy problems.
- Do not announce caution or neutrality. Demonstrate it with exact verbs.

## Avoid

- “Everything was connected.”
- Describing geographic overlap as a handoff.
- Calling ZunZuneo covert action; OIG did not make that finding.
- Presenting the Naruvu vignette as an actual operation.
- Presenting Afghan development failures as intentional heroin support.
- Treating the contested IRI file as authenticated.
- Making the article a tour of every modern USAID controversy; those are Part 7 material.

## Formatting

- 2,500–5,000 words.
- Descriptive H2s that advance the reveal.
- Five concise FAQs.
- Eight image markers with public-domain/official-document concepts.
- Four internal links to Parts 1–5 using verified slugs.
- Sources linked through exact phrases mirrored in `output/sources.csv`.
