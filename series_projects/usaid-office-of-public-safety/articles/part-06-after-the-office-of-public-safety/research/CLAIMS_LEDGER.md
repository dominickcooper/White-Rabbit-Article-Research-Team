## Governing evidentiary conclusion

The post-OPS story is not institutional survival through renaming. It is functional survival through law, interagency funding, specialization, intelligence/law-enforcement coordination, post-conflict exceptions, grantees and contracting. The architecture was more fragmented than OPS, often larger in aggregate, and harder to audit. Benz’s strongest structural proposition survives the evidence: the network became the organization. His universal CIA-proxy proposition does not.
