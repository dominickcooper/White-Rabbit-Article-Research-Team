Neither entry is `DOCUMENTED EMPLOYMENT` through an acquired corporate personnel file. ## INDIVIDUAL CONTINUITY ≠ CORPORATE CONTINUITY

These two trajectories demonstrate that selected aviation experience and relationships reappeared. They do not establish that Air America survived as a company, became the Contra air network, transferred corporate control, or directed the later operation.

Safe article-level concept: “Some veterans of the Southeast Asian covert-air world later appeared in Central American covert logistics.”
