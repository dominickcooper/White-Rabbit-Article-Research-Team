# Part 3 Tier 1 Source Acquisition

Date completed: 2026-09-20  
Scope: Phase 2A source acquisition and provenance only. This record does not revise article prose, create Claim Ledger V3, or authorize claims whose evidence gates remain open.

## Acquisition standard

A package is **RESOLVED** only when an authoritative, locally preserved source directly supports the required proposition. **PARTIAL** means useful authoritative or near-authoritative evidence was found but a material link remains unverified. **NOT FOUND** means the requested primary record was not acquired. Search-result snippets, requester descriptions in FOIA logs, newspaper clippings, and compiled aircraft databases are discovery aids, not substitutes for the requested primary record.

All local files below are in `series_projects/usaid-office-of-public-safety/articles/part-03-the-laboratory-countries/sources/`. Access date for all URLs: 2026-09-20.
