# SECTION 660 PRIMARY RECORD

Date: 2026-09-20  
Gate: GATE-SECTION660  
Verdict: **CLOSED FOR THE PART 3 CORE CHRONOLOGY**

## Enactment

Congress enacted Section 660 through §30(a) of Public Law 93-559 on December 30, 1974. The provision added §660 to the Foreign Assistance Act of 1961.

## Effective Date

The enacted text states that the restriction applied **“On and after July 1, 1975.”** Enactment and effective date must not be collapsed.

## Original Statutory Text

The law broadly barred use of Foreign Assistance Act funds and local currencies generated under that act for training, advice, financial support, or other assistance to foreign police, prisons, other law-enforcement forces, and programs of internal intelligence or surveillance on behalf of a foreign government.

Controlling location: P3-S026, PDF 9-10; 88 Stat. 1803-1804.

## Original Exceptions

The original law already excluded:

- assistance under §515(c) of the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act;
- assistance relating to DEA or FBI authority concerning conduct unlawful under U.S. law;
- assistance authorized by FAA §482 for international narcotics control; and
- completion of qualifying personnel contracts entered before enactment, while barring renewals or extensions made on or after enactment.

## Narcotics-Control Authority

The international narcotics-control exception existed at enactment. It was not first created by a later amendment. Part 3 may therefore say that Congress imposed a broad police-assistance prohibition while preserving the FAA §482 narcotics-control channel from the start.

## DEA/FBI Status

The text preserved assistance relating to the authority of DEA and FBI concerning crimes unlawful under U.S. law. The later official GAO chronology also explains that agencies operating with their own appropriations were not necessarily governed by the FAA funding prohibition in the same way. This does not make DEA or FBI an OPS replacement.

## Later Amendments

Public Law 99-83, enacted August 8, 1985, added country exceptions and FAA §534 Administration of Justice authority. Section 534(b)(3) authorized qualifying investigative-capability programs notwithstanding §660. The law also added a maritime-law-enforcement exception. GAO's official retrospective records that ICITAP began in January 1986 under the later authority.

## What Part 3 May Say

“Congress broadly restricted U.S. foreign police assistance effective July 1, 1975, while the enacted law preserved international narcotics-control assistance and specified DEA/FBI authorities.”

Part 3 may briefly distinguish the 1985 amendments to avoid implying that every exception existed in 1974.

## What Part 3 Must Leave to Part 5

The detailed development, operations and later amendments of ICITAP belong in Part 5. Part 3 should not use the 1985 authority to characterize the original OPS period, or present ICITAP, DEA or FBI as OPS under another name.
