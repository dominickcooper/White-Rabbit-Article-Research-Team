## 3. Section 660 Gate

### Primary Legal Timeline

- December 30, 1974: Public Law 93-559 enacted Section 660.
- July 1, 1975: the prohibition became effective.
- At enactment: FAA §482 narcotics-control assistance and specified DEA/FBI authorities were excepted.
- August 8, 1985: Public Law 99-83 added country exceptions and §534 Administration of Justice authority, including qualifying programs notwithstanding §660.
- January 1986: ICITAP began under the later authority, according to the official GAO chronology.

### Final Interpretation

Congress broadly restricted Foreign Assistance Act support for foreign police and related forces while preserving narcotics-control and specified federal law-enforcement channels. Later amendments expanded or clarified other routes. Distinct legal channels are not institutional succession.

### Safe Wording

“Congress broadly restricted U.S. foreign police assistance effective July 1, 1975, while the enacted law preserved international narcotics-control assistance and specified DEA/FBI authorities.”

### Prohibited Wording

“DEA replaced OPS,” “Section 660 secretly preserved OPS,” or any motive claim not established by the legal record.
