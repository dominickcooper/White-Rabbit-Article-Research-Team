# Final John Hull Chain

## Documented CIA relationship

CIA OIG vol. II records extensive Agency contact with Hull and use of his Costa Rican property in the Southern Front support environment. Internal cables and congressional briefings treated him as a known Contra support figure. This is **DOCUMENTED FACT**.

## Documented Contra logistics and airstrips

Hull's ranches/airstrips formed part of the Costa Rican Contra logistics network. The OIG record discusses pilots, flights, communications with the strip, and searches connected to aircraft activity. This is **DOCUMENTED FACT**.

## Firsthand drug testimony

Kerry witnesses, including pilots and people connected to George Morales, alleged weapons-south/cocaine-north flights and use of Hull-associated strips. The OIG reproduces and tests several statements. Witness credibility and details conflict, and no complete flight/cargo ledger was located. This remains a **CORROBORATED ACCOUNT**, not documentary proof of every flight.

## Law-enforcement findings

Costa Rican proceedings accused Hull of hostile acts and included drug-trafficking allegations in court or investigative materials. U.S. and Costa Rican authorities did not produce a final adjudication establishing the full alleged drug scheme in the reviewed record. This is **DOCUMENTED LAW-ENFORCEMENT CONCERN**, not a conviction finding.

## Unresolved allegations

- Whether Hull knowingly authorized drug loads on his strips.
- Whether every named pilot or flight was tied to his operation.
- Whether political protection, evidentiary weakness, or both explains failed prosecution/extradition.

## Verdict

The CIA relationship, Contra logistics, and airstrip access are documented. Drug-flight use rests on multiple firsthand accounts and investigative records but remains contested. **CORROBORATED ACCOUNT / STRONG INFERENCE** for drug traffic intersecting the same logistics; do not state that a court proved Hull directed trafficking.
