## Independence conclusion

- Strongest genuinely independent convergence: official Laos admissions + McCoy's local witnesses + Marchetti/Marks insider account.
- Strongest institutional convergence: Kerry committee + CIA OIG on Morales and the existence of Contra-drug problems.
- Most commonly overcounted clusters: McCoy repeated by Scott; Kerry repeated by Scott/Marshall; Blandón repeated across Webb and OIG; CIA denials repeated across Agency publications.
- The unit of independence must be the underlying witness/document, not the book title.
