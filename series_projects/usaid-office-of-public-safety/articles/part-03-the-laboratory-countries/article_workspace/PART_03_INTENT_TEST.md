## Best-fit intent conclusion

The evidence does not stop at accidental proximity. It supports conscious tolerance, episodic protection, material facilitation, and exploitation when narcotics-connected actors were operationally useful. The system-level intent best supported is **intent to preserve covert capability despite known narcotics consequences**. That differs from intent to cause U.S.-bound trafficking and differs still more from seeking U.S. narcotics distribution as an objective.
