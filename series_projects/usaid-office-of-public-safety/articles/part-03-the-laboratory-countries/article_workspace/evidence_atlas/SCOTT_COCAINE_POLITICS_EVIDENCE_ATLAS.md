# Scott & Marshall — Cocaine Politics Evidence Atlas

Primary reviewed source: Peter Dale Scott and Jonathan Marshall, *Cocaine Politics* (local PDF).

## Source-integrity and method note

Each card separates what the cited passage establishes from what it does not. Page numbers are PDF pages unless a printed page is explicitly identified. Short quotations are locator phrases, not substitutes for the surrounding passage. Repeated authors do not count as independent when they share an underlying witness or report.

Much of the Contra section synthesizes the Kerry record. Those repetitions are dependent, not independent corroboration.

**WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH:** Contract cargo itself contained drugs.

**CORROBORATING SOURCES:** Kerry report PDF 27–30.

**CONTRADICTING SOURCES:** None established in this passage.

**UNDERLYING SOURCES TO ACQUIRE:** Full Kerry exhibits and company payment files.
