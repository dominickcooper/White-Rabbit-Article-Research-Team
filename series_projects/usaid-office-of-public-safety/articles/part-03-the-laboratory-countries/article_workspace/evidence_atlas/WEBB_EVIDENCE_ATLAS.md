# Gary Webb — Dark Alliance Evidence Atlas

Primary reviewed source: Gary Webb, *Dark Alliance* (local PDF), tested against CIA OIG Volumes I and II.

## Source-integrity and method note

Each card separates what the cited passage establishes from what it does not. Page numbers are PDF pages unless a printed page is explicitly identified. Short quotations are locator phrases, not substitutes for the surrounding passage. Repeated authors do not count as independent when they share an underlying witness or report.

Webb is strongest on court records, interviews, and the California endpoint. The CIA OIG finding of no CIA relationship with Ross, Blandón, or Meneses is material contrary evidence.

## WEBB-001

**SOURCE:** Gary Webb, *Dark Alliance*

**AUTHOR / AGENCY:** Gary Webb

**DATE:** 1998

**PAGE:** PDF 31–40

**FOOTNOTE / ARCHIVE ID:** Blandón grand-jury transcript; federal court files; Kerry hearings

**COUNTRY / REGION:** California / Nicaragua

**PEOPLE:** Oscar Danilo Blandón; Norwin Meneses

**ORGANIZATIONS:** Contra support network; DOJ; DEA

**PROGRAM:** Contra fundraising

**EXACT CLAIM:** Court and grand-jury records placed Blandón in major cocaine dealing and tied his early activity to Contra fundraising; Meneses was a long-standing target of drug investigations.

**SHORT QUOTATION:** “selling cocaine for the Contras”

**EVIDENCE TYPE:** investigative secondary source using primary court records

**NARCOTICS CONNECTION:** Large-scale cocaine sales.

**FINANCIAL CONNECTION:** Claimed purchases and donations for Contra support.

**POLITICAL / MILITARY CONNECTION:** None established in this passage.

**U.S. ENDPOINT CONNECTION:** California market.

**WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES:** Trafficker–Contra fundraising overlap and U.S. distribution.

**WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH:** CIA direction, knowledge of each sale, or creation of crack demand.

**CORROBORATING SOURCES:** CIA OIG Vol. I confirms trafficking histories and investigates relationships.

**CONTRADICTING SOURCES:** CIA OIG found no CIA relationship with Ross, Blandón, or Meneses and no proof proceeds materially sustained Contras.

**UNDERLYING SOURCES TO ACQUIRE:** None established in this passage.

**ARTICLE 3 VALUE:** High U.S.-endpoint evidence with major scope limits.
