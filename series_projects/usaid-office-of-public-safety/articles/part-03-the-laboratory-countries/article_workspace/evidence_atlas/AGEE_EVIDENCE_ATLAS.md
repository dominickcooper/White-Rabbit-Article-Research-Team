# Philip Agee — Verified Evidence Atlas

Primary reviewed source: Philip Agee, *Inside the Company: CIA Diary* (Stonehill Publishing Company, 1975), complete local scan. Page numbers below give PDF page followed by printed page.

## AGEE-001 — Public Safety Missions as cover and recruitment access

**PAGE:** PDF 55–56 / printed 47–48  
**COUNTRY / REGION:** Latin America  
**ORGANIZATIONS:** CIA; ICA/AID Public Safety Missions; police and civil intelligence services  
**EXACT CLAIM:** Agee describes Public Safety Missions as valuable CIA cover and says police-assistance relationships supported spotting, penetration, and recruitment.  
**EVIDENCE TYPE:** Firsthand former-officer account reconstructed after service.  
**WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES:** AID police assistance could supply officer cover, institutional access, and recruitment opportunities.  
**WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH:** That every mission or adviser was CIA, or that the arrangement served narcotics activity.  
**CORROBORATION:** Marchetti/Marks; MSU Smuckler oral history; Motta's structural account; Ahern's official histories.  
**EVIDENCE LEVEL:** **CORROBORATED ACCOUNT.**

## AGEE-002 — Named AID Public Safety cover cases

**PAGE:** PDF 268 / printed 260; PDF 405–406 / printed 397–398; PDF 409 / printed 401; PDF 420–421 / printed 412–413  
**PEOPLE:** Ted Shannon; Alejandro Otero; Bill Cantrell  
**EXACT CLAIM:** Agee identifies officers under AID Public Safety cover, an accepted proposal to place an officer full-time in police intelligence under that cover, Otero's police-school and CIA intelligence training sequence, and Cantrell's cover status.  
**EVIDENCE TYPE:** Named firsthand operational account.  
**WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES:** Multiple specific examples of cover, training, and police-intelligence placement.  
**WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH:** Independent personnel-file confirmation for each named person.  
**EVIDENCE LEVEL:** **MULTIPLE SPECIFIC FIRSTHAND ACCOUNTS; STRUCTURALLY CORROBORATED.**

## AGEE-003 — Mexico as covert analogue

**PAGE:** PDF 461 / printed 453  
**COUNTRY / REGION:** Mexico  
**EXACT CLAIM:** Agee describes a secret arrangement functioning as the equivalent of an AID Public Safety mission under nonofficial cover.  
**WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES:** The access function could be reproduced without a formal overt mission.  
**WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH:** A USAID funding stream or a narcotics connection.  
**EVIDENCE LEVEL:** **FIRSTHAND ACCOUNT.**

## Source-integrity note

Agee says he reconstructed the diary in 1973–74 using research to reinforce his recollection. An official CIA review recognizes the book and its operational detail while disputing Agee's interpretation and conduct. The source is authentic and firsthand but is not a contemporaneous verbatim log. No Agee claim is treated as conclusive without corroboration.
