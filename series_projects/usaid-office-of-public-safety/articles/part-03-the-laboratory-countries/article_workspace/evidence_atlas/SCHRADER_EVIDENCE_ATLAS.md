# Stuart Schrader — Badges Without Borders Evidence Atlas

Primary reviewed source: Stuart Schrader, *Badges Without Borders* (local PDF).

## Source-integrity and method note

Each card separates what the cited passage establishes from what it does not. Page numbers are PDF pages unless a printed page is explicitly identified. Short quotations are locator phrases, not substitutes for the surrounding passage. Repeated authors do not count as independent when they share an underlying witness or report.

Schrader is strongest on OPS scale, police-led counterinsurgency, and the MSU/ICA/CIA-cover connection. It contains no drug route.

**U.S. ENDPOINT CONNECTION:** None established in this passage.

**WHAT THIS ESTABLISHES:** Scale, institutional location, and an acknowledged CIA funding channel within the training ecosystem.

**WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH:** That all trainees or missions were CIA-controlled, or any narcotics role.

**CORROBORATING SOURCES:** None established in this passage.

**CONTRADICTING SOURCES:** None established in this passage.

**UNDERLYING SOURCES TO ACQUIRE:** The AID and academy records cited in nn.15–16.
