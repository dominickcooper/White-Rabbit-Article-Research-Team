# Rodrigo Patto Sá Motta — Modernizing Repression Evidence Atlas

Primary reviewed source: Rodrigo Patto Sá Motta, “Modernizing Repression: USAID and the Brazilian Police” (local PDF).

## Source-integrity and method note

Each card separates what the cited passage establishes from what it does not. Page numbers are PDF pages unless a printed page is explicitly identified. Short quotations are locator phrases, not substitutes for the surrounding passage. Repeated authors do not count as independent when they share an underlying witness or report.

This source materially strengthens the USAID-as-access thesis in Brazil. It does not complete a narcotics chain.
