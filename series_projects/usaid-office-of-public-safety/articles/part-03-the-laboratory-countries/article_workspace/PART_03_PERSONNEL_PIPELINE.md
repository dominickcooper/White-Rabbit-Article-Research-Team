## Personnel finding

The people map shows three different categories that must not be collapsed: aid personnel providing access/capacity; CIA or proxy personnel pursuing covert objectives; and narcotics actors supplying money/logistics. The thesis is strongest where named individuals occupy more than one category and where support continued after knowledge.
