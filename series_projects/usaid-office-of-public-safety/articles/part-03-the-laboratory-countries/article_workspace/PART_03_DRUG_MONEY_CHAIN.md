# PART 03 DRUG MONEY CHAIN

## Laos

Opium crop → village/officer buyers → premium/reliable market → Royal Lao/Hmong commander revenue → troop/political maintenance.  
**Evidence:** McCoy PDF 210–211, 231–233; interviews/fieldwork.  
**Weight:** CORROBORATED ACCOUNT / STRONG INFERENCE.  
**Break:** No audited ledger connects CIA or USAID accounts to opium proceeds; no profit-sharing with CIA is documented.

## Morales / Southern Front

Cocaine-trafficking organization → about $100,000 + aircraft + training → ARDE/FRS personnel and facilities → proxy operational capacity; reciprocal use of pilots/facilities for drug movement.  
**Evidence:** Kerry PDF 30–32; OIG Vol. II PDF 22–23.  
**Weight:** CORROBORATED ACCOUNT approaching DOCUMENTED FACT for the exchange.  
**Break:** No proof CIA received drug proceeds.

## Contractor payments

U.S. appropriated “humanitarian” funds → SETCO / DIACSA / Frigorificos / Vortex → aircraft and freight supporting the Contra system; firms/principals had drug records.  
**Evidence:** Kerry PDF 27–30.  
**Weight:** DOCUMENTED FACT.  
**Break:** Payments purchased legitimate logistics; no proof the paid shipments contained drugs or that payments laundered drug revenue.

## Blandón / Meneses

Cocaine sales in California → limited claimed purchases/donations (truck, radios, medical supplies, office costs) → Contra support.  
**Evidence:** Webb PDF 128–130; OIG Vol. I PDF 15–16.  
**Weight:** Firsthand claim, unsubstantiated in amount; PLAUSIBLE CONNECTION.  
**Contrary evidence:** OIG found no CIA relationship and no evidence the trafficking was motivated by the Contra cause.

## Bueso

Planned drug transaction → assassination financing → prosecution → operational-value advocacy/leniency.  
**Evidence:** Kerry PDF 66–67.  
**Weight:** DOCUMENTED FACT for enforcement accommodation, not a proxy-funding stream.

## Barry Seal

DEA sting proceeds → proposed $1.5 million diversion to Contras → DEA rejection.  
**Evidence:** Kerry PDF 26, 66–67.  
**Weight:** DOCUMENTED attempted redirection; no actual transfer.

## Noriega / BCCI

Noriega and cartel-linked networks → BCCI cash/account system → intelligence payments and illicit funds coexist in the same bank environment.  
**Evidence:** Kerry PDF 12, 46–47, 64; Cocaine Politics PDF 17–19.  
**Weight:** DOCUMENTED shared infrastructure; **DOCUMENTARY BREAK** for commingling or CIA control of drug money.

## Bottom line

The corpus supports several bounded benefit chains: drug-linked money/assets benefited local commanders, Southern Front operators, and proxy logistics. It does not support a central CIA drug-revenue account or a single bank-to-covert-operation laundering chain.
