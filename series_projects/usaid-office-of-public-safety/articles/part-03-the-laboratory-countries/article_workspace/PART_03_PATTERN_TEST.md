## Pattern adjudication

The pattern repeats most fully in Laos and Central America. It does not support a single centrally managed intercontinental CIA narcotics program. It does support a recurring institutional behavior: strategically useful narcotics-connected allies and logistics were preserved after knowledge, while enforcement yielded to covert-war priorities. That recurring behavior is a **STRONG CROSS-SOURCE INFERENCE** and must not be generalized to countries where the middle links are absent.
