## Bottom-line classification

- **Association:** YES — DOCUMENTED.
- **Knowledge:** YES — STRONGLY SUPPORTED in Laos and Contra cases.
- **Tolerance:** YES — STRONGLY SUPPORTED in selected theaters.
- **Protection:** LIKELY — STRONG INFERENCE overall; DOCUMENTED in at least one narrow intervention; not generalizable without case proof.
- **Facilitation:** LIKELY — STRONG INFERENCE, mainly indirect/logistical/political.
- **Exploitation:** POSSIBLE to LIKELY for partner utility; specific intentional exploitation of drug activity remains incomplete.
- **Financial benefit:** YES for some local actors/Contra causes; INSUFFICIENT for CIA as an institution.
- **Direct participation:** INSUFFICIENT.
- **Intentional U.S.-bound system:** INSUFFICIENT, with significant contrary evidence.
