## Important correction

The comparative case does not prove a universal CIA policy to protect traffickers. It does show repeated decisions in which operationally valuable actors or carriers remained available after narcotics warnings, and reporting/prosecution was shaped by covert-program equities.

## Verdict

Central America remains a **MATCH** for the narrower recurring pattern: useful ally or contractor → serious narcotics information → government knowledge → continued relationship or logistics access → enforcement/reporting anomaly. It is a comparative pattern test, not proof of the Laos facts.
