# Final John Lewallen Source Check

## Identification

**Work:** John Lewallen, “The Reluctant Counterinsurgents: International Voluntary Services in Laos,” in Nina S. Adams and Alfred W. McCoy, eds., *Laos: War and Revolution* (Harper & Row, 1970), pp. 357–365; McCoy cites pp. 361–362 in note 86.  
**Source type:** Contemporary analytical chapter associated with IVS experience.  
**Archive:** Bibliographic record located; full chapter not acquired in this pass.

## What can be established

Later scholarship quotes Lewallen at p. 360 describing IVS volunteers' “agonizing reappraisal” as allegedly apolitical development became support for one side in a shooting war. McCoy uses Lewallen alongside Senate refugee-hearing material for integration of IVS/refugee activity into the covert-war system.

## What cannot be established

- The full pp. 361–362 wording was not recovered.
- The source has not been shown to document narcotics carriage.
- It does not independently establish Air America drug loads or Xieng Khouang Air Transport.
- Biographical search results create uncertainty over whether Lewallen personally served in Laos or reported from the IVS network; this was not resolved.

## Verdict

**PARTIAL INDEPENDENT CORROBORATION** for the conversion of relief/development work into counterinsurgency support. It is not a new narcotics source. Full-text acquisition reached diminishing returns and is not necessary to the article's central evidentiary chain.
