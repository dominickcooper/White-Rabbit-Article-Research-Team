## Asset and caption rules

- Prefer primary-document crops with date, sender, recipient, archive ID, and page.
- Do not use aircraft photography as proof of a drug shipment; captions must say what the image establishes.
- Do not draw a continuous Brazil cocaine arrow. Insert a visible **DOCUMENTARY BREAK**.
- Use McCoy/Webb/Scott book covers only as source-roadmap visuals, not as evidentiary proof.
- Reconcile Brazil training totals before converting them into an infographic.
- Treat Brazilian Truth Commission images/testimony with victim-centered captions and precise sourcing.

## Targeted-pass visual revisions

1. **Evidence ladder, not accusation cloud:** Place each major case on ASSOCIATION → KNOWLEDGE → TOLERANCE → PROTECTION → FACILITATION → EXPLOITATION → FINANCIAL BENEFIT → DIRECT PARTICIPATION → INTENTIONAL SYSTEM. Show most evidence clustering in the middle, with the last two rungs visibly unproved.
2. **1982 MOU gap diagram:** Staff/contract employees on a mandatory-report branch; assets/agents/independent contractors on the nonemployee branch; narcotics visibly absent until 1995. Caption that discretionary cooperation still existed.
3. **Air America claim splitter:** Separate “no headquarters order,” “knowing pilot carriage disputed,” “carriage accounts corroborated,” and “systemic facilitation strongly inferred.”
4. **Kerry/OIG convergence map:** Link Morales, Hull, Hyde, Bueso, Seal, SETCO, DIACSA, Frigorificos, Vortex, and Noriega by the specific mechanism—cash, aircraft, airstrip, fuel, contractor payment, intelligence value, or enforcement anomaly.
5. **Brazil documentary break:** Keep the dashed gap between security architecture and a named U.S.-bound cocaine route. Do not visually imply that Condor itself was a drug operation.
