# Final U.S. Narcotics Endpoint Chain

## Regional flow — DOCUMENTED FACT

Official U.S. records described Golden Triangle production, regional refineries, and movement through Vientiane, Luang Prabang, Bangkok, Saigon, Hong Kong, and other international channels. A 1972 CIA study described producers and refineries responding to the GI market and multiple commercial/smuggling channels.

## GI market — DOCUMENTED FACT

FRUS 1969–76, vol. E–1, doc. 220 identifies Laos as a source affecting U.S. forces after smugglers from Vientiane were caught with heroin at Tan Son Nhut and a Lao dealer admitted supplying heroin. CIA and congressional material placed the number of heroin-using U.S. troops in Southeast Asia in the tens of thousands.

## U.S. market — DOCUMENTED FACT

The August 1972 official fact sheet in FRUS doc. 225 estimated Southeast Asia supplied under ten percent of U.S. heroin at that time, while warning that heroin formerly directed to GIs was being redirected to the United States and that the region was a serious potential source. The CIA study likewise described ex-GIs mailing high-purity heroin to the United States and other maritime/art-object channels.

## Specific Laos-to-U.S. shipment — DOCUMENTARY BREAK

No reviewed record traces one continuous load from a named Lao producer, commander, airline, or airfield to a named American distributor. That narrower link should not be asserted.

## Verdict

The required system endpoint is **DOCUMENTED AT THE REGIONAL, GI, AND U.S.-CONNECTED MARKET LEVELS**. The missing door-to-door shipment record is a real limitation only for a claim about a particular load, not for the regional pipeline thesis.
