# Final Kerry Primary-Source Upgrades

## Search result

The published Kerry report was not counted as a new source. The targeted search instead used the CIA Inspector General's two-volume *Report of Investigation: Allegations of Connections Between CIA and the Contras in Cocaine Trafficking to the United States*, which reproduces and describes cables, memoranda, law-enforcement traces, interview statements, congressional briefing records, and agency responses.

The complete Kerry exhibit/deposition archive was not located as a discrete public collection. Original Costa Rican case files, every sworn deposition, and all referenced exhibits therefore remain incomplete.

## Documentary upgrades

- **SETCO:** CIA OIG vol. II, PDF pp. 275–278, cites a 1983 Customs investigative report identifying SETCO as formed by people dealing with Juan Matta Ballesteros and smuggling narcotics into the United States; CIA/Contra records show SETCO became a principal FDN carrier. A 1989 CIA response incorrectly reported no SETCO record even though CATF use was common knowledge internally.
- **Vortex/Universal:** CIA OIG vol. II, PDF pp. 280–289, records the carrier's Contra contracts, CIA contacts, late traces, Customs reporting that company personnel were suspected traffickers, and termination after derogatory information.
- **Alan Hyde:** CIA OIG vol. II, PDF pp. 307–321, supplies the strongest documentary chain in the comparative section: repeated Coast Guard/USDAO/cable allegations, operational-necessity approval, extensions, lower enforcement priority, and a 1993 cable warning that his CIA connection could complicate prosecution.
- **John Hull:** CIA OIG vol. II preserves witness statements, cables, congressional briefings, Costa Rican proceedings, and Agency denials. It corroborates Contra logistics and CIA contact but does not resolve contested drug-flight allegations.
- **Southern Front/Morales:** OIG records Pastora's receipt of aircraft and funds from trafficker George Morales and his account of later terminating the arrangement.

## Verdict

**MEANINGFUL PRIMARY-DOCUMENT UPGRADE THROUGH THE CIA OIG REPORT, NOT THROUGH A COMPLETE KERRY EXHIBIT ARCHIVE.** Existing Kerry conclusions remain valid when narrowly framed. The strongest new comparative evidence is Hyde and the SETCO record-handling anomaly. Further exhibit hunting reached diminishing returns.
