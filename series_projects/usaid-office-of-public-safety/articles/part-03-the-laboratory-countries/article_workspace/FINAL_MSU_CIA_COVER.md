# Final MSU / CIA Cover Check

## Primary records located

- In a Michigan State University oral history, former project chief Ralph Smuckler said President John Hannah agreed after National Security Council contact to accept a CIA officer seconded into the MSU police team. Smuckler called the arrangement “no big secret” inside the relevant leadership and described negotiating withdrawal from the Sûreté/VBI role, after which AID assumed that work.
- The MSU archive finding aid documents a 71-box, roughly 4,000-folder Vietnam Project collection, including police-administration records.
- The MSU research guide records contracts among MSU, the U.S. Foreign Operations Administration, and the Vietnamese government for government and police administration.

## Five-officer allegation

The *Ramparts* account names Raymond Babineau, Raymond Beed, Charles Jones, Robert Smith, and Frank Stein and alleges a five-person CIA team inside police administration, some on university salary or academic rank. No complete personnel/payroll file independently confirming all five was acquired.

## Answers

**WHO KNEW?** Senior MSU leadership, including Hannah and Smuckler, knew at least one CIA officer was embedded.  
**WHO OBJECTED?** Smuckler's oral history describes institutional concern and a negotiated retreat from the sensitive police-intelligence role; Jack Ryan's separate complaint remains a cited but unacquired correspondence chain.  
**WHO WERE THE CIA PERSONNEL?** One officer is established by primary oral history but unnamed there. The five names remain a secondary-source allegation.  
**WHO PAID?** FOA/USOM funded the project. The pay status of individual CIA personnel remains unclear.  
**WHAT COVER WAS PROVIDED?** Placement inside the ICA/FOA-sponsored university police-administration team.  
**DID ICA / USOM KNOW?** Project leadership and NSC involvement are documented; no primary record located proves the awareness of every ICA/USOM official.

## Verdict

**DOCUMENTED FACT** that at least one CIA officer operated inside the MSU police project with senior institutional awareness. **CORROBORATED ACCOUNT** for a larger five-person team. This upgrades the USAID/AID cover-access pattern but does not itself establish a narcotics link.
