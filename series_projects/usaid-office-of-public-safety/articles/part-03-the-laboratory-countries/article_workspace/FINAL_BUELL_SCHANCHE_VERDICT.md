# Final Buell / Schanche Verdict

## Source and context

Don A. Schanche, *Mister Pop: The Adventures of a Peaceful Man in a Small War* (David McKay Company, 1970), complete 337-page scan. Title and imprint appear on PDF pp. 4–5. The jacket states that Schanche knew Buell in Laos, and the book records repeated visits to his area (photo caption, PDF p. 206). McCoy's note 83 also records his interview with Schanche on February 12, 1971.

## Subclaim findings

| Subclaim | Evidence | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buell promoted sweet potatoes as an alternative cash crop | Schanche PDF p. 142 / printed p. 131 says the effort failed because transport and a market were absent, costing villagers a year's opium production. | **DOCUMENTED** |
| Buell later abandoned immediate crop elimination during the war | Schanche PDF p. 267 / printed p. 242 explains that he regarded eradication without another cash source as quixotic. | **DOCUMENTED** |
| Buell helped improve poppy cultivation and said, “If you're gonna grow it, grow it good” | Direct narrative attribution in Schanche PDF p. 267 / printed p. 242. | **DOCUMENTED IN SCHANCHE; CORROBORATED ACCOUNT**, not archival transcript |
| Cultivation help preceded or coexisted with substitution work | The earlier substitution failure and later cultivation passage form a coherent chronology. | **CORROBORATED** |
| Modern medicines reduced local opium smoking while output rose | Schanche PDF p. 267 says medicine reduced smoking and “there was more opium.” | **DOCUMENTED IN SCHANCHE** |
| Buell knowingly flew opium | Schanche PDF pp. 266–269 says Buell refused to fly it and feared prosecution. | **CONTRADICTED** |
| Air America/CIA carriage is established by Schanche | At PDF p. 269 Schanche labels the conclusion speculative and reasons from control of transportation. | **WEAK as direct proof** |
| Buell once inadvertently moved villagers' opium and observed a modern aircraft loading a large opium consignment | Schanche PDF pp. 50–51. The episode is narrated directly, but its interview basis is not itemized. | **CORROBORATED ACCOUNT** |

## Did Schanche witness or interview Buell?

The book does not identify the cultivation quotation as a contemporaneous transcript or say Schanche personally witnessed that exact conversation. Schanche nevertheless had direct access to Buell and his field area. The proper classification is a direct biographical attribution by a firsthand-access author, not an independently documented Buell admission.

## Did McCoy represent Schanche accurately?

Yes. McCoy's formulation is faithful to Schanche's printed account. The earlier ledger classification “WEAK / UNCORROBORATED” was too severe because the cited underlying source was not then local. It should now be **CORROBORATED ACCOUNT** with the limitation above.

## Final verdict

The cultivation-help and crop-substitution accounts are not contradictory. They describe different responses to the same wartime cash-crop problem. Schanche upgrades the cultivation claim but simultaneously undercuts any use of his book as direct proof that Buell or Air America knowingly transported opium.
