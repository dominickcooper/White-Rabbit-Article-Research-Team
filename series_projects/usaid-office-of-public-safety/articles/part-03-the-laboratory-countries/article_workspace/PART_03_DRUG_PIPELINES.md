# PART 3 DRUG PIPELINE MAPS

These maps distinguish a commodity route from the political-security architecture around it. An arrow means the preceding node plausibly or demonstrably feeds the next; it does not imply CIA command unless stated.

### Knowledge ladder

- **Association — documented:** CIA-backed forces occupied opium-producing territory; Air America served them.
- **Knowledge — strongly supported:** CIA knew the narcotics economy and allegations involving allies existed.
- **Tolerance — strongly supported:** narcotics suppression was subordinated to the war mission.
- **Protection — likely / case-dependent:** preservation of implicated allies and territory insulated networks; specific protective interventions need files.
- **Facilitation — strong inference:** logistical and territorial support improved the resilience of the surrounding opium economy.
- **Exploitation — possible:** CIA benefited from allies sustained partly by the local economy; proof it intentionally exploited drug revenue is incomplete.
- **Financial benefit — likely for local forces, insufficient for CIA accounts:** the crop supported actors inside the proxy system.
- **Direct participation — disputed:** knowing Air America carriage is credibly alleged and officially denied.
- **Intentional system — contradicted/insufficient:** no evidence CIA designed a heroin pipeline into the United States.

## Intersection verdict

The pipelines intersect most clearly at **territory, transport access, political protection, intelligence relationships, and enforcement priority**. They intersect less clearly at **central finance** and least clearly at **direct CIA handling of drugs**. The article can defend a system that made allied trafficking more resilient; it cannot yet defend a single intentional CIA supply chain into the United States.

## Targeted-pass revisions

- **Southeast Asia:** The chain is now documented/corroborated from opium territory through CIA-backed forces, allied protection, transport opportunity, refining, South Vietnam, and the U.S. troop market. Regional heroin also reached the U.S. civilian market. The missing bridge is a flight-by-flight, knowingly CIA-directed chain from origin to a U.S. shipment.
- **Central America:** Kerry and OIG make the overlap concrete: traffickers provided aircraft, pilots, cash, and services; implicated contractors received U.S. payments; intelligence value and foreign-policy goals affected enforcement. The record establishes multiple pipelines using common infrastructure, not one CIA-owned conveyor.
- **Brazil/Southern Cone:** Security/intelligence capacity and regional cocaine/security overlaps are established separately. The named protected actor → route/finance → U.S. destination bridge remains broken.

The publication verb remains **enabled** or **preserved**, not **ran**.
