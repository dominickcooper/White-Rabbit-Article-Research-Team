# Final Philip Agee Verification

## Edition

**Title:** *Inside the Company: CIA Diary*  
**Author:** Philip Agee  
**Publisher:** Stonehill Publishing Company  
**Year:** 1975  
**Local source:** Complete 572-page scan; Google Books records the Stonehill edition and 639 printed pages.

Agee says in the introduction (PDF p. 6) that he reconstructed the diary in 1973–74 from his operational experience and four years of research. This is a firsthand former-officer source reconstructed after the fact, not a contemporaneous daily log.

## Authentication and limits

An official CIA review acknowledges the 1975 book and its detailed classified/operational content while sharply disputing Agee's conduct and interpretation. That review authenticates the work's identity, not every claim.

## Verdict

**MULTIPLE SPECIFIC FIRSTHAND ACCOUNTS, STRUCTURALLY CORROBORATED.** Agee materially upgrades the AID/OPS-as-cover and recruitment-access thesis. Because the diary was reconstructed and named personnel files were not obtained, it should not be treated alone as conclusive proof of every individual identification.
