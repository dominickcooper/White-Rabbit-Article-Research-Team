# PART 3 FIRSTHAND ACCOUNTS

“Firsthand” applies only to the portion a witness personally observed or decided. Statements about another person's intent, distant cargo, or institutional policy remain hearsay/inference.

## Southeast Asia / Air America

**WITNESS:** Gen. Ouane Rattikone  
**ROLE:** Senior Lao military figure with proximity to aviation and the opium economy.  
**DATE OF ACCOUNT:** McCoy interview, early 1970s.  
**FIRSTHAND OBSERVATION:** Told McCoy Air America carried opium.  
**HEARSAY COMPONENT:** Knowledge of individual flight-crew awareness or CIA headquarters direction is not necessarily personal.  
**DOCUMENTARY CORROBORATION:** Multiple local accounts; official inquiry conceded occasional carriage risk and relations with suspected traffickers.  
**MOTIVE / POSSIBLE BIAS:** Self-interest, factional rivalry, and possible displacement of responsibility.  
**CONTRADICTING SOURCE:** 1972 CIA inquiry's clean bill for pilots/no policy.  
**CURRENT WEIGHT:** MEDIUM-HIGH for carriage in the operating system; MEDIUM-LOW for institutional authorization.

**WITNESS:** Gen. Thao Ma  
**ROLE:** Lao Air Force commander/aviation insider.  
**DATE OF ACCOUNT:** McCoy interview, early 1970s.  
**FIRSTHAND OBSERVATION:** Described opium carriage within the U.S.-supported air environment.  
**HEARSAY COMPONENT:** CIA policy/crew knowledge beyond operations personally observed.  
**DOCUMENTARY CORROBORATION:** Same as above.  
**MOTIVE / POSSIBLE BIAS:** Political conflict and exile/factional interests.  
**CONTRADICTING SOURCE:** Official denial.  
**CURRENT WEIGHT:** MEDIUM-HIGH for operational carriage, lower for policy.

## Contra / Central America

**WITNESS:** Octaviano César  
**ROLE:** ARDE political official/Southern Front insider.  
**DATE OF ACCOUNT:** Kerry investigation, 1980s.  
**FIRSTHAND OBSERVATION:** Morales arrangement and claimed CIA notification/response.  
**HEARSAY COMPONENT:** What all CIA components knew or intended.  
**DOCUMENTARY CORROBORATION:** Morales's trafficking conviction, aircraft/support relationship, continuing contacts described by OIG.  
**MOTIVE / POSSIBLE BIAS:** Factional and self-protective incentives.  
**CONTRADICTING SOURCE:** No written CIA permission; OIG disputes broader institutional inference.  
**CURRENT WEIGHT:** HIGH for arrangement, MEDIUM for the precise CIA response.

**WITNESS:** Gary Betzner  
**ROLE:** Pilot/participant in Contra support and trafficking milieu.  
**DATE OF ACCOUNT:** Kerry investigation.  
**FIRSTHAND OBSERVATION:** Claimed guns were unloaded at Hull's site and cocaine loaded for Florida.  
**HEARSAY COMPONENT:** Hull's/CIA's full knowledge and control.  
**DOCUMENTARY CORROBORATION:** Multiple witnesses linked Hull to cocaine; documented airstrips and Contra role.  
**MOTIVE / POSSIBLE BIAS:** Criminal exposure, cooperation benefit, notoriety.  
**CONTRADICTING SOURCE:** No manifest/independent loading record; only direct loading witness identified by report.  
**CURRENT WEIGHT:** MEDIUM; important but insufficient alone.

**WITNESS:** Werner Lotz  
**ROLE:** Costa Rican pilot/air-service owner; convicted in a U.S. drug case.  
**DATE OF ACCOUNT:** Kerry hearing.  
**FIRSTHAND OBSERVATION:** Described small-aircraft patterns in which weapons moved south and drugs north using war-zone airstrips.  
**HEARSAY COMPONENT:** CIA authorization and universal route practice.  
**DOCUMENTARY CORROBORATION:** Other aviation cases and protected-airstrip evidence.  
**MOTIVE / POSSIBLE BIAS:** Conviction/cooperation and personal involvement.  
**CONTRADICTING SOURCE:** Lack of a flight-by-flight record.  
**CURRENT WEIGHT:** MEDIUM-HIGH for route mechanism; not enough for institutional control.

**WITNESS:** Jeffrey Feldman  
**ROLE:** Assistant U.S. Attorney assigned to Miami Contra neutrality/weapons cases.  
**DATE OF ACCOUNT:** Iran/Contra deposition/Kerry investigation.  
**FIRSTHAND OBSERVATION:** Embassy resistance, internal DOJ handling, and his decision that officials were protecting Hull.  
**HEARSAY COMPONENT:** Ultimate motive of every official.  
**DOCUMENTARY CORROBORATION:** Meeting chronology and related FBI/investigative records.  
**MOTIVE / POSSIBLE BIAS:** Professional dispute and retrospective defense of investigation.  
**CONTRADICTING SOURCE:** Officials denied an agreement to undermine the inquiry.  
**CURRENT WEIGHT:** HIGH for experienced interference; MEDIUM-HIGH for protective motive.

**WITNESS:** CIA field and headquarters officers quoted in OIG Vol. II  
**ROLE:** Decision-makers managing Contra relationships/logistics.  
**DATE OF ACCOUNT:** OIG interviews, 1990s, concerning 1980s events.  
**FIRSTHAND OBSERVATION:** Operational priorities, reasons to retain services, vetting/reporting decisions.  
**HEARSAY COMPONENT:** Actual trafficking where not personally observed.  
**DOCUMENTARY CORROBORATION:** Cables, legal memoranda, continued-contract records.  
**MOTIVE / POSSIBLE BIAS:** Institutional/self-protection, faded memory.  
**CONTRADICTING SOURCE:** Some recollections conflict or minimize allegation credibility.  
**CURRENT WEIGHT:** HIGH for decision process; variable for underlying drug conduct.

## Weighting conclusion

The firsthand record is convergent on **carriage opportunity, agency knowledge of allied narcotics risk, operational retention, and enforcement conflict**. It is fragmented on **who knew the contents of a particular load** and weak on **a headquarters intent to route narcotics to the United States**. The appropriate synthesis is not to discard the witnesses because official inquiries rejected a broader charge, nor to treat every witness inference as documentary fact.
