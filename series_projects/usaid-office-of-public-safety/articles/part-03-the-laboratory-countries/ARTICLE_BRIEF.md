# Article brief

## Topic
The Laboratory Countries

## Central mystery
What capabilities did U.S. police assistance build inside recipient states, and what can be proved about their later political use?

## Preferred title, if any
THE LABORATORY COUNTRIES

## Scope
Historical/geographic boundaries and exclusions:
South Vietnam, 1955–62, as the pre-OPS prototype; Brazil, 1959–72, as the principal OPS test under authoritarian rule. Country catalogue, Uruguay and Mitrione are excluded.

## Key questions
- Which institutions received communications, records, identification, training and riot-control capacity?
- What transferred from MSU/ICA-era Vietnam into later AID practice?
- Which Brazilian uses are directly documented, strongly inferred or unproven?
- Did advisers control operations or build capabilities under local command?
