# Part 4 open leads

## 1. Operation 40 roster to later institutions

- **Question:** Which documented Operation 40 participants later held roles in Watergate, DEA-linked work, Central American security programs or Iran-Contra?
- **People:** E. Howard Hunt, Bernard Barker, Eugenio Martínez, Frank Sturgis, Félix Rodríguez and other alleged members.
- **Organizations/dates/places:** CIA Cuba project, 1960–61; Watergate, 1972; Central America, 1980s.
- **Existing evidence:** Bay of Pigs and Watergate records establish several individual careers; prior White Rabbit article supplies a provisional roster.
- **Missing evidence:** authenticated Operation 40 roster and role descriptions; personnel files; later agency/employer records.
- **Why it matters:** tests the requested Cuba → Watergate → Central America human chain.
- **Sources to search:** JFK files, CIA Reading Room, Watergate Special Prosecution Force, Walsh files.
- **Classification:** OPEN LEAD.
- **Next action:** build one identity packet per person; never infer membership from group photographs alone.
