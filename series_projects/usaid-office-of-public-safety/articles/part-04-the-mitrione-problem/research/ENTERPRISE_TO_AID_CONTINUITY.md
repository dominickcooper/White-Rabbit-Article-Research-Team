# Enterprise to AID continuity

## Baseline

The Enterprise was the private corporate, banking and aviation network associated with Richard Secord, Albert Hakim and Oliver North's Contra/Iran operations. NHAO was a State Department office administering congressionally authorized nonlethal Contra assistance. AID later administered distinct humanitarian phases through task forces.

## Exact government record

- GAO NSIAD-89-152: AID disbursed $17.7 million; separate DOD appropriations covered about $3.36 million in air, parachute/rigging, Honduran air cargo and trucking costs.
- GAO NSIAD-91-7: Public Law 101-14 transferred $49.75 million in unobligated DOD funds to AID for the 1989–90 phase.
- GAO's 1986 NHAO review found regional control weaknesses and inability to verify some delivery/end use.

## Judgment

The evidence establishes a **policy and functional handoff** from prohibited/private military support toward authorized overt humanitarian support. It does not yet establish that AID inherited the Enterprise's corporate network.

The “cleanup crew” metaphor is therefore partly supported: AID administered the politically and legally acceptable residue of sustaining the Resistance, including transport and communications. Whether it cleaned up with the same people and assets remains an open archival question.
