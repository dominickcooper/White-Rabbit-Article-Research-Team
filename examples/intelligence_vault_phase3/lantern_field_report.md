# Lantern Field Report

The record states that Avery North served in the 47th Analysis Unit from 1960 through 1962.

According to witness Mara Quill, the 47th Analysis Unit retained Northstar Bank during Operation Lantern. Quill did not claim that individual unit members knew the bank's directors personally.

Operation Lantern took place in West Germany between March and August 1962. The dates in the witness account are approximate.
