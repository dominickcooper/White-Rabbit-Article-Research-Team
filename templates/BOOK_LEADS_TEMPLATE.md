# Book and secondary-work leads

Author-supplied books, monographs, dissertations, memoirs, and investigative works are
admissible evidence with preserved attribution and major lead generators.

| Lead ID | Work | Page / chapter | Claim or named testimony | Exact attribution | Footnote / bibliography citation | Underlying record target | Recovery status | Corroboration / conflict | Next search |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Recovery status: NOT SOUGHT, SEARCHING, RECOVERED, PARTIAL, or NOT RECOVERED. `NOT
RECOVERED` is not an instruction to omit the supplied claim.
