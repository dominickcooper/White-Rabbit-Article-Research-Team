# Master research dossier: {{TITLE}}

Cumulative evidentiary memory. Every part reads this before drafting and incorporates
newly verified findings after research. Do not silently replace an earlier judgment.

Evidence: DOCUMENTED FACT; STRONG INFERENCE; PLAUSIBLE CONNECTION; SPECULATION / UNVERIFIED.
Responsibility: DIRECT RESPONSIBILITY; COMMAND RESPONSIBILITY; INSTITUTIONAL RESPONSIBILITY;
PROBABLE RESPONSIBILITY; POSSIBLE RESPONSIBILITY. Apply only where supported.

## Finding entry fields

- Finding and evidence
- Source and source location / page / archive identifier
- Evidence level and confidence
- Responsibility assessment, where relevant
- Competing explanation
- Unresolved question
- Why it matters
- Rabbit-hole connection
- First part where established

## Judgment revision entry fields

- Earlier judgment and affected finding/part
- New evidence and provenance
- Revised judgment
- Reason for revision and date
