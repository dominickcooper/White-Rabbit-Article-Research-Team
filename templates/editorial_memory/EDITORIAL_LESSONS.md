# Approved editorial lessons

Permanent lessons require explicit human promotion. Topic-specific edits stay in pending_learnings until reviewed.

## Seed lessons from Operation TWO-FOLD

- Strong research is insufficient when the article reads like a research report.
- Follow important people backward and forward across institutions; a Garland Williams-type career bridge can reveal the actual story.
- Present Tom Tripodi / Adrian Swain / Terry Baldwin-type chains as narrative sequences, not scattered biographical facts.
- Treat named participant testimony as evidence with accurate attribution; do not append a primary-document disclaimer reflexively.
- Preserve an Anthony Triponi-type reversal: apparently unbelievable claim → investigators check → unexpected confirmation → larger implication.
- Build “no dismissals from TWO-FOLD” from the contradiction with the program's stated anti-corruption purpose.
- Search prior installments for conceptual payoffs, not only repeated names and dates.
- The stronger Part 5 pattern was OPS gatekeepers → OPS disappearance → DEA expansion → CIA personnel/intelligence integration → prosecution-immunity problems, not a generic bureaucratic dispute.
- Once a point is established, compress. Strong transitions and reader orientation matter as much as factual completeness.
