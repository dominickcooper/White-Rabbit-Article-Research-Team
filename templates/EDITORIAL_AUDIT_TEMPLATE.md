## Evidence attribution and caveat density

Check both failure modes:

- OVERCLAIM: proximity, testimony, analogy, pattern, or inference is rewritten as direct fact.
- CAVEAT COLLAPSE: “does not prove” is repeated after each receipt until the cumulative
  pattern disappears.
- DEFENSIVE COLLAPSE: canon is repeatedly re-proven, accepted testimony is treated as
  unusable, inference resets at each edge, corroboration is spent only validating an
  accepted premise, or conventional explanations interrupt every finding.

Preferred repair: show the receipts → accumulate them → state the inference → give the
meaningful boundary once → keep moving.
