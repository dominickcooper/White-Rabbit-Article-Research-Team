# Factual and adversarial audit

## Direct factual claims

Does each cited or supplied source support the fact at the stated scope?

## Attributed source claims

Does the article preserve who said, recalled, alleged, or reported the claim, without
silently rewriting secondary or testimonial evidence as a primary record?

## Inferential claims and evidence convergence

Does the cumulative chain reasonably support the stated inference? Are independent
streams distinguished from derivative repetition? Do not downgrade a strong inference
solely because no smoking-gun memorandum exists.

## Connection chains

Are all edges visible, classified, sourced, and dependency-aware? Are weak or broken edges
shown rather than hidden by stronger neighboring evidence?

## Historical analogy and continuity

Is precedent guiding research without being confused with proof of recurrence, direct
continuity, or common control?

## Disconfirmation and Zebra adjudication

Was the strongest alternative tested after the strongest case was assembled? Could Horse,
Hybrid, Unresolved, or Contradicted still win where the record requires it?
