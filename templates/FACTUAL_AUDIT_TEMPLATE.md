# Factual and adversarial audit

Audit against the three source-authority levels. Preserve provenance while detecting both
OVERCLAIM and DEFENSIVE COLLAPSE.

The auditor issues targeted findings rather than a replacement draft. Each finding must
include exact passage/line, problem, supporting evidence, required factual constraint,
and suggested minimum correction. The writer applies the surgical edit; this auditor then
rechecks it against the complete research record.

## Source Thesis and auditor authority

The auditor may identify contradiction or thesis drift but cannot silently rewrite the
locked investigation. A fundamental change requires AUTHOR THESIS DECISION REQUIRED.

## Published White Rabbit canon

Was published canon used without unnecessary re-verification or silent weakening? If new
evidence genuinely conflicts, record EXISTING CANON, NEW CONTRADICTORY EVIDENCE, WHY THEY
CONFLICT, and WHETHER AUTHOR REVIEW IS NEEDED.

## Author-supplied corpus and accepted testimony

Was supplied material used without a second permission gate? Did accepted testimony
become a downstream premise while remaining labeled as testimony internally?

## Newly discovered external material

Did Level 3 material receive ordinary verification, comparison, and provenance review?

## Direct factual claims

Does each cited or supplied source support the fact at the stated scope?

## Attributed source claims

Does the article preserve who said, recalled, alleged, or reported the claim, without
silently rewriting secondary or testimonial evidence as a primary record?

## Inferential claims and evidence convergence

Does the cumulative chain reasonably support the stated inference? Are independent
streams distinguished from derivative repetition? Do not downgrade a strong inference
solely because no smoking-gun memorandum exists.

## Connection chains

Are all edges visible, classified, sourced, and dependency-aware? Are weak or broken edges
shown rather than hidden by stronger neighboring evidence? Did accepted edges move forward
instead of resetting to zero? Did corroborating facts open new branches rather than being
spent only re-proving an accepted premise?

## Historical analogy and continuity

Is precedent guiding research without being confused with proof of recurrence, direct
continuity, or common control?

## Disconfirmation and Zebra adjudication

Was the strongest alternative tested after the strongest case was assembled? Could Horse,
Hybrid, Unresolved, or Contradicted still win where the record requires it?

## Dossier-to-article and section source coverage

Create a consequential-claim matrix: article passage/section, claim, reader-facing source
or traced linked source, source type, direct support at stated scope, and action. A valid
CSV is not proof of adequacy. Distinguish original records genuinely unavailable from
records the workflow simply neglected to link.

## Primary-source escalation and bibliography recovery

Prefer specific government documents, testimony, hearings, court/financial records,
official correspondence, document IDs, dates and page locators where available. Do not
replace an attributed interpretation with a primary document that does not make it.

## Opposite-failure check

- OVERCLAIM: fabrication; source-type conversion; direct documentation claimed where only
  inference exists; invented relationships; unsupported assignment of a specific act.
- DEFENSIVE COLLAPSE: excessive caveats; repeated canon verification; accepted testimony
  treated as unusable; inference reset at each edge; corroboration used only to validate an
  earlier premise; conventional explanations inserted after every finding; or prose
  weakened below the selected authority level.
