## Thesis substitution

Compare Author brief → Source Thesis → Story Decision → article. A fundamental shift
without author approval is a failure, not a stylistic preference.

## Gold-article comparison

Compare actual passages against at least three relevant approved full-body Gold articles
where available: narrator presence, paragraph distribution, discoveries, transitions,
observations, section movement, and ending. Loading a style prompt is not evidence.
