# Connection report

## Most consequential documented connections

- ZR/RIFLE's assassination purpose, QJWIN's criminal recruitment, Harvey's Roselli/Castro
  channel and Helms's special accounting authority meet inside one tightly held file set.
- The project produced domestic operational expenses in April 1963: Miami/Perine rooms
  and calls, a Wallston receipt and an Islamorada boat, all charged to QJWIN/ZR/RIFLE.
- Harvey's termination was explicitly incomplete: one “precautionary lifeline” remained,
  and QJWIN's relationship/payments continued into 1964.
- QJWIN and WIROGUE occupied the same Congo operational field, but the strongest official
  review did not establish their role in Lumumba's capture or death.

Leopoldville, Nov.–Dec. 1960: withheld high-risk QJWIN task plus WIROGUE offer. Italy,

domestic operational event in the reviewed ZR/RIFLE file and deserve narrative priority.

## Evidence convergence

Independent documentary streams converge on capability plus concealment: project design,
special accounting, criminal recruitment, technical support, Mafia access, broad Cuba
authority, domestic operational spending and qualified termination. Together they show a
usable apparatus and a built-in records problem. The Hunt and Furiati materials share
many names and may be derivative; they are not counted as two independent proofs.

the Roselli card opens aliases and travel; the Florida vouchers open phone/hotel/marina
records; the lifeline opens successor files; QJWIN retention opens post-termination cable
and payment records.
