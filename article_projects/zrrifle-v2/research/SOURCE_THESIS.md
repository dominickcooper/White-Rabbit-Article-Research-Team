## Principal source-derived thesis

The selected corpus directs an investigation into whether **ZR/RIFLE was a real,
compartmented CIA assassination capability whose recruiting market, criminal
intermediaries, Cuba operations, Congo personnel, financial controls, and residual
infrastructure intersected the people and operational network alleged to have been used
against President Kennedy**. The corpus does not present Dallas as an incidental add-on:
Furiati argues that the anti-Castro machinery was redirected, while E. Howard Hunt's late
account names Harvey, Morales, Sturgis, Cord Meyer, David Atlee Phillips, and Antonio
Veciana in an “off the board” plot and describes Hunt as a “benchwarmer.” Those are
ACCEPTED TESTIMONY and author-supplied secondary allegations, not authenticated orders;
they nevertheless define people, meetings, places, chains, and footprints to investigate.

The documentary core already establishes important architecture beneath that allegation:
Harvey controlled a nonattributable capability; Helms authorized QJWIN and other agents;
QJWIN was recruited for a high-risk, one-shot Congo mission; he met WIROGUE in
Leopoldville; ZR/RIFLE later carried domestic Miami/Islamorada expenses; a Roselli alias
appeared inside its project file; and Harvey preserved one “precautionary lifeline” after
terminating most aspects. The investigation must determine whether these are one
operational continuum, partially overlapping compartments, or separate programs whose
proximity has been overstated.

## Supporting source-derived subtheories

1. **Dual-use compartment:** the real burglary/safecracking program supplied productive
   cover, recruiters, legends, access, and deniable personnel for an executive-action
   capability rather than merely sharing a coincidental cryptonym.
2. **Congo proving ground:** QJWIN's November 1960 recruitment, withheld requirements,
   personal-risk warning, one-shot compensation, urgent Leopoldville travel, and proximity
   to active Lumumba planning may mark an assassination-related mission; WIROGUE's
   execution-squad offer and “action” profile may expose a parallel asset market.
3. **Cuba operationalization:** Harvey's control of the Roselli/Mafia Castro channel and
   Task Force W may have converted a standby capability into live operations, with
   ZR/RIFLE accounting and personnel spanning nominal project boundaries.
4. **Miami logistics:** April 13–21, 1963 Miami/Perine hotel calls, John A. Wallston's
   hotel bill, and a $75 operational boat charter at Islamorada may identify a specific
   Cuba or other covert activity and additional personnel.
5. **Residual capability:** the June 1963 “precautionary lifeline” may denote retained
   access, an asset, communications, finance, or an emergency route after nominal
   termination; its documentary and personnel trail should be recoverable in adjacent
   files even if the project file is incomplete.
6. **Dallas network:** the Hunt and Furiati accounts assert overlapping CIA, anti-Castro,
   organized-crime, Mexico City, New Orleans, Miami, and Dallas participants. Some links
   are separately documented, but the operational bridge to Kennedy's murder must be
   reconstructed rather than assumed or dismissed wholesale.
7. **Post-operation concealment:** compartmentation, destroyed/minimized papers,
   certification accounting, aliases, false commercial purpose, and selective disclosure
   were features of the capability before Dallas; later investigative impairment may be
   a consequence of that design, whether or not the capability was used against Kennedy.

## Genuine contradictions already inside the supplied corpus

- The project files describe real burglary/safecracking work; the same file contains an
  executive-action outline, Helms's open-ended agent authorization, and a Roselli alias.
- Cargill found QJWIN's high-risk one-shot Congo payment compatible with an assassination
  mission but also documented prolonged burglary recruitment; the two functions may be
  cover, sequential work, or separate assignments.
- QJWIN reportedly rejected WIROGUE's execution-squad offer, while later cables planned
  continued Congo use; this cuts against treating QJWIN as a willing killer yet keeps his
  original mission unexplained.
- Harvey denied recruiting assassins/targets, while oversight bodies described a standby
  assassination capability and linked his Roselli/Castro operation to ZR/RIFLE.
- Hunt's late account conflicts with his earlier denials and varies across versions; its
  named network overlaps documented relationships but the exact meeting and command chain
  are not independently established inside the supplied corpus.
- Furiati often presents Cuban intelligence conclusions and derivative witness accounts
  as a unified operational story; the independence, dates, and original records behind
  several claims need separation.
