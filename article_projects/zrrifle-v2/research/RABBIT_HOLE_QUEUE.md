Active branches may be marked ACTIVE. A completed high-value branch must use DEVELOPED,
CONTRADICTED, EXHAUSTED, DEFERRED, or BLOCKED. One failed keyword search never establishes
EXHAUSTED. Record what was searched, what was read, what emerged, and why work stopped.
Prioritize approximately three to five high-value branches per article, adjusted to scope;
use a documented stopping rule rather than endless research or premature closure.

Published canon, accepted testimony, a documented record, or a corroborated inference may
support the next research question; it does not prove that next edge. Keep every edge's
evidence classification visible. Prioritize PERSON → EMPLOYER / FAMILY / INVESTOR / BOARD /
INTELLIGENCE / CONTRACT / FOUNDATION / BANK / POLITICAL FIGURE / ORGANIZED CRIME;
COMPANY → CONTRACTOR / SUBCONTRACTOR / INVESTOR / SHARED COUNSEL; PROGRAM → PERSONNEL /
CONTRACTOR / RECIPIENT; RECIPIENT → OTHER PROGRAM; and TESTIMONY → NAMED PERSON / PROGRAM /
PLACE / EVENT. Do not spend the queue rechecking whether an accepted starting node exists.
