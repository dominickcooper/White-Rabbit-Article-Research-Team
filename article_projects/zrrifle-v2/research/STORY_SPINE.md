# Story spine — Stage 2A editorial reconstruction

Status: SHOWRUNNER PROPOSAL — AUTHOR APPROVAL REQUIRED — DRAFTING NOT STARTED
Source Thesis: version 1, unchanged. Research is approved for story development.
This is a new narrative architecture, not a draft or a polishing pass on the Stage 1 outline.

## Governing mystery

How far can we follow the assassination capability assembled around William Harvey and
the anti-Castro operations into the people, meetings and division of labor that Hunt and
Furiati describe in the plot against President Kennedy?

The practical questions are who could recruit, authorize, pay, move and protect the
people in those accounts—and what those people were doing during 1963. Florida and the
lifeline are investigative doors into that question, not replacement subjects.

## Opening and immediate promise

Open inside the surviving file review, with its description of Harvey's April 13–21,
1963 South Florida expenses: rooms and telephone calls, another man's hotel bill and a
$75 operational boat at Islamorada, charged to QJWIN/ZR/RIFLE. Identify the source as
Cargill's May 30, 1975 review of the expense package. Do not stage a scene aboard the
boat or suggest we possess the original vouchers.

Before leaving the opening movement, tell the reader why this account matters to Kennedy:
Hunt described a 1963 Miami solicitation involving Morales, Sturgis and Harvey, with
Morales attributing authorization upward to Johnson through Meyer. Furiati describes
Phillips taking over ZR/RIFLE after Harvey's removal from the anti-Cuba center, and
Escalante describes five anti-Castro/criminal sectors fusing from late 1962.

These are distinct attributed accounts. They supply a proposed mechanism and names to
follow immediately. The April trip is not assigned to that meeting, and its purpose is
not labeled Dallas. The reader should know the Kennedy stakes within the first narrative
movement, before any extended institutional history.

## Reveal ladder

### 1. The Kennedy allegation has an address book

**Receipt:** Hunt, Chapter Eight; Furiati, pp. 130–132; Escalante interview, pp. 147–148.
Start with the solicitation, claimed chain and alleged reassembly of personnel. Keep the
opening cast manageable: Harvey, Hunt, Morales/Sturgis, then Phillips. Introduce Johnson
and Meyer as the alleged authorization chain, not as documentary findings.

**Change in understanding:** This is a claim about recruiting and repurposing an existing
apparatus, not merely a claim that several men disliked Kennedy.
**Next question:** What machinery did Harvey actually control, and who gave it to him?
The full Furiati roster and the differences between Hunt's versions wait until their
functions become relevant.

### 2. An assassination capability with room to recruit

**Receipts:** Executive-action project notes, Helms's authorization, QJWIN contract and
recruiting records; Church reconstruction (Writer Packet D1–D3).
Show nonattribution, lethal purpose, certification accounting and authority for additional
agents/subagents. Explain the real burglary and code-theft recruitment in ordinary
language: criminal access was useful for more than one sort of assignment.

**Change:** Harvey was more than a conspicuous name in a late allegation. The records
give him an actual recruitment and support mechanism. Exceptional authority is specific;
it is not an unlimited budget or presidential authorization.
**Next:** Who was the recruiter, and why had he already been sent into Congo?

### 3. The recruit did not know the whole job

**Receipts:** November 2, 1960 dispatch, subsequent completion/payment account, December
WIROGUE offer; QJWIN's identification as José Marie André Mankel.
Follow a person: the risk warning, withheld requirements, urgent Leopoldville assignment,
then the encounter with another action asset offering an execution-squad job.

**Change:** Compartmentation operated inside the recruitment itself. QJWIN's refusal of
WIROGUE's offer is a concrete event, not a solution to his original assignment.
**Necessary distinction:** The committee's inability to substantiate Lumumba participation
is non-substantiation. A later official denial is contrary testimony, with its own scope.
Neither supplies the withheld instructions. Do not call a completed Lumumba role proved,
or the original mission disproved.
**Return to Kennedy:** This branch establishes how personnel could receive narrow tasks
without knowing the entire operation. It does not turn a Congo deployment into Dallas
participation. Return through Harvey's recruiting apparatus; do not detour into a full
Lumumba history.

### 4. The underworld connection became an operation

**Receipts:** Harvey's handoff of the Roselli Castro channel, poison/arms chronology,
Task Force W records, the Ralston alias card; Bissell and Helms on the relationship
between the two operations.
Follow Roselli into the criminal-syndicate Castro channel and identify the delivery
intermediaries when the source supports them. Furiati's account of Tony Varona gives a
named CIA/exile/Mafia liaison to examine, not an interchangeable crowd of conspirators.

**Change:** The criminal network had an operational function in attempts against a
specific leader. The same officer and a Roselli alias connect that history to the
ZR/RIFLE file.
**Contradiction here:** Bissell said the operations became one; Helms said their lines
never crossed. Show what each was describing and what the records actually share.
**Next:** When Furiati says this personnel field was redirected, what precisely does she
claim happened?

### 5. A proposed reorganization, not just recurring names

**Receipts:** Furiati pp. 130–136; Escalante interview pp. 142–148; Hunt's attributed
memo as reproduced in Eric Hamburg's afterword.
Expand the opening allegation into functions:
- Phillips succeeds Harvey in Furiati's account.
- Escalante dates the fusion of five sectors to late 1962: brigade secret police, Nino
  Diaz's detachment, Panama-trained special-missions personnel, exile organizations,
  and ZR/RIFLE/Mafia contractors.
- His spring-1963 New Orleans planning claim names Roselli, Cabell, Sturgis and Hemmings;
  his Ruby/Sturgis two-group claim expressly draws on *Double Cross*.
- Hunt's reproduced memo alleges a Miami plan shifted to Dallas and says Sturgis wanted
  Hunt's help with a cover-up. This is more specific than the son's oral-account narrative.

**Change:** The allegation now has claimed recruitment pools, organizers, locations and
jobs. It can be followed beyond a list of acquaintances.
**Productive tensions:** Furiati's Phillips succession must be considered alongside
Harvey's April expenses and June termination memo; a task transfer and retained accounting
could coexist, but that explanation is not a discovered fact. Hunt's Johnson/Meyer chain
and Furiati's Helms-centered account are not silently fused. Hunt's April 2005 reversal
belongs here, briefly and specifically, alongside the differences in his accounts.
**Next:** Did the alleged operation also require a story about who killed Kennedy?

### 6. The proposed operation includes the blame

**Receipts:** Veciana/Bishop account as relayed by Furiati, pp. 132 and 143–144.
Follow the reported September 1963 Dallas encounter involving Bishop and Oswald, then
the reported approach to recruit Guillermo Ruiz to support an Oswald/Cuba story.
Separate Veciana's reported observations from Escalante's inference that the earlier
encounter deliberately prepared the later operation to blame Cuba.

**Change:** In this account, the alleged network's work extends to attribution and
political consequences. Phillips's claimed function matters more than another familiar
name appearing.
**Bounded side branch:** Lorenz's specific November itinerary and Ruby visit can enter
here only briefly. Furiati herself reports the committee's inconsistency finding.
Do not stage the trip as fact or make it carry the whole logistics case.
**Next:** What kind of operational trail would this machinery leave?

### 7. The paperwork was being restricted before Dallas

**Receipt:** Harvey's August 14, 1962 MONGOOSE memorandum requesting removal of
“liquidation of leaders” from the written record (FRUS supplement, document 290).
Place this beside the already-established compartmentation and accounting—not in a
separate generalized CIA-secrecy essay.

**Change:** An incomplete operational paper trail has a documented institutional context.
That helps explain why money, aliases and travel deserve attention. It does not establish
the content of an absent order.
**Return:** Go back to the Florida expense package with the people and mechanisms now
in view.

### 8. Read the Florida bill a second time

**Receipts:** Cargill review, Wallston entry, Ralston card, June 27 termination language.
Now unpack the other man's name, malformed Wilshire address, hotel calls and boat charge.
Roselli is a plausible identity for Wallston because the alias and operational relationship
converge; the name/address discrepancy remains meaningful.

Then reveal the June termination exception: one precautionary lifeline and Knight's
familiarity with it. Place QJWIN's later relationship/payments on a separate chronological
track. They do not identify the lifeline or establish its operation on November 22.

**Change:** The opening bill is now a recoverable personnel-and-logistics problem inside
an identified apparatus. The closing memo leaves a specific retained function to find.
**Alternative with real explanatory power:** Cuba work and cover/termination obligations
could explain the spending and later payments. It does not identify Wallston, the boat's
assignment or the lifeline. The Kennedy accounts give additional names and dates against
which those records can be tested; proximity alone does not choose the target.

### 9. The payoff: a capability whose uses can be traced

Accumulate what the investigation has actually gained: authority to build a deniable
assassination capability; access to criminal recruits; a concealed Congo assignment;
an operational Mafia/Castro channel; deliberate restriction of written discussion;
Florida spending in 1963; and an expressly qualified termination.

The meaningful inference is that assassination capacity could reside in retained access,
intermediaries and support arrangements, rather than requiring an intact public-facing
organization. The combination makes personnel, payments and assignments the right unit
of investigation. It does not identify all these arrangements as one surviving Dallas team.

End on two converging searches, not a verdict refrain:
1. Who and what did the April phone, hotel and boat expenses support?
2. What did Knight know the lifeline preserved, and do either set of records intersect
   the specific participants and functions in Hunt's and Furiati's accounts?

Those questions seek records capable of distinguishing operations, not a mythical single
memo confessing the entire assassination. No promise that missing records necessarily
survive or will answer every question.

## Character and tangent discipline

Harvey carries the story; Roselli makes criminal access operational; Hunt supplies the
early solicitation; Phillips carries the alleged reorganization and attribution operation;
QJWIN exposes concealed tasking; Knight opens the final records search. Introduce other
names when they perform a job. Defer Jerry Soul's full Congo career, adjacent ZR crypts,
the complete Operation 40 history, Mary Meyer's murder, shooter/ballistics debates and
later regime-change biographies. These remain preserved research, not rejected evidence.

## Voice and visual direction

Use the narrator to explain what drew attention, what changed on a second reading and
which person the next document led to. No invented reporting scenes, dialogue or personal
access. Favor connected conversational paragraphs; let occasional dry humor emerge from
bureaucratic behavior. Do not write a hearing summary, stack theatrical one-liners, or
repeat a “not X, but Y” correction after each discovery.

Gold behaviors and exact reading targets are in the Writer Packet. Any later visual
should clarify one relation: an actual document crop of the file review, a sourced
timeline separating documentary dates from alleged events, or separate attributed
network diagrams. Never render an alleged command chain as an unqualified organization
chart. No visual assets are commissioned in this stage.
