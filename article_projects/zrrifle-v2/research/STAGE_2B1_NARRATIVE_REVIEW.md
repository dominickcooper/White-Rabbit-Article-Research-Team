## Main weaknesses in the incoming draft

1. **Procedural commentary interrupted movement.** The draft explained that a chart was
   not recovered, a repetition was not independent, or a connection was worth pursuing
   after attribution had already done most of that work.
2. **The people kept becoming categories.** Phrases such as “the concrete mechanism
   underneath it” and “the function it assigns Hunt” sounded like an editor describing
   the planned story.
3. **First person sometimes contributed no inference.** “I keep coming back” and “I would
   like to know” largely announced curiosity. The replacement passages show the
   consequence of the missing instruction or possible identity.
4. **The JFK account arrived in pieces.** The reader got rosters, source provenance and
   caveats more readily than the claimed succession of organizing, planning, recruiting,
   moving the proposed location and shaping blame.
5. **The conclusion shrank to a file-search question.** Knight had become the destination
   of an investigation whose governing question was Kennedy. The larger operational
   finding needed to carry the ending.
