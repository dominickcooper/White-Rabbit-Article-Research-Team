## Dependency finding

Chains C4 and C5 are the strongest new operational chains because their receipts are
contemporaneous and internal. C6 is narratively explosive but evidentially weakest at its
core. C7 and C10 open useful searches but are canon-premised rather than independent proof.
C2 is the clearest example of a chain whose neighboring edges are strong while its target
edge remains broken.
