# Extreme-Thesis ledger

Protocol status: ACTIVATED by brief. This ledger deliberately builds the strongest
version, then subjects it to disconfirmation and Zebra adjudication.

## Maximum thesis

ZR/RIFLE was not merely a shelved CIA assassination plan. It created a compartmented,
financed and field-tested network of criminal recruiters, Mafia intermediaries, technical
support and anti-Castro operators; in 1963 Harvey used that compartment in Florida,
retained a “lifeline,” and the same network was redirected into the operation against
Kennedy.

## Strongest surviving formulation

The CIA unquestionably built a deniable executive-action capability under ZR/RIFLE,
connected it to criminal recruitment and the Castro machinery, charged an unexplained
Florida operation to it in 1963, and terminated it with an unnamed “lifeline” still in
place. Those facts make the alleged Kennedy connection a legitimate operational inquiry,
but the reviewed record has not yet supplied the final command, personnel or logistics
edge from the capability to Dallas.

## Why this is not a retreat

The surviving formulation is stronger than a vague “questions remain” thesis because it
states the proven architecture plainly and isolates the precise missing edge. It rejects
two overclaims—completed Congo assassination role and proved Dallas deployment—without
substituting the unrelated withholding story the author excluded.
