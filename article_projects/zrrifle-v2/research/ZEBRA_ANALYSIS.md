# Zebra analysis

## The zebra claim

The unlikely explanation is that an apparatus openly designed around nonattribution,
criminal recruitment and executive action was preserved after nominal termination and
redirected against a U.S. president through the same anti-Castro/Mafia personnel field.

## Ordinary explanation

ZR/RIFLE combined a real burglary/recruiting program with contingency planning for
foreign executive action. QJWIN's Congo task and WIROGUE's freelancing occurred in the
same dangerous station but did not produce a joint assassination. Harvey's 1963 Florida
expenses concerned a Cuba operation or administrative closeout; the “lifeline” was an
emergency contact, cover or financial cleanup. Later witnesses assembled documented but
separate Cuba relationships into a Dallas story.

## Adjudication

Zebra status: NOT PROVED, NOT DISMISSED. The ordinary explanation currently accounts for
more of the specific record with fewer unsupported edges. It does not explain the Florida
operation or lifeline because those events remain unlabeled, not because the zebra claim
has filled the gap. The correct editorial posture is aggressive investigation of those
two discriminators while refusing to let secrecy itself stand in for proof.

## Story use

Do not open with the zebra as a conclusion. Open with the contemporaneous receipt, build
the proven capability, show the official contradictions, then present the Kennedy claim
as a test that the file can partly structure but not yet close.
