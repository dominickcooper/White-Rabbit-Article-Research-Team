## Cumulative payoff

The records establish an operational architecture: authority, recruited criminal access,
a live Castro channel, selective documentation, 1963 logistical spending and a retained
exception at termination. It is reasonable to investigate continuity through people,
contacts and support arrangements instead of assuming a program's name defines the full
capability or its end.

This positive finding gives the Kennedy accounts concrete mechanisms to investigate.
The remaining uncertainty is specific: which people performed which functions on the
relevant dates, and what operation those arrangements supported. An unidentified target
is not a disproved target. Nor is a documented capability an attribution of Kennedy's
murder. Neither formula should become the article's recurring refrain.

The strongest alternative explanation—Cuba operations plus cover/termination obligations—
accounts for important parts of the documentary trail. It does not identify every
expense or retained function. Records of those functions could distinguish that account
from the proposed redirection without requiring one all-encompassing confession memo.
