# Network convergence

## Shared nodes reached by independent paths

### William Harvey

Reached through the ZR/RIFLE project papers, the QJWIN authorization, the Roselli/Castro
operation, Task Force W/FRUS, the April 1963 Florida vouchers, the June 1963 lifeline memo,
and Hunt's testimony. The first five paths are documentary; Hunt's command-chain path is
accepted testimony. Convergence proves Harvey controlled multiple relevant capabilities
and relationships. It does not prove he ordered Kennedy's murder.

### Miami / South Florida

Reached through documented Task Force W/JMWAVE activity, the Roselli Castro phase, the
April 1963 ZR/RIFLE expenses, the Islamorada boat, and Hunt/Furiati witness accounts.
Geographic convergence makes the voucher branch high value. Miami was also a dense hub of
ordinary anti-Castro activity, so geography alone has low discriminatory power.

### Criminal and extra-legal personnel markets

Reached through QJWIN's European criminal contacts, WIROGUE's action profile, Roselli and
the Mafia channel, and Operation 40 canon. The repeated operational requirement—people
who could act without attribution—is documented. A shared labor market is more plausible
than a single unified organization; the actors and assignments must still be linked.

### Congo ↔ Cuba

Reached first through QJWIN/WIROGUE and then independently through White Rabbit canon's
Jerry Soul bridge. This is the investigation's most important newly opened convergence:
the same era's Cuban exile handlers and CIA-backed Cuban personnel may connect the Cuba
and Congo operational theaters. Primary personnel files are still missing, so the
connection remains a research priority rather than a finished finding.

### Residual capability after formal endings

Reached through Harvey's qualified ZR/RIFLE termination and QJWIN retention, then through
the independently published TWO-FOLD/Handoff pattern. The common signature is not proof
of institutional continuity; it supplies a test: follow people, money, authorizations and
communications after the announced end.

## Conclusion

The network is not empty and not yet a closed conspiracy graph. It contains a documented
capability core, several real personnel and logistics overlaps, two exceptionally
important missing labels (the Florida operation and the lifeline), and a weak final edge
to Dallas. That shape should control the story.
