## Cumulative result

The documentary evidence proves capability, concealment design, criminal recruitment,
Congo/Cuba intersections, anomalous domestic Florida spending, and residual continuity.
It does not close the final edge from that infrastructure to Kennedy's murder. The
article's legitimate investigation is therefore not “did CIA possess such a capability?”
but “what exactly survived, who could access it, and what independent footprint would
show redirection?”
