# Thesis reduction

## Starting maximum claim

ZR/RIFLE created the team and mechanism later used to kill President Kennedy.

## Reductions forced by evidence

1. **“The team” → “a compartmented capability and overlapping asset markets.”** The
   record shows QJWIN, other possible agents, WIROGUE, Roselli and Cuba operators, but not
   one stable roster.
2. **“Proven in Congo” → “recruited and deployed amid Congo assassination planning.”**
   QJWIN refused WIROGUE's offer and official reviews did not substantiate a Lumumba role.
3. **“Activated in Florida” → “charged an unexplained Florida operation to the project.”**
   The vouchers are direct; the purpose and participants are not.
4. **“Preserved a kill team” → “preserved an unnamed lifeline.”** The exception is direct;
   its function is unknown.
5. **“Used against Kennedy” → “intersects the personnel, geography and methods named in
   the allegation, but lacks the final command/logistics edge.”**

## Final surviving thesis

ZR/RIFLE was a real CIA executive-action capability, not folklore: it joined deniable
personnel, criminal recruiting, exceptional accounting, technical support and the Castro
machinery. Its files then record an unexplained April 1963 Florida operation and a June
termination that left one “precautionary lifeline.” Those are the strongest documentary
reasons to investigate a Kennedy connection. They establish capability and unresolved
continuity, not deployment in Dallas.
