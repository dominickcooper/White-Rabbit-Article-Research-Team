## Narrator presence

The narrator should be visibly investigating: noticing the wrong account label, stopping
at the failed Congo edge, returning to the Florida receipts and naming what a file would
have to show. Avoid impersonal audit prose.

## Paragraph rhythm and short-paragraph use

Alternate receipt-dense medium paragraphs with one-sentence turns at discoveries. Use
short paragraphs for genuine reversals (“QJWIN said no”) and not as automatic emphasis
after every fact.

## Reveal pacing and transitions

Documents should create the next question. The structure is receipt → file identity →
capability → Congo constraint → Cuba activation → Florida return → lifeline → Dallas test.
Do not group all caveats in a defensive section.

## Rhetorical questions

Use sparingly and specifically: Who was Wallston? What did Knight understand by lifeline?
Where did the phone calls go? Avoid generic “coincidence?” questions.

## Blunt transitions, reactions, humor and sarcasm

Blunt reactions belong at true anomalies—the foreign-action account paying for a Florida
boat, or the project being “terminated” with an exception. Sarcasm should not target
witnesses or substitute for evidence.

## Personal theory and “wait a minute” moments

The author's theory can be stated after the receipts: the meaningful object may be a
portable capability rather than a fixed squad. Mark it as theory and immediately specify
the discriminatory record.

## Quotes and uncertainty

Quote only high-value primary language: “large element of personal risk,” “execution
squad,” and “precautionary lifeline.” Paraphrase long reports. Attach uncertainty to the
specific weak edge, not to the whole paragraph.

## Callbacks and section openings

Open sections on an object, person or contradiction rather than a topic label. Callback to
the Florida boat after the Congo/Cuba build; callback to QJWIN's refusal when testing the
Hunt chain, reminding the reader that proximity is not performance.

## Conclusion mechanics

End on the named record target—Knight's understanding plus phone/boat records—not a thesis
recap or generic appeal for transparency.

## How sections open the next rabbit hole

Every section ends with one concrete edge: alias → hotel record; Congo task → 201 file;
Castro merger dispute → Helms reports; termination → finance; Hunt testimony → travel and
communications.
