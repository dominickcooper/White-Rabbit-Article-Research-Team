## Search branches and negative results

- Exact searches for “precautionary lifeline” produced only repetitions of the June 27
  memorandum; no document identifying the retained asset, route, account or person was
  found.
- “John A. Wallston” resolved to the project receipt and a likely John Ralston/Roselli
  alias interpretation. The supplied record itself lists `56510 Wilshire Boulevard`, an
  address string that is malformed or OCR-corrupted. No independent hotel, phone or boat
  record was found in the online pass.
- ZRACORN appears repeatedly in QJWIN traffic. ZRRAGTIME appears in retrospective notes.
  Harvey could not recall whether they were projects or generic/control labels. Their
  function remains unresolved.
- Searches for Jerry Soul's Operation 40/Congo role did not yield a primary personnel
  record in the online official collections. The canon-opened edge remains deferred.
- Official online material did not corroborate Hunt's late “benchwarmer” command chain.
  It did corroborate some surrounding relationships and preserved official counterclaims.
