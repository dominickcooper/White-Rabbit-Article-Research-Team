## Findings and minimum corrections

### CRITICAL C-1 — QJWIN's Congo activity was described too narrowly

**Incoming passage:** “Later records treated the one-shot assignment as completed, yet
the review still couldn't explain what he had originally been sent to do”; later, “None
of those accounts supplies the instructions O'Donnell withheld.”

**Problem:** FRUS 1964–68, volume XXIII, document 46 reproduces a November 29, 1960
station cable. It says QJWIN and a local contact planned to enter a redacted target's
home using UN-marked vehicles and remove him under escort. After the target moved,
QJWIN proposed going to Stanleyville and executing the plan himself. Headquarters was
prepared to consider “direct action,” subject to a security assessment. The footnote
states that the language could be interpreted as an assassination order while preserving
the Church Committee's conclusion that there was no clear evidence QJWIN actually joined
an assassination plan or attempt.

**Constraint:** The target remains redacted in the published cable; “execute the plan”
does not by itself mean execute the person; the committee did not prove a Lumumba
assassination role; the final task and outcome remain undisclosed.

**Correction applied:** Added the removal/direct-action cable and changed the summary to
say that the files disclose a direct-action proposal against a redacted target but not
the target, final task or outcome. The FAQ was reconciled to the same distinction.

### IMPORTANT I-1 — WIROGUE's execution-squad offer lacked the station's contrary account

**Incoming passage:** WIROGUE's preparation and offer were stated, followed later by the
committee's inability to substantiate a Lumumba role.

**Problem:** The offer is documented through QJWIN's report, but FRUS document 50 also
records the station's concern that WIROGUE was “free wheeling.” The chief of station later
testified that he had not authorized the approach or tasked WIROGUE to form an execution
squad. Omitting this evidence could make an asset's reported offer read like recovered
station tasking.

**Constraint:** The contrary testimony does not erase the offer or WIROGUE's documented
paramilitary preparation. It limits the inference about official authorization.

**Correction applied:** Added the station's freewheeling/unauthorized account in the same
paragraph, without turning it into a verdict on QJWIN's separate mission.

### IMPORTANT I-2 — Major documentary claims required granular reader-facing links

**Problem:** The accepted draft relied mainly on the Cargill review and the full Church
report while leaving the Helms authorization, QJWIN identity, Congo cables, Task Force W,
Harvey record-restriction memorandum and 2025 lifeline release unlinked.

**Correction applied:** Added authoritative first-use links to NARA and FRUS and created
`sources.csv` after prose stabilization. No private-source URL was fabricated for Hunt or
Furiati; their chapter/page provenance remains in the article source note and this audit.

### MINOR M-1 — A source discrepancy was attributed too broadly

**Incoming passage:** “Hunt's versions leave different answers” about whether Hunt and
Morales had met before.

**Problem:** Chapter Eight attributes the no-prior-meeting claim to Hunt, while the
Guatemala statement is Hamburg's afterword narration. Calling both “Hunt's versions”
blurred the dependency.

**Correction applied:** “The book leaves different answers.”

### MINOR M-2 — Lifeline link destination

**Problem:** The same 2023 Cargill URL was linked twice although the full research record
identified a 2025 NARA release containing the exact lifeline and Ralston material.

**Correction applied:** The first Cargill link remains on the opening expense review; the
lifeline passage now links the 2025 release once.

## Remaining limitations

- The original QJWIN target is redacted in the published November 29 cable. The record
  does not establish that “direct action” meant assassination, that the plan was executed,
  or that the target was Lumumba.
- No underlying Florida voucher set, telephone destinations, hotel folio, boat operator,
  passenger manifest or normalized Wallston address was recovered.
- Wallston/Ralston remains plausible, not documented identity.
- The lifeline's referent, duration and use remain unknown. Knight's briefing is named but
  not recovered.
- Hunt's safe-house meeting, command chain and itinerary shift lack contemporaneous
  independent authentication in the reviewed record.
- Furiati/Escalante contain dependent and disputed witness streams. Their allegations are
  publishable as attributed claims, not as recovered operational orders.
- No reviewed record closes the command, money, travel, communications or logistics edge
  from ZR/RIFLE to Kennedy's murder.
- This was an exhaustive audit of the supplied and developed project record plus targeted
  official recovery, not a claim to have exhausted every page of the JFK Collection or
  onsite archival holdings.
