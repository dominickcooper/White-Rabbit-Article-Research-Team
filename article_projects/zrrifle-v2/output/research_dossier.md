# ZR/RIFLE V2 — research dossier

Research stage only. No article draft. Source Thesis v1 remains locked and aligned.

## Executive finding

ZR/RIFLE was a real, compartmented CIA executive-action capability. It was built around
nonattribution, a principal agent with criminal contacts, authority to use additional
agents and subagents, exceptional accounting, technical support and a capacity to find
people willing to perform acts the Agency could not acknowledge. The record also shows
real burglary/safecracking recruitment, so the capability was dual-use rather than a
simple paper fiction.

The strongest new investigative sequence is documentary and narrow. In April 1963,
William Harvey charged Miami and Perine rooms and calls, another man's lodging and a $75
operational boat at Islamorada to QJWIN/ZR/RIFLE. A Roselli alias card sat in the same
project file. On June 27, Harvey wrote that every ZR/RIFLE aspect had been terminated
except one “precautionary lifeline.” QJWIN's relationship with CIA nevertheless continued
into February 1964.

Those facts establish unexplained 1963 activity and qualified termination. They do not
establish that ZR/RIFLE was redirected against Kennedy. Hunt's late account and Furiati's
network theory name a field of CIA, Mafia and anti-Castro actors that overlaps the proven
capability. The reviewed record has not yet supplied the decisive contemporaneous order,
money, travel, communications or logistics edge to Dallas.

## What the sources say before outside research

The deliberately supplied corpus made the Kennedy connection the controlling inquiry.
The documentary files established QJWIN's high-risk Congo deployment, WIROGUE's execution-
squad offer, ZR/RIFLE's recruitment design, Helms's authority, Harvey's control, Roselli's
alias, Florida expenses and the lifeline. The author-supplied Hunt and Furiati materials
then provided attributed accounts of a later network reaching Harvey, Morales, Sturgis,
Meyer, Phillips, Veciana, Trafficante, Giancana and other anti-Castro figures.

The correct evidentiary move was neither to accept that network whole nor to discard it.
It was to turn each name, place and link into a test against the contemporaneous files.

## The capability

Project notes described extreme compartmentation, nonattribution, a singleton or ruthless
operator, cover/blame-shifting and maximum security. Helms authorized Harvey to handle the
principal agent and other agents/subagents through certification accounting. QJWIN was
paid at a level consistent with a sensitive contract agent and tasked to spot criminals
for burglary/code-theft work. Oversight investigators concluded that kidnapping or murder
sat behind at least part of the project purpose.

Senior testimony did not settle the exact boundary. Bissell said the underworld Castro
contact and ZR/RIFLE folded together and became one. Helms said the lines never crossed.
The files behave like partially overlapping compartments: the Roselli alias appears in
the project file; Harvey used its accounting; the same officer controlled the Castro
phase; yet QJWIN's documented recruiting work continued on its own track.

## Congo: the asset market, not the solved murder

QJWIN—José Marie André Mankel—was recruited in late 1960 for a rapid Leopoldville trip.
The precise mission was withheld; he was warned it carried substantial personal risk.
The project paid him for a one-shot assignment that later records called completed.

In Leopoldville he encountered WIROGUE, a CIA asset prepared for surveillance, package
interception, bridge demolition and other positive action. WIROGUE offered QJWIN $300 a
month plus bonuses to join an intelligence net and execution squad. QJWIN refused.

This is genuine convergence with the contemporaneous Lumumba assassination environment,
but it does not close the target edge. Church investigators could not substantiate that
either man took part in an assassination plan or attempt, and later official testimony
denied involvement in Lumumba's arrest or death. The stronger conclusion is that Congo
exposed a market of deniable action assets and a command system whose true tasking was
deliberately withheld.

## Cuba: where a standby capability met a live theater

By late 1961 Bissell had given Harvey executive-action and Castro responsibilities. Helms
authorized the project; Harvey took over the Roselli phase; poison pills and arms entered
the operation; and Harvey led Task Force W under MONGOOSE. FRUS records show both the
scale of that operational authority and Harvey's insistence that “liquidation of leaders”
be removed from the written record.

That evidence matters in two directions. It makes the capability real and live. It also
means a missing written order is weak negative evidence. But a system designed to avoid
records does not make every later allegation true; the investigation must follow the
receipts that did survive.

## Florida: the receipt that changes the story

The April 13–21, 1963 expense package is the strongest surviving operational lead. It
includes rooms and calls in Miami and Perine, another man's lodging, and an operational
boat at Islamorada. All were charged to QJWIN/ZR/RIFLE even though the file reviewer
thought QJWIN was not involved.

The other man is recorded as John A. Wallston at an implausible `56510 Wilshire
Boulevard` address. A card in the project file identifies John Roselli's alias as John
Ralston. That makes Roselli a plausible identification, especially given his documented
Harvey relationship, but the spelling and address defect keep the edge open.

The ordinary explanation is a Cuba operation using a convenient sensitive account. The
more consequential explanation is that the project funded a broader residual capability.
Neither can be selected until the phone destinations, hotel folios, boat vendor/manifest,
Harvey calendar and alias records are recovered.

## The lifeline

Harvey's June 27 memorandum says the ZR/RIFLE aspects were terminated except for one
precautionary lifeline and minor accountings. Fletcher M. Knight was fully familiar. Later
records say QJWIN's CIA relationship ended only in February 1964.

“Lifeline” could mean a person, communications channel, emergency contact, exfiltration
route, fund or standby operational access. The reviewed files do not say. That ambiguity
is the story's cleanest discriminator. The next records are not hypothetical: Knight's
office files, Lloyd correspondence, certification accounting, QJWIN post-June traffic,
name-trace folders and adjacent ZR crypts.

## The Kennedy claim under test

Hunt's late account places Morales and Sturgis at a 1963 Miami safe house and traces an
alleged chain through Harvey, Cord Meyer, David Atlee Phillips and Antonio Veciana. Saint
John Hunt also relays a family recollection placing his father in Dallas. Furiati assembles
a broader CIA/Mafia/anti-Castro network, including Roselli, Trafficante, Giancana, Maheu,
Hunt, Sturgis and exile figures.

These accounts are admissible and important. They are not independent contemporaneous
orders. Hunt's versions changed; some Furiati chains depend on Cuban intelligence or later
witnesses; Lorenz's account faced official credibility challenges. Rockefeller rejected
the Hunt/Sturgis “derelict” theory and found no concrete CIA connection to Oswald or Ruby
within its limited scope. HSCA later found probable conspiracy but did not identify the
other participant and did not find anti-Castro groups involved as groups, while leaving
individual participation open.

The present evidence therefore supports a serious capability-and-access investigation,
not a solved attribution.

## Published canon and the new branches it opened

White Rabbit canon already establishes Operation 40's Cuban exile network, recurring
Cuba/Watergate personnel, covert handoffs after nominal endings, Gottlieb's front/cutout
methods and the domestic-conspiracy/patsy conclusion. V2 does not re-prove those premises.
It uses them to open five new edges:

1. Operation 40 personnel records against the ZR/RIFLE calendar.
2. Jerry Soul's movement from Cuban exile handling to CIA-backed Cuban forces in Congo.
3. Gottlieb/Technical Services records for ZR/RIFLE and the Castro poison phase.
4. Personnel, money and authorizations after Harvey's formal termination.
5. A direct test of whether the proven ZR/RIFLE architecture supplies the missing
   mechanism in the prior Kennedy canon.

## Adjudication

The extreme thesis—that ZR/RIFLE itself was the team used in Dallas—does not survive as a
fact. Its Congo “proof” fails; the Florida purpose is unknown; the lifeline is unnamed;
and Hunt's command chain lacks contemporaneous authentication.

The strongest supported thesis is still consequential: CIA built and operationalized a
deniable executive-action capability, connected it to criminal recruitment and the Castro
machinery, spent from it in Florida in 1963, and ended it with a residual exception. Those
facts make the Kennedy connection a legitimate unresolved investigation. They do not
permit the article to announce a solved murder.

## Editorial decision

The story is viable and thesis-faithful. Its opening should be the Florida receipt; its
middle should prove the capability and show the Congo constraint; its late reveal should
be the lifeline; and its Kennedy section should test the witnesses against the two
unresolved receipts. The ending should name the records that would decide the question.
