# Research Plan

1. Reconstruct the exact July 1954 attack chronology and target list.
2. Establish Unit 131’s institutional origin, command structure, recruitment and training.
3. Test the false-flag label using official characterization, intended attribution and device design.
4. Place the operation against the Anglo-Egyptian Suez withdrawal negotiations using FRUS and diplomatic history.
5. Separate operational responsibility from the unresolved personal authorization dispute.
6. Reconstruct the arrests, Cairo trial, sentences, executions and prisoner return.
7. Trace the Israeli inquiries, forged evidence, Lavon/Gibli conflict, and Ben-Gurion-era political consequences.
8. Examine Avri Elad allegations without overstating betrayal/double-agent claims.
9. Document later public acknowledgment and honors.
10. Test legitimate contextual connections: early Israeli covert doctrine, Suez, France/nuclear cooperation, and carefully bounded false-flag comparisons.
11. Present the strongest conventional explanation: a reckless defensive effort by a vulnerable young state to preserve a British buffer—not proof of an unlimited conspiracy.
