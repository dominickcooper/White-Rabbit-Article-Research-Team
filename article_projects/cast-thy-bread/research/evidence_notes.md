# EVIDENCE NOTES

## Core chronology

- Feb. 18, 1948: Yadin sends Alexander Keynan to Jaffa to organize HEMED B (reported through interviews/history; later structure documented).
- Apr. 1: Ben-Gurion diary discusses accelerating application of science to warfare.
- Apr. 7–10: Yadin arranges Dayan’s special mission; Apr. 10 cable names Cast Thy Bread.
- Apr. 16–23: material/courier traffic; CTB reported implemented at Biddu and Bayt Surik.
- Late Apr.–May: policy expands from abandoned villages toward invading armies and inhabited sites.
- Early May: Acre epidemic and alleged Kabri aqueduct contamination.
- May 13: Ben-Gurion original diary records Ephraim Katchalsky and $2,000 in biological materials.
- May 14: Ben-Gurion places science units and mistaʿarvim under Ratner.
- May 17 onward: Halutza and later Beersheba/Negev CTB reports.
- May 21–22: Mizrahi and Horin/Afgin cross toward Gaza and are captured.
- May 27: Egyptian allegation reaches UN Security Council; Ben-Gurion diary notes capture report.
- Summer/autumn: operations/attempts in Negev and ʿAylabun.
- Sep. 26: Yadin initiates CTB abroad; Cairo plan stopped.
- Jan. 1949: Lebanon/Syria water-system reconnaissance request.

## Technical notes

- Organisms named: typhoid and dysentery bacteria; contemporaries often confused typhoid and typhus. “Malaria bacteria” in Ben-Gurion diary appears garbled.
- Delivery: liquid cultures in bottles/flasks/canteens; one Guttman recollection describes powder mixed with water.
- Operational problem: viability, dose, dilution, transport, friendly-force exposure.
- Acre medical evidence: loss of chlorination, exposed/damaged aqueduct, B. coli count 180/ml on Apr. 13, no salmonella isolated, likely British infection Apr. 9–13.

## Evidence discipline

- CIA preservation ≠ CIA confirmation.
- Egyptian accusation ≠ independent proof; later Israeli evidence changes assessment.
- Contamination ≠ epidemic causation ≠ material contribution to Acre’s fall.
- HEMED membership ≠ individual operational culpability.
- Nakam personnel overlap ≠ organizational/operational continuity.
- IIBR lineage ≠ continuous offensive BW program.
- Modern destruction/deprivation mechanism ≠ covert pathogen placement.
