# RESEARCH DOSSIER: OPERATION CAST THY BREAD

## Executive assessment

The accessible record establishes that the Haganah and then IDF operated a biological-warfare campaign in 1948 under the code name **Cast Thy Bread (Shallah Lahmekha)**. It focused on contaminating water sources to prevent return to depopulated Palestinian villages, weaken inhabited Arab locations, and impede invading Arab armies. Yigael Yadin exercised direct operational command. David Ben-Gurion most likely knew about and authorized the campaign: that judgment is supported by his diary, private correspondence, command changes, personal scientific apparatus, contact with Yadin, and the program’s scale, although no surviving public order explicitly says “poison wells.”

The program’s operational effect appears limited. Gaza is the best-correlated case across Egyptian allegations, UN proceedings, U.S. Embassy reporting preserved by CIA, and later Israeli testimony. Acre is the most consequential but causally difficult case: deliberate Israeli contamination of the Kabri aqueduct is strongly supported, while contemporary British epidemiology also documents absent chlorination, aqueduct damage, fecal contamination, and a likely infection window that may precede the attack.

## Finding 1: A state-directed biological-warfare program existed

- **Finding:** Cast Thy Bread was a real Haganah/IDF program, not merely an Arab accusation.
- **Evidence:** April 10 Yadin cable; April 16 and 22–23 “Neptune” traffic; Dayan’s report that CTB was implemented at Biddu and Bayt Surik; later General Staff and brigade records.
- **Sources:** Morris and Kedar, Middle Eastern Studies 59:5 (2023), DOI 10.1080/00263206.2022.2122448; IDFA 5440/1949; IDFA 2315/1950.
- **Evidence type:** DOCUMENTED FACT.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Why it matters:** It shifts the core inquiry from existence to scope, responsibility, and effect.
- **Natural question:** Who authorized and supplied it?
- **Implication:** This was coordinated through senior operations channels and a compartmented scientific unit.
- **Contrary explanation:** Some individual episodes could have been local improvisations.
- **Unresolved gap:** HEMED B’s central operational and laboratory files remain closed.

## Finding 2: Command authority reached the top

- **Finding:** Yadin exercised direct operational command; Ben-Gurion most likely knew about and authorized CTB.
- **Evidence:** Yadin’s repeated instructions, reports, transport interventions, and September 26 order concerning activation abroad. Ben-Gurion discussed applying science to warfare on April 1, placed science units and mistaʿarvim under Ratner on May 14, recorded Katchalsky’s biological-material purchase, and met Yadin before the foreign activation.
- **Sources:** IDFA 5440/1949; IDFA 2384/1950-8; Ben-Gurion diary entries of April 1, May 13, May 27, and September 26; private Ben-Gurion letter to Ratner, May 14.
- **Responsibility:** Yadin—DIRECT and COMMAND RESPONSIBILITY. Ben-Gurion—PROBABLE and COMMAND RESPONSIBILITY.
- **Confidence:** High for Yadin; medium-high for Ben-Gurion.
- **Why it matters:** The program cannot be dismissed as misconduct by isolated soldiers.
- **Competing explanation:** Yadin or Ratner ran CTB without Ben-Gurion’s explicit approval.
- **Evidence-weighted judgment:** The unauthorized-program theory is possible but substantially less likely because CTB crossed fronts, used Ben-Gurion’s scientific apparatus, was reorganized through his private correspondence, and expanded abroad after his meeting with Yadin.
- **Unresolved gap:** No publicly available explicit Ben-Gurion authorization naming CTB.

## Finding 3: HEMED B supplied the biological side

- **Finding:** HEMED B/Beit (“Neptune”) was the compartmented biological arm linked to General Staff Operations.
- **Evidence:** July 6 General Staff definition; Alexander Keynan’s assignment; container and courier traffic; Shelomo Gur’s account that the unit bypassed normal HEMED reporting.
- **Sources:** IDFA 2315/1950-21; Avner Cohen (2001); Morris and Kedar (2023); 1993 Hadashot interviews cited by Cohen.
- **Evidence type:** DOCUMENTED FACT for structure and Keynan’s operational awareness; STRONG INFERENCE for Ephraim Katchalsky’s knowledge of offensive application and preparation of CTB batches.
- **Confidence:** High / medium-high.
- **Evidence-weighted judgment:** Keynan almost certainly knew CTB’s purpose. Ephraim Katchalsky most likely knew HEMED’s biological work had offensive application. Evidence is insufficient to assign Aharon Katchalsky a CTB mission or specific batch.
- **Unresolved gap:** No accessible culture logs, recipes, viability tests, or complete personnel roster.

## Finding 4: The Katchalsky/Katzir evidence is strong but bounded

- **Finding:** Ephraim Katchalsky sought biological/poison material abroad, returned with $2,000 in “biological materials,” and became HEMED commander. Aharon and Ephraim earlier supplied poison to Abba Kovner.
- **Evidence:** Ben-Gurion diary original; Ephraim’s interview with Dina Porat; biographies and memoirs.
- **Evidence type:** DOCUMENTED FACT for acquisition, roles, and Nakam contact; PLAUSIBLE CONNECTION for transfer of specific imported material into CTB.
- **Confidence:** High / medium.
- **Why it matters:** It establishes personnel and technical continuity without proving operational continuity from Nakam.
- **Unresolved gap:** Identity and destination of the imported materials.

## Finding 5: Acre was targeted, but causation is mixed

- **Finding:** The Kabri aqueduct was deliberately contaminated by a Carmeli officer with flasks reportedly supplied by Dayan.
- **Evidence:** Yaacov Pundaq’s later testimony via Uri Milstein; CTB’s contemporaneous operation and Acre-related cable context; postwar Israeli discussion.
- **Evidence type:** STRONG INFERENCE, not a fully contemporaneous confession.
- **Confidence:** Medium-high.
- **Effect finding:** A waterborne enteric epidemic existed and contributed to fear. The available evidence cannot show that CTB caused all or most cases.
- **Evidence:** ICRC reporting; British conference notes; Ittihad Hospital report; Shaw and Mackay medical study.
- **Contrary explanation:** Chlorine exhausted March 17; engineer departed March 18; damaged open aqueduct; gross fecal contamination; British illness onset implies likely infection April 9–13; no salmonella isolated from water.
- **What contrary evidence explains:** A natural/infrastructure route for the outbreak and early cases.
- **What it does not explain:** Pundaq testimony, CTB infrastructure, or Israeli intent to contaminate the water.
- **Evidence-weighted judgment:** Mixed causation best fits the record: a naturally generated waterborne epidemic probably began first, and deliberate CTB contamination was added later. It may have aggravated disease, but its clearest supportable effect is amplification of panic.
- **Military-effect assessment:** Disease and rumor were contributing factors, not a demonstrated primary cause of Acre’s fall. Siege, isolation, refugee pressure, shortages, leadership problems, and assault were independently substantial.
- **Unresolved gap:** Exact date, dose, organism viability, location, and case attribution.

## Finding 6: Gaza was a genuine CTB-related mission

- **Finding:** David Mizrahi and Ezra Horin/Afgin were sent toward Gaza on a mission that included contaminating wells used by Egyptian forces.
- **Evidence ladder:**
  1. Egyptian allegation to UN, May 27, 1948.
  2. Egyptian defense communiqué describing recovered bacteria containers.
  3. U.S. Embassy Cairo press review preserved as CIA-RDP83-00415R001100020015-0.
  4. UN Security Council Official Records, 306th meeting.
  5. Ben-Gurion diary records capture report.
  6. Israeli Arab Platoon memoirs and Guttman interview describe the mission and internal objection.
- **Evidence type:** Contemporaneous allegation + contemporaneous diplomatic preservation + later Israeli archival/testimonial confirmation.
- **Confidence:** High for mission intent and capture; medium for successful contamination of one or two wells; low for casualties.
- **Critical wording rule:** The CIA did not “confirm” the mission; its archive preserves U.S. Embassy reporting.
- **Unresolved gap:** Original Egyptian laboratory report, trial record, full confessions, and health outcomes.

## Finding 8: CTB was militarily weak and internally contested

- **Finding:** The campaign triggered opposition from Guttman, Aharoni, Gur, Shiloah, Kotzer, and possibly Weizmann.
- **Evidence:** Interviews, memoirs, destruction of delivered bottles, canceled Cairo plan, brigade rebuke.
- **Evidence type:** DOCUMENTED in later testimony; some motivations inferred.
- **Confidence:** Medium-high.
- **Effect:** Outside Acre, the public record shows no epidemic and little military benefit. Aharoni reported no Egyptian soldiers harmed and several Bedouin and Israeli soldiers sickened.
- **Conventional explanation:** Biological agents in open water were unpredictable, difficult to transport, morally contentious, and inferior to conventional denial/sabotage.
- **Evidence-weighted judgment:** Dissent was operationally meaningful, not rhetorical. It stopped or disrupted at least the Aharoni shipment and Cairo plan. Because insiders recognized moral and friendly-force dangers at the time, later claims that CTB was merely an ordinary wartime expedient are less persuasive.

## Finding 8A: Britain privately credited the Gaza allegation enough to avoid, not dismiss, it

- **Facts:** Egypt complained to London; the Foreign Office maintained E 7162/1/31/48 within FO 371/68509–68510; Morris’s archival account records a decision to “keep out” and an official’s view that the incident was “obnoxious” enough to merit possible private disgust.
- **Competing explanation:** Officials regarded the allegation as propaganda and declined to dignify it.
- **Evidence-weighted judgment:** The moral language fits private credibility and diplomatic avoidance better than outright disbelief. Britain most likely considered the charge plausible and repellent but subordinate to larger post-Mandate and UN strategy.
- **Confidence:** Medium, because the complete underlying file was not digitally available.
- **Responsibility:** British policy responsibility for non-escalation; no evidence of an active cover-up.

## Finding 8B: The United States monitored but did not visibly elevate the issue

- **Facts:** The U.S. Embassy Cairo collected and internally distributed Egyptian press reporting. Public FRUS traffic is extensive but no investigation, protest, or factual U.S. assessment was located.
- **Competing explanation:** A further assessment exists in unpublished or unlocated files.
- **Evidence-weighted judgment:** The public record supports awareness and compartmented monitoring, not acceptance or rejection. Strategic priorities most likely kept the allegation below policy level, but confidence is limited.
- **Confidence:** Medium-low.
- **Unresolved evidence:** Embassy dispatches, State Department working files, or intelligence evaluations beyond the press review.

## Finding 9: Archival secrecy shaped the history

- **Finding:** CTB remained obscure because of classification, purposeful sanitization, euphemism, and dispersed files.
- **Evidence:** HEMED B files unavailable; “biological materials $2,000” deleted from published Ben-Gurion diary; code-name documents passed review; key letter in private hands; wider Akevot evidence of reclassification.
- **Evidence type:** DOCUMENTED FACT.
- **Confidence:** High.
- **Competing explanation:** The diary phrase was removed through ordinary editorial abridgment and other gaps reflect routine archival disorder.
- **Evidence-weighted judgment:** Some dispersal was accidental, but the sensitive nature of the omitted phrase, closure of HEMED B records, and wider reclassification practices make secrecy-driven obscurity substantially more likely than a purely accidental gap.
- **Implication:** The accessible record is incomplete by design and circumstance.
- **Caution:** Wider reclassification practices do not prove the content of any missing CTB file.

## Finding 10: HEMED-to-IIBR lineage is institutional, not proof of continuity

- **Finding:** IIBR’s official history states it was founded in 1952 by IDF Science Corps and academic scientists.
- **Evidence type:** DOCUMENTED FACT.
- **Connection:** HEMED personnel and research capacity continued into a permanent government institution.
- **Limit:** SNIE 30-2-63 found scientific capability but no evidence of an offensive BW R&D program in 1963. FOI-R--1734--SE (2005) found past offensive capability but could not conclude it remained active.
- **Assessment:** Institutional lineage—high confidence. Continuous offensive program—UNVERIFIED.

## Finding 11: Modern “water as a weapon” allegations use a different mechanism

- **Finding:** Contemporary Gaza sources allege destruction/restriction of water, fuel, chlorine, repairs, sanitation, and healthcare, producing disease conditions.
- **Key record:** State of Palestine working paper BWC/MSP/2024/WP.10 calls this an “indirect form of biological warfare.”
- **Evidence type:** DOCUMENTED ALLEGATION regarding legal/political characterization; separate empirical evidence exists for infrastructure damage and disease risk.
- **Critical distinction:** These records do not document present-day covert placement of cultured pathogens into wells.
- **Assessment:** Strong analogy at the level of water vulnerability; weak analogy at the level of mechanism.

## Strongest unexpected connection

The strongest unexpected connection is not a direct Nakam-to-CTB organizational handoff. It is the narrower, documented overlap of the Katchalsky brothers: scientists who supplied poison for a post-Holocaust revenge plan later occupied key positions in the military-scientific environment from which HEMED B emerged. This suggests continuity of personnel, knowledge, and ethical emergency—not proof of continuity of command.

## Strongest counterargument

The Acre outbreak has a complete conventional pathway: interrupted chlorination, damaged and exposed water infrastructure, gross fecal contamination, wartime crowding, and illness timing that may predate deliberate contamination. This makes claims that CTB created the entire epidemic or caused Acre’s fall unsustainable.

## Most important unresolved question

What do the closed HEMED B scientific files show about organisms, viability, batch production, target doses, after-action sampling, and actual casualties?

## Material archival gaps

- HEMED B laboratory and operational files.
- Original Gaza bacteriology results and Egyptian military trial file.
- Full, authenticated confessions of Mizrahi and Horin/Afgin.
- Complete Ben-Gurion/General Staff authorization trail.
- Acre dose, timing, and organism-typing evidence.
- Complete foreign-operation planning file for Cairo and other capitals.
