# Factual and adversarial audit

## Thesis boundary

The draft states as fact only what the congressional, CIA and operational records support:
standby assassination capability, dual-use cover, authorization, Castro fusion and later
withholding. It explicitly rejects the unsupported claim that ZR/RIFLE is proven to have
killed Kennedy.

## Disconfirmation

Harvey's denial of recruiting an assassin or creating a target list is included. QJWIN's
extensive safecracking work is treated as real. Church Book V's statement that it did not
prove a JFK conspiracy is included. Ordinary secrecy/source-protection explanations are
acknowledged but do not explain the full nonattribution plan.

## Source dependence

The Cargill memorandum is a 1975 review of underlying files and includes the staffer's
interpretations. It is not double-counted as independent proof from the same files. The
Church report and CIA IG sometimes depend on overlapping testimony and records. FRUS
provides an independent documentary stream for MONGOOSE scale and Harvey's role.

## Adversarial conclusion

A skeptical reader can argue ZR/RIFLE remained preparatory and that ordinary source
protection explains some secrecy. That does not defeat the Senate's direct finding that it
was an assassination capability or the documented Castro application. A maximalist reader
can argue the same architecture could have been used in Dallas; possibility is retained,
but the missing operational edge prevents publication as fact.
