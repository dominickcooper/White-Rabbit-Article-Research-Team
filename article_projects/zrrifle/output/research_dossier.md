# Research dossier: Project ZR/RIFLE

## Research question

What did ZR/RIFLE actually build, when did it move from preparatory capability into a
live assassination context, and does the surviving record support the claim that it was
redirected against President Kennedy?

## Source base and authority

The project inspected all thirteen author-supplied files: six PDFs with usable text, two
scan-only PDFs reviewed by page render, five OCR text files, one EPUB and one saved HTML
book capture. The strongest sources are CIA operational records and congressional reviews.
The two books are accepted as author-supplied testimony and lead generators with
attribution preserved. Published White Rabbit articles are canon for their prior findings
and are used only for structural callbacks.

External primary research reopened:

- Senate Church Committee interim report, *Alleged Assassination Plots Involving Foreign
  Leaders*, S. Rep. 94-465.
- Church Committee Book V, *The Investigation of the Assassination of President John F.
  Kennedy: Performance of the Intelligence Agencies*, S. Rep. 94-755.
- CIA 1967 Inspector General assassination-plots report as released through NARA.
- NARA JFK Collection, including the 1975 Cargill file-review memorandum.
- State Department *Foreign Relations of the United States*, Cuba volumes documenting
  Operation MONGOOSE and Task Force W.

## Core chronology

- **November 1960:** QJWIN/Mankel recruited for a concealed, high-risk Congo mission and
  tasked to spot criminal talent; true requirements withheld.
- **January–June 1961:** financial records call the Congo task a one-shot sensitive
  operation; QJWIN begins European criminal recruitment; ZR/RIFLE name appears.
- **November 1961:** Bissell tells Harvey to take over the Roselli/Castro contact; Kennedy
  administration launches MONGOOSE.
- **February 19, 1962:** Helms formally authorizes Harvey to retain QJWIN and subagents,
  use certification accounting and handle the activity EYES ONLY.
- **1962:** Harvey directs CIA's Task Force W implementation of MONGOOSE.
- **1963:** authorization extended; Miami-area expenses charged to ZR/RIFLE; Harvey says
  most ZR/RIFLE aspects are terminated except a precautionary “lifeline.”
- **1964:** QJWIN contract terminated.
- **1967:** CIA Inspector General reconstructs the Castro plots and ZR/RIFLE.
- **1975–1976:** Rockefeller and Church investigators recover the dual-purpose program,
  executive-action mandate, Castro fusion and investigative omissions.

## Direct findings

1. ZR/RIFLE included a CIA standby capability for assassination.
2. A genuine burglary/safecracking program shared the cryptonym and supplied cover.
3. The executive-action design emphasized nonattribution, singleton chains, forged
   records, blame-shifting, restricted traffic and low-detail accounting.
4. Helms funded and authorized Harvey's use of QJWIN and additional agents.
5. Harvey took over the organized-crime channel against Castro; Church concluded the
   Castro operation became an aspect of ZR/RIFLE.
6. Harvey simultaneously directed Task Force W under MONGOOSE.
7. CIA senior officers did not tell the Warren Commission or working investigators about
   the Castro assassination plots.

## Strong inferences

The genuine burglary apparatus did more than provide a written cover story: it created
legends, international access and a labor market of deniable criminals. The repeated
secrecy controls were designed to make later attribution difficult, not merely to protect
ordinary operational privacy.

## Unresolved but story-changing

- QJWIN's exact Congo objective and its relation to the Lumumba plot.
- Whether WIROGUE received lethal tasking beyond his action-oriented assessment.
- Purpose of Harvey's April 1963 Miami/Islamorada expenses.
- Meaning and destination of the June 1963 “precautionary lifeline.”
- Complete contents of missing project-notebook pages.

## Rejected or failed claims

- No recovered evidence shows QJWIN or WIROGUE carried out an assassination.
- No ZR/RIFLE target list, authenticated order, payment trail or contemporaneous travel
  chain connects the program to Dallas.
- Hunt and Lorenz testimony does not independently establish a JFK operational chain.
- Similarity to later programs does not prove direct organizational continuity.

## Final thesis

ZR/RIFLE was a real CIA standby assassination capability hidden inside a genuine burglary
and criminal-recruitment program. Harvey fused that capability with the Castro plots and
MONGOOSE. The program is not proven to have killed Kennedy; the record proves that senior
officials withheld the relevant plot architecture from the investigation of his murder.

## Publication boundary

The article may state the capability, dual-use cover, Castro fusion and investigative
withholding as fact. It must label the QJWIN–Lumumba connection as unresolved and the
ZR/RIFLE–JFK deployment theory as unproven.
