# Published White Rabbit canon retrieval

Archive search was completed before external research. The closest canon is structural,
not a prior ZR/RIFLE article.

## Canon premises carried forward

1. **Operation Gladio in America, Part 2** — covert programs can migrate through shared
   personnel, liaison and law-enforcement infrastructure; labels may change while
   operational functions persist. Use only as a precedent for how to ask the next
   question, not as proof ZR/RIFLE was Gladio.
2. **USAID & the CIA, Part 5: Operation TWO-FOLD** — a documented CIA personnel system
   used spotting, assessment, proprietary cover and concealed Agency involvement to put
   people into another institution. This is canon for the mechanics of covert personnel
   placement. The new edge is that ZR/RIFLE used an earlier version of the same personnel
   verbs—spot, assess, recruit—against deniable criminal talent.
3. **USAID & the CIA, Part 4: The Handoff** — a function can survive an institutional
   transition through personnel and statutory exceptions. Use as a comparison for
   functional migration, not direct continuity.
4. **USAID & the CIA, Part 6: The Network** — the archive already treats built-in access,
   liaison and personnel seams as more revealing than a mythical memo saying one agency
   “owned” another. Apply that discipline to ZR/RIFLE's compartmented design.
5. **The MK-Ultra Files, Part 1** and **Operation Gladio: The Hidden History** surfaced as
   relevant internal-link candidates for laboratory capability and deniable networks.
   They are callbacks, not evidentiary substitutes.

## Canon boundary

No retrieved White Rabbit article establishes that ZR/RIFLE killed Kennedy, controlled
all later CIA lethal action, or directly became another named program. Those claims must
stand or fall on this project's evidence.
