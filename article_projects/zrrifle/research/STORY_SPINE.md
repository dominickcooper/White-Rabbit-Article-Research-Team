# Story spine

This—not dossier or claims-ledger order—is the principal structural source for drafting.

## CENTRAL MYSTERY

Why did Richard Helms authorize a principal agent, open-ended subagents, certification
accounting and EYES ONLY handling in a memorandum “in lieu of project”—and why do the
underlying files look, at first, like a safecracking business?

## OPENING RECEIPT

The February 19, 1962 Helms-to-Harvey authorization: QJWIN salary, additional funds,
subagents, no conventional project paper, strict EYES ONLY handling.

## WHAT THE READER THINKS AT FIRST

This may be an exotic but essentially nonlethal CIA burglary project.

## REVEAL LADDER

1. **The money** — Helms gives Harvey unusually flexible authority. Question: why this
   handling? Next: see what QJWIN was hired to do.
2. **The recruiter** — QJWIN scouts safecrackers and criminals under commercial cover.
   Question: why did the true requirement have to be withheld even from him?
3. **The second program** — Church identifies ZR/RIFLE as executive action and says the
   genuine second program provided cover. Question: what did the hidden capability need?
4. **The architecture** — singleton agents, no station discussion, false 201 files,
   political nonattribution, Soviet/Czech blame. Question: was it ever aimed at a target?
5. **Congo** — QJWIN's one-shot high-risk trip and WIROGUE's action profile overlap the
   Lumumba plot, but the last edge is missing. Question: where does proof resume?
6. **Castro** — Harvey takes the Roselli channel and runs MONGOOSE; Church says the Castro
   operation became an aspect of ZR/RIFLE. Capability becomes operational context.
7. **The files fight back** — Harvey says no assassin was recruited and no target list
   existed; the record supports that narrower statement for QJWIN while still proving the
   capability.
8. **Dallas enters** — Hunt's late testimony alleges redirection. Test it. No target/order/
   finance chain appears. The theory fails its decisive edge.
9. **The documented cover-up** — CIA did not tell the Warren Commission or its own working
   investigators about the Castro plots. Book V says that impaired the inquiry.
10. **Payoff** — The scandal is not that every murder theory becomes true. It is that a
    state-built deniable murder architecture existed and then denied a murder inquiry the
    information needed to judge its relevance.

For each of approximately 5–12 reveals record discovery, evidence, why it changes the story, the question it creates, and what the reader learns next.

Use FACT → QUESTION → IMPLICATION → NEXT RECEIPT. Mark which reveals rest on published
canon, accepted testimony, documented record, corroborated inference, or new external
material. Internal labels guide structure; they do not have to appear in article prose.

## HUMAN BRIDGES

Harvey carries the story between executive action and MONGOOSE. QJWIN carries it between
paper authority and a transnational criminal labor market. Roselli connects the state to
organized crime. Hunt carries the reader to the temptation—and evidentiary limit—of
Dallas.

## RABBIT HOLES WE ARE FOLLOWING

QJWIN's Congo task; WIROGUE's candidate profile; Roselli/Castro fusion; the June 1963
“lifeline”; withholding from the Warren Commission.

## RABBIT HOLES WE ARE DEFERRING

Complete Lumumba culpability chain; John Wallston/Miami receipt identity; possible later
successor programs; every Hunt/Lorenz named participant.

## PRIOR-SERIES CALLBACKS

TWO-FOLD for spotting/assessment mechanics; Gladio for deniable-network precedent;
MKULTRA for the technical-services context. Brief only.

Use canon briefly and move to the new edge; do not re-teach or re-prove it.

## “WAIT, WHAT?” MOMENT

The Church Committee footnote: the other ZR/RIFLE area was real, but it was also meant to
provide cover for executive action.

## ORDINARY EXPLANATION

Sensitive burglary and foreign-agent work naturally require secrecy, criminal contacts,
flexible funds and compartmentation. Later investigators may have overread ambiguous
notes through the lens of known Castro plots.

## WHAT THE ORDINARY EXPLANATION ACCOUNTS FOR

Most QJWIN cables, safecracker recruitment, commercial-document legends, and much of the
financial/cover paperwork.

## WHAT REMAINS UNRESOLVED

The forged counterintelligence-file plan, blame-shifting, ruthless-officer criteria,
Helms's broad subagent authority, Congo anomalies, Roselli slip, Miami expenses and
residual lifeline are not explained by ordinary burglary alone.

## BIGGER PATTERN

The most deniable state capability is not a permanent squad. It is a repeatable method for
finding disposable talent, separating every link and making each surviving file look like
something else.

## PAYOFF

Capability, deployment context and concealment are proven. JFK deployment is not. The
reader gets a sharper scandal instead of a louder but unsupported one.

## FINAL QUESTION / NEXT RABBIT HOLE

What exactly was the one mission whose purpose the CIA would not put in QJWIN's briefing,
and what survived inside Harvey's “precautionary lifeline” after the project was said to
be over?

Prefer an evidence-opened door over a sterile section or thesis recap.
