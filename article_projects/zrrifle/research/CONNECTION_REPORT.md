# Connection report

## Most consequential documented connections

- Bissell's executive-action assignment became funded authority when Helms authorized
  Harvey to retain QJWIN and additional agents under EYES ONLY, certification-based
  accounting.
- The safecracking files and assassination mandate are not mutually exclusive. The Church
  Committee said the second ZR/RIFLE program was genuine and served as cover.
- Harvey, the executive-action officer, also took over the Roselli/Castro channel and ran
  CIA's portion of MONGOOSE. The capability and a live target program converged in one
  officer.
- Senior CIA officials later withheld the Castro plots from the Warren Commission and
  from their own investigators. That is a documented concealment of relevant operations,
  not proof of CIA participation in Kennedy's murder.

## Human career bridges

Harvey is the central bridge: counterintelligence operator → FI/D chief → ZR/RIFLE →
Roselli/Castro channel → Task Force W → Rome. Gottlieb connects technical-services work
to lethal-method research. QJWIN connects commercial/criminal cover to recruitment.
Roselli connects state planning to organized crime. Hunt is a late testimonial bridge to
Dallas, but that edge remains unproven.

## Institutional / program chains

FI/D housed a compartment that could move through sealed traffic and certification
accounting. ZR/RIFLE supplied candidate spotting and method research. Task Force W gave
Harvey a large Cuba platform. The Church investigation later reconstructed the overlap
from records the original oversight and JFK inquiry did not receive.

## Geographic, chronological and financial overlaps

- Congo, November 1960–January 1961: QJWIN's one-shot high-risk mission overlaps the
  Lumumba plot but lacks a recovered target instruction.
- Europe, 1961–1963: QJWIN recruits burglars and underworld talent under commercial cover.
- Miami/Florida, April 1963: Harvey expenses charged to QJWIN/ZR/RIFLE include hotels,
  calls and a boat charter; Cargill suspected a Cuba connection, but the record reviewed
  did not resolve it.
- Funding: $7,200 salary plus additional operational funds, with general-category
  certification rather than conventional vouchers.

## Accepted testimony stepping stones

Saint John Hunt attributes a late “benchwarmer” account to E. Howard Hunt and names
Harvey, Sturgis, Morales and others. The account opens records searches for travel,
payments and contemporaneous communications. Because Hunt's versions changed and no
independent ZR/RIFLE-to-Dallas order emerged, the testimony is presented as the theory's
most provocative failed bridge, not as a conclusion.

For each accepted account, record the attributed first-use formulation and the next
person, organization, program, place, event, or document it opens.

## Connections tested and rejected

- The Boris Shub royalty letter and German Gisevius clipping do not connect to the case.
- WIROGUE's Congo file does not establish a role in Lumumba's death.
- Similarity to Gladio, MKULTRA, TWO-FOLD or later lethal programs does not establish a
  successor chain.
- CIA concealment from the Warren Commission does not establish CIA authorship of the
  assassination.

## Strongest unresolved connections

The withheld objective of QJWIN's Congo trip; the exact function of Harvey's 1963 Miami
expenses; the content of missing project-notebook pages; and the destination of the
“precautionary lifeline” after most ZR/RIFLE aspects were terminated.

## Evidence convergence

Primary authorizations prove resources and unusual handling. Operational files prove the
criminal-talent search and genuine burglary work. The CIA IG and Church report identify
the assassination component. FRUS independently places Harvey atop Task Force W. Book V
proves investigators were denied relevant plot information. Together they establish a
designed, usable architecture and its concealment; none alone proves a particular murder.

What do the independent receipts show together that none establishes alone? Identify
derivative citations so they are not double-counted.

For every corroborating fact, also record the new branch it opens. Corroboration should
expand the network rather than serve only as permission to accept an earlier premise.

## Historical precedents and modern signatures

The durable signature is functional: deniable personnel, cutouts, singleton chains,
plausible legends, compartmented money and a genuine program that can mask a more
sensitive one. Later programs may repeat those mechanics without descending from
ZR/RIFLE.

Distinguish analogy, functional migration, personnel or institutional continuity, and
direct continuity. Precedent guides the next search; it does not prove recurrence.
