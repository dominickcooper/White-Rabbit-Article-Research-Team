# Extreme-thesis ledger

## Original maximum thesis

ZR/RIFLE was not merely a study or a safecracking project. It was a CIA-built,
compartmented assassination network whose deniable personnel, criminal intermediaries,
false records and cover channels were operationally fused with the Castro plots and were
then capable of being redirected against President Kennedy while the same
compartmentation crippled the investigation.

## Activation reason and scope boundary

The supplied records concern a covert, deniable and compartmented capability, while the
author-supplied books extend it into a JFK murder theory. The protocol tests that maximum
case. It does not presume that capability proves deployment or that concealment proves
culpability.
