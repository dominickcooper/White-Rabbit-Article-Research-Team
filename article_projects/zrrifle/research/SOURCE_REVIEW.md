# Supplied-source review

All thirteen files in `sources/` were inspected. PDF text was extracted page-by-page;
the two image-only JFK-record PDFs were rendered and read visually. The EPUB and saved
HTML were mechanically extracted into `research/generated/local_text/`. Originals were
not modified.

Records the two-track file: burglary/safecracking cover and a compartmented capability organized around nonattribution, singleton agents and ruthless operators. Notes QJWIN's one-shot Congo trip, FI/D handling, Helms authorization, Miami expenses, Roselli alias slip, and possible assassins in the personality file. | Primary government review; central. ## Source conflicts retained

- Harvey later testified that no agent was recruited for assassination and no target list
  was drawn. The Church Committee nevertheless found that ZR/RIFLE's purpose included a
  standby assassination capability, and that Harvey later ran the Castro operation as an
  aspect of it.
- The records show a real burglary/safecracking program. That does not negate the
  executive-action component; the Church Committee described the second program as both
  genuine and useful as cover.
- The record establishes concealment of Castro plots from the Warren Commission and an
  investigation impaired by compartmentation. It does not establish that ZR/RIFLE was
  turned against Kennedy.
