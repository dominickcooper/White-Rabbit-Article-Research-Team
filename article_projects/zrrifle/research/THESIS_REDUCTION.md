# Thesis reduction

## ORIGINAL MAXIMUM THESIS

ZR/RIFLE was a CIA assassination network that fused with the Castro plots and was then
redirected against President Kennedy, after which its own compartmentation helped bury
the crime.

## DIRECTLY PROVEN CLAUSES

- CIA created ZR/RIFLE as a standby executive-action capability that included
  assassination.
- The same cryptonym also covered a genuine burglary/safecracking program.
- Helms formally authorized Harvey to retain QJWIN and other agents, with unusual
  accounting and EYES ONLY handling.
- Harvey took over the Mafia contact against Castro, and the Castro operation became an
  aspect of ZR/RIFLE.
- Senior CIA officials withheld assassination plots from the Warren Commission and from
  investigators who said the knowledge would have changed their work.

## STRONGLY INFERRED CLAUSES

- The nonlethal program was useful not only in its own right but as cover, legend and a
  recruitment channel for the executive-action capability.
- Compartmentation was a design feature rather than an accidental paperwork failure.

## PLAUSIBLE CLAUSES

- QJWIN's one-shot Congo assignment concerned the Lumumba assassination plot.
- Some candidates catalogued in the files were being assessed for lethal rather than
  merely burglary work.

## FAILED / CONTRADICTED CLAUSES

- No recovered evidence shows that QJWIN carried out an assassination.
- No recovered target list or operational order ties ZR/RIFLE to JFK's murder.
- Hunt's late “benchwarmer” story and Lorenz's allegations cannot independently carry the
  Dallas claim.
- A later, unbroken successor assassination office is not established.

## FINAL SURVIVING THESIS

**ZR/RIFLE was a real CIA standby assassination capability hidden inside a genuine
burglary-and-recruitment program. William Harvey fused that capability with the Castro
plots, using criminal intermediaries, extreme compartmentation and accounting designed
to leave little conventional trace. The record does not prove that this machine killed
President Kennedy. It proves something historically consequential on its own: the CIA
had built the kind of deniable murder architecture investigators later needed to examine,
then withheld key parts of that architecture from them.**

## Why this is the strongest supported version

It states every element the Senate, CIA and operational records establish without asking
late, credibility-impaired testimony to bridge the missing Dallas edge. It also preserves
the cumulative meaning: capability plus operational fusion plus concealment is more than
a theoretical study, even though it is not proof of a particular assassination.

## Evidence that would change the final thesis

A contemporaneous target list, operational order, financial trail, travel record or
independent participant account connecting ZR/RIFLE personnel to Dallas would strengthen
the JFK clause. Complete original notebooks showing no lethal planning, or reliable proof
that the Senate conflated distinct record systems, would weaken the executive-action
interpretation.
