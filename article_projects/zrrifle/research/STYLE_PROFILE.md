# Article style profile

Build this from approximately 2–4 relevant approved Gold or representative published
White Rabbit articles. Published work is voice canon. Do not copy sentences or turn
recurring phrases into a formula.

## Gold articles consulted and why

- **USAID & The CIA, Part 5: Operation TWO-FOLD** — closest document-led network
  investigation; model for opening on a bureaucratic receipt, stacking records, stating a
  cumulative inference once and keeping uncertainty bounded.
- **Pearl Harbor Cover-Up** — model for confronting an extreme historical thesis while
  retaining open questions and a visible narrator.
- **Villain by Design** — representative long-form voice, blunt transitions, rhetorical
  questions and a system-level ending.

## Narrator presence

First person appears at moments of discovery or adjudication (“That is where the file
changes”), not as a constant diary. The narrator is skeptical of both official euphemism
and sensational overreach.

## Paragraph rhythm and short-paragraph use

Alternate compact document explanation with one-line impact paragraphs. Use occasional
sentence fragments for a genuine reveal. Avoid turning every sentence into a separate
paragraph or every reveal into bold type.

## Reveal pacing and transitions

Begin with Helms's authorization and the phrase “in lieu of project.” Let the reader first
see a strange safecracking operation. Then reveal the second mandate, the personnel
market, the Castro fusion and finally the investigative concealment. Each section should
open the next receipt rather than summarize the last one.

## Rhetorical questions

Use sparingly at true discontinuities: Why would a burglary project require forged
counterintelligence files? What was QJWIN doing in the Congo? What happens when the same
officer receives a live target program? Answer what the record can answer.

## Blunt transitions, reactions, humor and sarcasm

Favor blunt reactions (“That is not a bookkeeping footnote.” “And here the safecracker
story stops being enough.”). Humor should be dry and rare because the subject is state
violence.

## Personal theory and “wait a minute” moments

The theory grows from anomalies inside documents, especially cover language, money and
distribution. The “wait” moment is the Church footnote saying the second program was both
real and cover.

## Quotes and uncertainty

Quote short phrases from primary records. Attribute later testimony immediately. Give
one meaningful boundary after evidence accumulation; do not append “this does not prove”
to every paragraph.

## Callbacks and section openings

Internal callbacks should be one or two sentences that connect mechanics—spotting,
assessment, concealed personnel, functional migration—then return to the new evidence.

## Conclusion mechanics

State the surviving thesis plainly, identify the documented cover-up precisely, then
leave the reader at the unopened door: QJWIN's Congo assignment and the residual
“lifeline.” Do not end in a generic plea to question everything.

## How sections open the next rabbit hole

Money opens recruitment; recruitment opens Congo; Congo opens executive action; executive
action opens Castro; Castro opens the Warren Commission omission; the omission opens the
distinction between a proven hidden machine and an unproven Dallas deployment.
