## A. Published canon

The archive search returned full article bodies and passages, not title shells. The most
useful retrieved articles were *Operation TWO-FOLD*, *USAID & the CIA: The Handoff*,
*USAID & the CIA: The Network*, *Operation Gladio: The Hidden History* and *The MK-Ultra
Files, Part 1*. TWO-FOLD supplied the established personnel-method premise: spotting,
assessment and recruitment can sit behind a real visible function. Handoff and Network
opened the question of whether operational functions can outlive the office or label that
first housed them. Gladio and MKULTRA supplied bounded callbacks for deniable outsiders
and Gottlieb's technical role. None was used as proof of ZR/RIFLE continuity, and the
article did not rerun those earlier investigations.

## B. Accepted testimony

Two author-supplied testimonial/interpretive streams were reviewed: Saint John Hunt's
presentation of E. Howard Hunt's late account and Claudia Furiati's book, including its
interview-derived allegations. Hunt's named-network claim was accepted as a lead and
carried forward into searches for Harvey, Roselli, anti-Castro personnel and documentary
tasking. Furiati's chronology likewise generated searches in the Church, HSCA, CIA and
JFK records. One stream—the Hunt account—appears in the final article because it is
necessary to test the Dallas thesis; neither stream is promoted into an authenticated
tasking record. The workflow did not repeatedly demand that these witnesses re-prove
their testimony before opening the next research hop.

## C. Rabbit-hole depth

The five strongest chains were:

1. **Bissell → Harvey → Helms → QJWIN → criminal talent.** Church findings establish the
   assignment; the Helms memoranda establish authority, money and secrecy; the QJWIN
   dispatch and Cargill review establish concealed spotting and assessment.
2. **Real burglary program → shared ZR/RIFLE label → Executive Action cover.** The Church
   report's footnote resolves the false choice between safecracking and assassination:
   the nonlethal program was genuine and also useful as cover.
3. **ZR/RIFLE → Roselli → Castro operation → Task Force W/MONGOOSE.** Church records place
   the underworld channel under Harvey as a ZR/RIFLE aspect; FRUS independently places
   Harvey atop the CIA's much larger MONGOOSE machinery.
4. **Congo plot → QJWIN one-shot mission → WIROGUE action profile → Lumumba question.**
   The Cargill and IG records establish QJWIN's concealed high-risk assignment, and the
   WIROGUE file establishes a parallel extra-legal personnel assessment. The final edge
   to Lumumba remains broken for want of direct tasking or an after-action record.
5. **Castro plots → senior CIA secrecy → Warren Commission impairment.** Church Book V
   establishes nondisclosure and investigative damage. The further edge from withholding
   to CIA responsibility for Kennedy's murder remains broken.

Three consequential connections changed the story. First, the burglary record was not a
failed cover story but active infrastructure, making the cover more durable. Second,
Harvey simultaneously held the protected capability and a live anti-Castro program, so
the machinery moved beyond an abstract contingency. Third, the provable cover-up is the
withholding of materially relevant assassination plotting from the Warren inquiry—not a
proved cover-up of who killed Kennedy. The unsuccessful Dallas and Lumumba edges are
reported as failures rather than filled with speculation.

## D. Narrative quality

The article opens on the Helms authorization and the phrase “in lieu of project,” then
lets a strange safecracking file become the doorway into Executive Action. Each section
adds a new operational layer: authority, recruitment, dual-purpose cover,
nonattribution, Congo, Castro/MONGOOSE, Dallas disconfirmation and Warren Commission
withholding. Revelations are held until their documentary support appears. The ending
delivers a narrower but stronger conclusion than the opening mystery: a reusable,
deniable capability and a structurally incomplete assassination inquiry are documented,
while Dallas deployment is not. It closes on the unresolved “lifeline” and Congo task,
which are source-generated next records rather than generic mystery bait.

## E. Author voice

The finished draft was compared against three full-length Gold articles:

- *Operation TWO-FOLD* supplied the receipt-led rhythm: the opening authorization and
  dollar figure do narrative work before the system-level inference arrives.
- *Pearl Harbor Cover-Up* supplied the model for testing an extreme historical thesis in
  public: the article follows the theory far enough to find its missing bridge, then
  states the failure without flattening the documented scandal around it.
- *Villain by Design* supplied blunt transitions, natural questions and a payoff about a
  system rather than a catalogue of names.

The result uses full explanatory paragraphs around short reveal lines, dry judgments
such as “An unexplained boat is not a solved plot,” and questions only when they open the
next record. The narrator remains skeptical of both official euphemism and maximalist
certainty. Internal callbacks are brief and mechanical rather than decorative. No Gold
sentence was copied or turned into a phrase template.

## G. Source integrity

- **Supplied documents examined:** 13 of 13, including two scan-only PDFs inspected from
  rendered pages.
- **Consequential accepted testimonial streams:** 2 reviewed; 1 materially used in the
  publication text as a thesis-generating and thesis-testing account.
- **New authoritative external destinations recovered and used:** 5 unique Senate, NARA
  and State Department records in `sources.csv`, including the two Church reports, the
  Cargill file review, CIA Inspector General material and FRUS MONGOOSE records.
- **Substantive contradictions retained:** Harvey's denial versus the official standby-
  capability finding; genuine burglary work versus its simultaneous cover function; and
  proven investigative withholding versus the absence of proof of a Dallas deployment.
- **Major uncertainties:** the exact purpose of QJWIN's Congo mission; the meaning and
  disposition of Harvey's residual “lifeline”; the precise White House origin of the
  capability request; and the absent target/order/payment bridge to Dallas.
- **Limitations affecting the thesis:** overlapping CIA-file/testimony dependencies in
  later reviews, missing or compartmented operational traffic, and late testimony that
  cannot substitute for contemporaneous tasking. These limitations reduced the maximum
  thesis but did not erase the documented capability, Castro application or withholding.

## Extreme-thesis survival result

The direct assassination-capability, dual-use-cover, Castro-fusion and investigative-
withholding clauses survive. The QJWIN–Lumumba clause remains plausible but unproved. The
ZR/RIFLE–JFK deployment clause fails.

## Remaining research risk

The program's incomplete notebooks and heavily compartmented traffic mean absence of a
recovered order is not proof that no order existed. Publication language handles that
epistemic limit without converting it into affirmative evidence.

## Stress-test verdict

PASS for research, thesis reduction, story logic, publication mechanics, final validation
and page-by-page export/render QA.
