## What the original brief thought this article was about

A CIA assassination network, possibly the apparatus used to kill both Castro and Kennedy.

## What the evidence actually revealed

The strongest story is not a solved JFK assassination. It is the documented creation of
a standby murder capability inside a genuine criminal-recruitment program, its fusion
with the Castro plots under William Harvey, and the later withholding of those plots from
the JFK investigation.

Covert functions move through personnel, liaison and administrative seams. TWO-FOLD is a
documented later example of spotting and concealing personnel. Gladio and MKULTRA supply
relevant reader context for deniable networks and technical capacity, not proof here.
