## Mandatory personnel pass

For every significant person, check at least two career/network hops when sources permit: prior and later roles, employers, associates, programs, geography, prior investigations, and intersections with intelligence, narcotics, military, organized crime, finance, covert aviation, policing, technology or surveillance.

The mandatory personnel pass was applied to Harvey, Bissell, Helms, QJWIN, WIROGUE,
Roselli, Gottlieb, O'Donnell, Hunt, Lumumba, Castro and Kennedy. The article centers only
the bridges with direct documentary payoff; speculative Dallas associations remain in the
research record rather than being promoted into narrative fact.
