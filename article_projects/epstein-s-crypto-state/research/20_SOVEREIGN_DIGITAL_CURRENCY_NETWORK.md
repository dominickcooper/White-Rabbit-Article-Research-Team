# Sovereign digital-currency network

## Bottom line

Epstein repeatedly reused a recognizable proposal: copy selected Bitcoin/blockchain mechanics, reject Bitcoin as a full currency because it lacked sovereign backing, add state legitimacy and local political/religious branding, and route the concept through an elite intermediary. The repeated language is **VERIFIED**; treating it as a coherent operational “strategy” is **STRONG_INFERENCE** because implementation teams, central-bank mandates, and completed projects are absent.

## Common elements

1. **Sovereign guarantee:** the repeated dividing line between a crypto asset and a “real currency.”
2. **Borrowed architecture:** Bitcoin/blockchain/Zcash are references or components, not the currency to adopt.
3. **State/elite sponsor:** Putin, a president-elect, a crown prince, rulers, or a government-owned firm.
4. **Local wrapper:** Russian version, Sharia compliance, “Genghis Coin,” Dubai/DP World.
5. **Political attention:** global prestige and first-mover framing recur.
6. **Weak execution:** almost no named engineering team, central bank, budget, charter, pilot, or deliverable.

## Differences

- **Russia:** shortest, most rhetorical pitch.
- **Qatar:** most detailed technical/legal/Sharia questioning.
- **Mongolia:** clearest articulation of replacing Bitcoin while copying blockchain architecture.
- **Dubai:** clearest affirmative response and proposed government-owned vehicle.
- **Saudi Arabia:** strongest religious/monetary branding and apparent high-level intended recipient, but delivery remains uncertain.
- **Abu Dhabi:** strongest report of internal delegation, but identities and project boundaries rely on context.

## ADGM test

EFTA00994138 contains two numbered items: a digital-currency update (“MBZ handed to Khaldoon”) and a separate question about Leon Black meeting Ahmed Ali Al Sayegh, then ADGM chairman. ADGM issued ICO/virtual-currency guidance on October 9, four days later, treating virtual currencies as commodities ([official announcement](https://www.adgm.com/media/announcements/abu-dhabi-global-market-sets-out-guidance-on-initial-coin-offerings-and-virtual-currencies)).

No document located connects Epstein's proposal to the guidance, says ADGM discussed it, or merges the two numbered items into one project. Regulatory guidance of that scope would have been developed before October 5. Treat the timing as **coincidental unless a bridge document appears**.

## Documented outcomes

- Discussion and questions in Qatar and Mongolia.
- Express enthusiasm and a proposed DP World vehicle in Dubai.
- Reported delegation from MBZ to “Khaldoon” in Abu Dhabi.
- Repeated routing attempts toward Putin and an apparent Saudi crown-prince letter.

## What did not happen in the located record

- No central-bank mandate or government resolution.
- No technical adviser/team demonstrably recruited.
- No completed white paper, legal charter, pilot, coin launch, or adoption.
- No proof that later national digital-currency developments resulted from Epstein.
- No basis to interpret Battulga's thanks for “support” as election financing or crypto support.

## Boring explanation tested

The threads can be read as speculative networking by a financier repackaging a fashionable technology for high-status contacts. Their brevity and lack of execution artifacts support that explanation. The Qatar and Dubai exchanges show more than idle name-dropping, but still fall well short of state projects.

## Conclusion

This looks like a repeated Epstein pitch at the level of language, routing, and sales logic. It does not look like a demonstrated government program. For the article, characterize it as a **repeatable sovereign-digital-currency proposal** or **campaign of pitches**, not a successfully executed sovereign-coin strategy.
