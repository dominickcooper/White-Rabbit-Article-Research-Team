## Limitations

1. The released record is incomplete and redacted.
2. DOJ direct PDF endpoints sometimes returned an age-gate HTML page; mirror copies were used for local review while canonical DOJ URLs were retained.
3. OCR is uneven; image-only attachments and misspellings can evade search.
4. Search indexes may omit attachments, calendars, or duplicate productions.
5. “No result” is repository- and query-bounded, not proof of absence.
6. The October 30 recipient remains redacted; thread reconstruction is therefore incomplete.
7. A full Coinbase closing packet and a source-by-source MIT gift expenditure trace remain future work.
