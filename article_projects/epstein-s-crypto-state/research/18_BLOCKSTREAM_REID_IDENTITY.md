# Blockstream “Reid” identity

## Classification

**STRONG_INFERENCE:** “Reid” was Reid Hoffman. The exact July 15 allocation email uses only the first name, so the identity is not labeled VERIFIED.

## Evidence for Hoffman

1. In [EFTA01917472](../sources/EFTA01917472.pdf), Austin Hill tells Joi Ito and Epstein that “Reid” had instructed the last-minute increase of “your allocation” from $50,000 to $500,000.
2. In [EFTA01748990](../sources/EFTA01748990.pdf), a July 31/August 2 Blockstream–Stellar conflict thread explicitly copies **Reid Hoffman**, Ito, and Epstein. Hill asks that the investors reduce or lose their allocation because they were backing Stellar/Ripple; Ito answers, “Reid/Jeffrey, I think we're synch'ed.”
3. In [EFTA00631402](../sources/EFTA00631402.pdf), Epstein later asks Ito whether they are out of “thee reid bitcoin positon”; Ito answers that they are not out yet and ties the timing to Reid returning from China.

Together, the same first name, same Blockstream allocation, and explicit full-name investor thread make an alternative Reid unlikely.

## Evidence against absolute certainty

- Hill did not write the surname in the July 15 message.
- No cap table, subscription agreement, or allocation spreadsheet naming Hoffman was located in this sprint.
- The pronoun “your” can refer jointly to Ito/Epstein's allocation; it does not prove Hoffman controlled their vehicle.

## Role and implications

The record supports that Hoffman was an influential participant in the allocation discussions and later conflict resolution. It does **not** support that he knowingly “funneled Epstein money” into Blockstream.

### What Hoffman may have known

- Hoffman was copied with Epstein on the later Stellar/Blockstream allocation dispute.
- This establishes awareness of Epstein as a fellow participant/contact in the allocation discussion by late July 2014.

### What is not established

- No located Hoffman-authored message says he knew Southern Financial was Epstein-funded.
- No located document shows he reviewed Kyara III's beneficial ownership or the $500,001 wire.
- No direct Hoffman↔Epstein Blockstream negotiation was located.
- The investment paperwork and funding route were mediated through Joi Ito/Kyara III.

## Conclusion

Use: “Context strongly indicates ‘Reid’ was Reid Hoffman.” Do not use: “Hoffman knowingly increased or routed Epstein's investment.” The first is **STRONG_INFERENCE**; the second remains **OPEN/unsupported**.
