## Anti-confirmation-bias conclusion

The strongest conventional reading is that Epstein wrote crisis-era systems speculation in 2008, encountered Pierce’s virtual-currency world by late 2010, noticed Bitcoin’s growth in 2011, tried to meet its visible representatives, and later invested around people and institutions in the ecosystem. The evidence becomes denser as Bitcoin becomes more successful—opposite the pattern expected if he had participated in its hidden creation.
