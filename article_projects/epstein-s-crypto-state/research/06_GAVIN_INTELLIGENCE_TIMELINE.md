## Organizer, sponsor, and venue

- **Inviter/organizer evidenced:** In-Q-Tel.
- **Event:** In-Q-Tel’s annual emerging-technology conference; 2011 theme “Mobility of Money.”
- **Audience:** Gavin described it as for members of US intelligence agencies.
- **Venue:** CIA headquarters, according to Gavin’s contemporaneous and follow-up posts.
- **CIA role:** host venue and likely audience presence are supported. A direct CIA invitation, sponsorship distinct from venue, or private CIA operational meeting is not established by the located materials.
- **Slides:** no authenticated slide deck was located in this sprint.
- **Attendee list:** no full official list located. Gavin named categories/organizations of fellow speakers, not every attendee.

## Sequencing

- Satoshi email → Epstein “bit coin guys” request: **39 days**.
- Epstein request → Brockman outreach: **7 days**.
- Brockman outreach → presentation: **3 days**.
- Presentation → Gavin’s refusal: **1 day**.
- Epstein request → presentation: **10 days**.

These intervals justify a timeline graphic. They do not establish a causal link.

## Additional Epstein/Gavin search through 2015

Name, phrase, email-thread, Brockman, Calacanis, Bitcoin, and investment queries found no later completed contact between Gavin and Epstein through 2015. The strongest record remains the declined June 2011 approach.

## What this timeline does not prove

It does not prove that Gavin caused Satoshi to disappear; that CIA or In-Q-Tel created or controlled Bitcoin; that Epstein knew about the event before contacting Calacanis; that Brockman coordinated with intelligence personnel; that Gavin met Epstein; or that the timing reflects anything more than simultaneous attention to a fast-growing technology.
