# Adam Back, Blockstream, and Epstein

```text
Satoshi --documented Aug 2008 correspondence--> Adam Back
Adam Back --documented 2014 travel/event overlap--> Epstein
Epstein --documented 2014 investment vehicle--> Blockstream
```

These are separate edges formed six years apart. The record does not make them one continuous causal chain.

## 2008: Satoshi and Back

On 2008-08-20 Satoshi sent Back a draft/reference question concerning Hashcash. Back replied on 08-21, directed Satoshi to Wei Dai’s b-money, and thereby contributed a documented intellectual reference path. Satoshi then contacted Dai on 08-22. This is the strongest primary evidence that Back saw Bitcoin material before the public announcement.

No 2008 Epstein/Back, Epstein/Hashcash, or Epstein/b-money record was found.

## Earliest Epstein/Back overlap located

- **2014-04-14/15 — EFTA00987576:** Austin Hill arranges St. Thomas travel, discusses Adam arriving from Heathrow, and Epstein writes that “you and andy” can come to the island Friday/Saturday.
- **2014-04-16 — EFTA00987642:** reservations cover 04-17 through 04-20 and name Adam Back in the party; St. Thomas/island logistics continue.
- **2014-04-19 — EFTA00988395:** Epstein tells Amir Taaki, “i have Andy Back on my island this weekend.”
- **2014-05-01 — EFTA01926082:** Hill follows up with Epstein, copying Back, and sends a paper about Bitcoin as a commodity.

The combined record is strong evidence that Back traveled with the group and was at least in St. Thomas. Epstein’s own statement is the clearest evidence of physical presence on the island. No independent photograph, visitor log, or Back-authored confirmation was located, so the island-presence claim is best labeled **VERIFIED_WITH_ATTRIBUTION**.

Named/visible group members in the planning correspondence include Adam Back and Austin Hill, along with additional travelers recorded in EFTA00987642. The subject of any meeting is not stated. Investment and Bitcoin became explicit in subsequent correspondence, but that does not prove the island conversation’s content.

## Blockstream formation and allocation

In May–July 2014 Epstein and Joi Ito discussed an investment through an Ito vehicle. On 2014-07-15 Austin Hill wrote Ito and Epstein, copying Back, that the round was oversubscribed and the allocation associated with “Reid” had been increased from $50,000 to $500,000. The email proves Back was copied on an allocation communication; it does not prove he knew Epstein was the beneficial source of all funds or negotiated with Epstein personally.

## Kyara III / Southern Financial structure

EFTA00584696 is an operating agreement for **Kyara Investments III LLC**:

- purpose: solely to hold Blockstream equity;
- manager: Joichi Ito;
- Southern Financial LLC: 500 of 1,000 membership interests, listed contribution $500,001;
- Joichi Ito: 500 of 1,000 membership interests, listed contribution $2,000;
- Southern Financial signature: Jeffrey Epstein, as manager.

EFTA00027019 and EFTA00080250 record a 2014-09-09 transfer of **$500,001** from Southern Financial to Joichi Ito with details identifying a subscription to Kyara Investments III to fund the Blockstream investment.

This means Southern Financial held 50% of **Kyara’s membership interests**, not 50% of Blockstream. The contribution amounts and membership percentages differ, so neither percentage should be used as a shorthand for economic ownership of Blockstream without the cap table and full side agreements.

## Later interaction and unanswered questions

The located post-trip and investment emails show continued Hill/Epstein/Ito contact with Back sometimes copied. They do not show Epstein directing protocol work, selecting developers, controlling Blockstream, or receiving Satoshi-related information from Back.

Highest-value missing records:

1. a contemporaneous visitor manifest or photograph for Little St. James on 2014-04-18/19;
2. meeting notes or emails stating the trip’s purpose;
3. Blockstream’s full seed cap table and Kyara subscription documents;
4. Back-authored messages identifying what he knew about the funding source;
5. any pre-April-2014 contact record.

## How close did Epstein actually get to someone who received Satoshi’s Bitcoin paper before publication?

**Very close socially by 2014, but not close in time to Bitcoin’s creation.** The record strongly places Back in Epstein’s St. Thomas/island orbit and then in copied investment correspondence. That is the strongest documented Satoshi-adjacent connection found. It occurs roughly five and a half years after publication and supplies no evidence that pre-publication information moved to Epstein.
