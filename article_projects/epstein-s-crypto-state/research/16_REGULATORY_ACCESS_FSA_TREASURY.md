# Regulatory access: FSA and Treasury

## Finding

The record supports a recurring interest in placing digital-money entrepreneurs and ideas near regulators. The pattern is **STRONG_INFERENCE**, not a fully verified coordinated campaign: the 2011 FSA contact survives only as Donald Norman's report, while the 2014 U.S. Treasury contact is documented directly.

## Donald Norman / UK FSA

### Identity

The Donald in [EFTA00656005](../sources/EFTA00656005.pdf) is **Donald Norman of Bitcoin Consultancy/Intersango**, not design scholar Don Norman. A federal court record identifies Norman, Amir Taaki, and Patrick Strateman as Intersango's creators and describes Norman as the intended CEO/funder of the exchange. See [U.S. court order](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-cand-4_16-cv-03587/pdf/USCOURTS-cand-4_16-cv-03587-4.pdf).

### Sequence

| Date | Event | Classification |

| 2011-06-12 | Epstein calls Bitcoin “brilliant” but says it has serious downsides. | VERIFIED |
| 2011-07-07–09 | Taaki offers a member of his group in New York; Norman says it was good to meet Epstein. | VERIFIED |

No FSA official, meeting date, written response, attachment, or reviewed proposal is identified. Therefore:

- **A — regulatory access related to virtual currency:** supported with attribution.
- **B — generic financial policy:** contradicted by the explicit Bitcoin, digital-gold, KYC, and virtual-currency-tax context.
- **C — an introduction that went nowhere:** outcome remains open; no further FSA step was located.

## Boring explanation tested

A prospective investor reasonably asks whether an exchange is legal; an MIT donor/investor reasonably joins a policy call. Neither thread proves covert influence or regulatory capture.

> **Was regulatory access a recurring feature of Epstein's crypto involvement?**

**STRONG_INFERENCE.** There are at least two separated, crypto-specific regulatory touchpoints, and the Treasury evidence is strong. The FSA evidence is too thin to elevate the pattern to “VERIFIED” as an organized strategy.
